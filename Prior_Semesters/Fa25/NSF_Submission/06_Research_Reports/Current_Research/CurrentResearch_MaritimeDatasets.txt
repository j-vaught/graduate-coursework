Overview of Maritime Datasets for Vision-Based Navigation and
Detection
The table below summarizes a comprehensive list of maritime-related datasets used in scientific research
for tasks such as object detection, autonomous navigation, and maritime surveillance. Datasets span
various sensor modalities (camera, infrared, LiDAR, radar, AIS, satellite imagery, etc.) and cover diverse
maritime environments (open sea, coastal, port, rivers). Each dataset is accompanied by a reference to a
scientific paper or article introducing or using it, along with links to the dataset and publication for further
details.

An infrared maritime dataset for

images of marine scenes with
diverse conditions (night/day, fog,

maritime scenes (critical for visionbased navigation) 56 55 .

Each of these datasets is publicly available (fully or upon request) and backed by a publication, providing a
foundation and benchmarks for developing and evaluating maritime computer vision and autonomous
navigation algorithms. The variety of sensors and scenarios covered allow researchers to address tasks
from ship detection in port surveillance to obstacle avoidance for autonomous surface vehicles, across both
electro-optical and radar/IR modalities. All sources and links are provided for access to the data and the
respective papers.
