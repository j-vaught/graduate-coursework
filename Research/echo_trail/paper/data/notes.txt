Future Work / Ideas
====================

1. Color Similarity Threshold Experiment
   - Test how close the history (trail) and current echo colors can get
     before detection performance degrades.
   - e.g., sweep the hue separation between trail and current-frame colors
     from fully distinct (red vs green) toward identical (both green / mono).
   - Would reveal the minimum color contrast the detector needs to
     distinguish current target position from historical trail pixels.
