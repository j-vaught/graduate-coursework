## Eligibility Requirements

**All criteria must be met:**

1. **U.S. Citizenship** - Required (no exceptions)
2. **Enrollment** - Must be at SC Space Grant consortium member institution or educational partner
3. **Classification** - Rising sophomore, junior, or senior
4. **Enrollment Status** - Full-time at 4-year institutions
5. **Faculty Sponsor** - Must have faculty advisor sponsorship
6. **Research Interest** - Demonstrated interest in aerospace/space-related studies

**Eligible Research Areas:**
- Basic sciences
- Science education
- Astronomy
- Planetary science
- Environmental studies
- Engineering
- Aerospace/space-related studies

**Faculty Mentor Limit:** Each faculty member may mentor maximum 3 students across ALL SC Space Grant programs.

## Writing Your Research Proposal

### Required Sections (All Must Be Addressed)

Your 2-page proposal must include:

#### 1. General Project Outline
- What is the problem or question?
- Why does it matter?
- What is your approach?

#### 2. Research Methodology
- Specific methods and techniques
- Equipment or resources needed
- Data collection and analysis approach

#### 3. Goals and Expected Results
- Use SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- Concrete metrics for success
- Expected deliverables

#### 4. Project Timeline
- Realistic schedule for 350+ hours
- Key milestones
- Account for presentation and outreach requirements

#### 5. Relevance to NASA (Critical Section)
- Explicit connection to NASA mission directorates
- Reference specific NASA programs or documents
- See detailed guidance below

### Proposal Writing Best Practices

**DO:**
- Write clearly and directly - reviewers read many proposals
- Be specific about methods and outcomes
- Show you understand the research landscape
- Demonstrate feasibility within the timeframe
- Have multiple people review before submission

**DON'T:**
- Use vague language ("I will study...")
- Promise more than you can deliver in 350 hours
- Assume reviewers know your field's jargon
- Wait until the last minute to submit
- Ignore the formatting requirements

## NASA Relevance Statement Guide

This is often the most challenging and important section. Here's how to excel:

### NASA Mission Directorates

Identify which directorate(s) your research aligns with:

#### 1. Aeronautics Research Mission Directorate (ARMD)
- Sustainable aviation technologies
- Flight safety and efficiency
- Environmental protection (emissions, noise)
- Advanced aircraft systems

#### 2. Science Mission Directorate (SMD)
- **Earth Science** - Climate, environmental monitoring
- **Planetary Science** - Solar system exploration
- **Biological/Physical Sciences** - Life in space, materials
- **Heliophysics** - Sun, space weather
- **Astrophysics** - Universe origins, galaxies

#### 3. Exploration Systems Development (ESDMD)
- Artemis program (Moon missions)
- Moon-to-Mars architecture
- Human landing systems
- Life support systems
- Spacesuits and EVA

#### 4. Space Operations (SOMD)
- ISS operations and research
- Commercial space transportation
- Launch services

#### 5. Space Technology (STMD)
- Advanced propulsion
- Materials and structures
- In-space manufacturing
- Communication systems

### Writing Framework

**Weak Example:**
> "My research is relevant to NASA because it involves space."

**Strong Example:**
> "This project directly supports NASA's Science Mission Directorate priority to understand Earth's climate system. As outlined in NASA's 2025-2026 Science Plan, the Earth System Observatory initiative requires improved understanding of [specific topic]. This research addresses that need by [specific contribution], enabling more accurate [specific application]."

### Keywords NASA Prioritizes

- Climate & Earth systems
- Sustainable technologies
- Human exploration (Artemis, Moon-to-Mars)
- Advanced materials
- Autonomous systems
- Workforce development
- Interdisciplinary research

## Selection Criteria

Applications are evaluated by the SC Space Grant Consortium Director and Campus Directors based on:

| Criterion | What They're Looking For |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Academic Qualifications** | GPA, relevant coursework, academic performance |
| **Recommendation Letter** | Substantive, specific endorsement from mentor |
| **Personal Essay** | Clear career direction, genuine interest in space/aerospace |
| **Faculty Sponsorship** | Mentor's credibility and demonstrated support |
| **Proposal Quality** | Clear writing, feasible scope, sound methodology |
| **NASA Relevance** | Explicit, well-researched connection to NASA mission |

### What Makes Applications Stand Out

1. **Clear, uncomplicated writing** - The single most important factor
2. **Explicit NASA connection** - Don't make reviewers hunt for relevance
3. **Realistic scope** - Matches 350+ hour commitment
4. **Strong faculty support** - Evident in both letter and proposal
5. **Specific outcomes** - Measurable, concrete deliverables

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Application Errors

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| Missing deadline | Automatic disqualification |
| Wrong formatting | May be disqualified |
| Exceeding page limits | May be disqualified |
| Locked/unsearchable PDF | Cannot be processed |
| Missing components | Incomplete application |

### Proposal Weaknesses

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Vague objectives | Use SMART goals with measurable outcomes |
| No NASA connection | Dedicate a clear section with specific references |
| Overly ambitious scope | Match scope to 350 hours realistically |
| Generic writing | Tailor specifically to Space Grant |
| Poor organization | Use clear headings matching required sections |
| Jargon overload | Write for educated non-specialists |

### Process Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Avoid |
|---------|--------------|
| Last-minute submission | Submit 24+ hours early |
| No faculty coordination | Discuss letter content with mentor |
| No external review | Get feedback from multiple readers |
| Ignoring instructions | Read requirements multiple times |

## Post-Award Obligations

If selected, you must complete:

### Required Activities

1. **Final Research Report**
   - Due at end of academic year
   - Documents research activities and outcomes

2. **Research Presentation**
   - Present at research symposium or conference
   - Opportunity to share findings publicly

3. **Outreach/Public Engagement**
   - Participate in at least one outreach event
   - Build this into your timeline

4. **Longitudinal Tracking**
   - Participate in SC Space Grant tracking program
   - Monitors career progression of awardees

5. **NASA Email Tracking**
   - Provide email updates through first year of employment
   - Required by NASA

### Forms to Complete

- NASA Media Release Form (required)
