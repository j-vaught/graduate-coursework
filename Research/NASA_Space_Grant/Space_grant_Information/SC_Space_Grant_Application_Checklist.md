# SC Space Grant Application Checklist

**CRITICAL: Main deadline is January 26, 2026 at MIDNIGHT**

---

## Undergraduate Student Research Award Checklist

### Before Applying
- [ ] Confirm U.S. citizenship
- [ ] Confirm full-time enrollment at consortium member institution
- [ ] Confirm rising sophomore, junior, or senior status
- [ ] Identify faculty mentor (not post-doc or PhD student)
- [ ] Confirm faculty has capacity (max 3 mentees across all programs)

### Application Materials
- [ ] Resume (education + work history)
- [ ] Unofficial transcript
- [ ] Research proposal (max 2 pages)
  - [ ] General project outline
  - [ ] Research methods
  - [ ] Goals and expected results
  - [ ] Project timeline
  - [ ] **Relevance to NASA**
- [ ] Personal essay (max 500 words)
  - [ ] Past activities
  - [ ] Current interests
  - [ ] Future plans
- [ ] Faculty recommendation (due Feb 2, 2026)
  - [ ] Recommender contacted
  - [ ] Materials provided to recommender

### Formatting Requirements
- [ ] Single-spaced
- [ ] 12-point font minimum
- [ ] 1-inch margins
- [ ] Unlocked, searchable PDF
- [ ] Under 10MB file size

---

## Palmetto Academy Checklist

### Before Applying
- [ ] Confirm U.S. citizenship (or U.S. territory)
- [ ] Confirm undergraduate enrollment at member institution
- [ ] Confirm first-time applicant (no prior participation)
- [ ] Review all 9 project descriptions (download PDF)
- [ ] Identify top preferences

### Application Materials
- [ ] Resume
- [ ] Academic transcript
- [ ] Personal statement (max 2 pages) addressing:
  - [ ] Why you want to participate
  - [ ] Career objectives
  - [ ] How you'll contribute to chosen research
  - [ ] Expected outcomes/learning
  - [ ] Prior NASA-related experience
- [ ] Program rankings (rank ALL 9 projects)
- [ ] Faculty recommendation (due Feb 2, 2026)

### Program Commitment
- [ ] Available May 26 - August 7, 2026
- [ ] Can attend Charleston opening ceremony
- [ ] Can attend Charleston closing ceremony
- [ ] Can commit 350+ hours
- [ ] Can arrange own housing ($500 allowance provided)
