KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS
================================================================================

CRITICAL DISCOVERY #1: NIAH Unreliability
- Perfect NIAH scores (95%+) don't predict real-world performance
- HELMET finding: Low inter-benchmark correlation (0.35-0.45)
- Synthetic tasks insufficient for capability assessment

CRITICAL DISCOVERY #2: Context Underutilization
- BABILong: Models use only 10-20% of provided context
- Even when full context available, 80-90% ignored
- Suggests fundamental architectural issue, not implementation

CRITICAL DISCOVERY #3: Perplexity Metric Failure
- Standard PPL has NO correlation with long-context tasks (r ≈ 0.0)
- LongPPL (key-token focused) strongly correlates (r ≈ 0.96)
- Paradigm shift in evaluation methodology required

CRITICAL DISCOVERY #4: Claims vs. Reality
- RULER: ~50% of claimed 32K models fail at 32K tokens
- InfiniteBench: Models claim 100K+ but struggle significantly
- Context window size ≠ effective context size

CRITICAL DISCOVERY #5: Lost-in-the-Middle
- Models perform 63% better on beginning/end vs. middle tokens
- U-shaped accuracy curves observed
- Fundamental attention/reasoning weakness
