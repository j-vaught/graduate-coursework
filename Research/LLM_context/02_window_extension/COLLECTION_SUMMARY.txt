================================================================================
MAJOR BREAKTHROUGHS IN TIMELINE
================================================================================

2020: Foundational Methods
  - Reformer (LSH Attention): O(L log L) complexity
  - Longformer: Sliding window + global attention
  - BigBird: Sparse patterns, linear complexity

2021: Position Encoding Foundation
  - RoFormer: Rotary Embeddings (RoPE) introduced
  - Became basis for all modern LLM scaling

2022: Alternative Approaches
  - ALiBi: Extrapolation through linear biases
  - Flash Attention: IO-aware exact attention

2023: Breakthrough Year - Position Interpolation Era
  - Position Interpolation (Chen et al.): Simple 32x extension
  - NTK-Aware Scaling (bloc97): Better frequency handling
  - YaRN (Peng et al.): Industry standard emerges
  - Ring Attention: Distributed training enabled
  - StreamingLLM: Attention sinks for infinite context

2024: Consolidation & Specialization
  - LongRoPE: 2M tokens achieved
  - CLEX: Smooth ODE-based extrapolation
  - E²-LLM: Extreme length with minimal data
  - LongAlign: Comprehensive training recipe
  - Surveys: "Beyond the Limits" organizes landscape
  - Specialized: Methods for specific scenarios

2025: Emerging Frontiers
  - ComRoPE: Trainable, learnable position embeddings
  - SkyLadder: Training efficiency through scheduling
  - TokenSelect: Inference efficiency focus
  - Bayesian Attention: Theoretical foundations
  - Sliding Window Training: From-scratch long-context
