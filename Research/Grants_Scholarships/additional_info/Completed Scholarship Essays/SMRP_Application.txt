Throughout my career, I have gained varied experience in the engineering field,

to understanding and enhancing the reliability of nuclear power generation.

from 18-month to 24-month fuel cycles, showing my capacity to consider
strategic, long-term efficiency and reliability in operations.
While working on a project to increase the enrichment value of nuclear fuel, I
utilized CAD (SOLIDWORKS) to calculate POSITY (porosity) of a Combustion
Engineering™ grid strap. This experience allowed me to assess and improve
product design for better reliability.
These roles have not only enabled me to refine my technical skills but also to
grow my leadership and management abilities. I believe that these experiences
make me well-suited for a role in the maintenance and reliability field.
