AREMA Personal Statement[350 words]

My fascination with the railway industry traces back to my childhood, ignited by a captivating train set that was a gift from my grandparents. This miniature world of locomotives and tracks not only became my favorite pastime but also sparked a deep curiosity about the workings of real trains and vast network of railways. That train set became a symbol of connectivity, engineering marvels, and the thrill of discovery. As I grew older, this childhood fascination evolved into an appreciation for the industry's role in shaping economies, cultures, and technological advancements.

This early interest laid the foundation for my academic and professional journey, which has been richly diverse, encompassing research in additive manufacturing, structural health monitoring, wireless communication, and control systems development. These experiences, combined with my active community engagement and leadership roles, have provided me with a unique skill set that aligns with the ever-evolving needs of the railway industry.

In my role as an Undergraduate Researcher under Dr. Austin Downey, I specialized in developing Finite Element Analysis (FEA) models for the vibration analysis of complex structures. This expertise is crucial for the railway sector, where monitoring the structural health of infrastructure is paramount for ensuring safety and reliability. Similarly, my research in Laser Powder Bed Fusion Additive Manufacturing under Dr. Lang Yuan has potential applications in developing lightweight, high-strength components for railway systems, addressing current challenges in operational efficiency and sustainability.

My interest in wireless transmission and the development of sensors for data collection has led me to work on flexible electronics and wireless communication, including creating flexible antennas. This experience is particularly relevant for enhancing railway communication systems, improving data transmission, and ensuring real-time monitoring of train operations and infrastructure conditions.

In addition to my technical contributions, my involvement in various student organizations and government roles has honed my leadership skills. As a leader in multiple organizations, and a participant in student government, I have developed a deep understanding of strategic planning, teamwork, and community engagement. These experiences have equipped me to contribute effectively to the collaborative and multidisciplinary environment of the railway industry.
