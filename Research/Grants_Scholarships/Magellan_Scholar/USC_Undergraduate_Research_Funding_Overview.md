# University of South Carolina Undergraduate Research Funding Opportunities

**Last Updated:** January 29, 2026

---

## 1. Magellan Scholar Grant

**Primary funding mechanism for undergraduate research at USC**

### Funding Details
- **Amount:** Up to $2,500 per recipient
- **Eligible Expenses:** Salary, materials/supplies, research travel
- **Disciplines:** All disciplines welcome

### Eligibility Requirements
- Minimum institutional GPA: 3.200
- Faculty mentor collaboration required (mandatory)
- All USC campuses eligible
- Projects may be research, scholarly, or creative in nature

### Application Timeline (2025-2026 Academic Year)

| Cycle | Application Deadline | Award Announcement | Grant Period |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Fall 2025 | October 8, 2025 5:00 PM | By December 15, 2025 | December 2025 - November 2026 |
| Spring 2026 | February 11, 2026 5:00 PM | By April 17, 2026 | April 2026 - March 2027 |

### Special Notes
- College of Nursing and Arnold School of Public Health have modified procedures—contact respective coordinators
- **Important:** Expected high competition for 2025-2026; lower funding percentage anticipated compared to previous years
- Uses separate application portal (NOT the general undergraduate research application form)

### Contact
Office of Undergraduate Research, Legare College Suite 120
Phone: 803-777-1141 | Email: our@sc.edu

## 2. Undergraduate Research Mini-Grants

**Smaller-scale funding for students at any research experience level**

### Funding Details
- **Amount:** Up to $1,000 per recipient
- **Eligible Expenses:** Salary, materials/supplies, research travel (NOT conference travel)
- **Disciplines:** All disciplines welcome

### Eligibility Requirements
- Faculty mentor collaboration required (mandatory)
- All USC campuses eligible
- Students at any stage of research experience eligible

### Application Timeline (2025-2026 Academic Year)

| Fall 2025 | October 21, 2025 5:00 PM | By December 13, 2025 | January - December 2026 |

### Special Notes
- **Important:** Expected high competition for 2025-2026; lower funding percentage anticipated
- Uses same general application form as other OUR grants (Magellan Scholar Grant requires separate application)

## 3. Related Magellan Programs

### Magellan Voyager Award (Conference Travel)
- **Amount:** Up to $500
- **Purpose:** Travel to regional, national, and international conferences to present research
- **Key Advantage:** Research does NOT need to be sponsored by OUR to qualify
- **Covers:** Conference registration, travel expenses

- Offered through Galen Health Fellows program
- Supports health-related research
- Contact: Galen Health Fellows, Housing Office

## 5. SPARC Graduate Research Grant Program

**Important: Graduate students only (NOT undergraduate)**

### Program Overview
- **Name:** Support to Promote Advancement of Research and Creativity
- **Amount:** Up to $5,000
- **Merit-based:** Award designed to encourage outstanding research/creativity

### Eligibility (Graduate Only)
- All PhD-degree seeking students in 2nd year or later
- Master of Fine Arts (MFA) students
- Master of Arts (MA) in Public History students (first year or later)
- Master's-level students in other programs: NOT eligible

### Application Information
- Contact Research and Grant Development Services
- Reference: go.sc.edu/SPARC

**Note:** Undergraduate students should focus on Magellan Scholar Grant and Mini-Grants instead.

## 6. NSF Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU) Programs

**External funding opportunities hosted at USC**

### Available REU Sites at USC

#### Physics and Astronomy REU
- **Duration:** 10-week summer program
- **Participants:** 8 selected undergraduates
- **Research Areas:** Nuclear/particle physics, nanotechnology, theoretical/computational modeling
- **URL:** https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/artsandsciences/physics_and_astronomy/research/reu/

#### Criminal Justice REU
- **Program Name:** Disparities in the Criminal Justice System (DCJS)
- **Duration:** 10-week summer program
- **Participants:** 9 selected undergraduates
- **Focus:** Race/ethnicity, class, gender in criminal behavior and justice practices
- **URL:** https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/artsandsciences/criminology_and_criminal_justice/beyond_the_classroom/reu/

#### Chemical Engineering CO2 REU
- **Duration:** Summer program
- **Focus:** Nanostructured materials for energy conversion and storage
- **Contact:** College of Engineering and Computing

#### Quantum Information Science REU
- **Duration:** Summer program
- **Focus:** Quantum computing and information science
- **URL:** https://reu.cse.sc.edu/

#### Mathematics REU
- **Program Name:** Mathematical Foundations of Data Analysis
- **Supported by:** NSF RTG Grant
- **URL:** https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/artsandsciences/mathematics/research/rtg/reu.php

## 12. Recommended Strategy for Funding

### Tier 1: Immediate Internal Funding
1. **Magellan Scholar Grant** (Primary - $2,500)
   - Highest funding amount
   - Broadest eligibility
   - Faculty mentorship required

2. **Mini-Grant** (Secondary - $1,000)
   - Backup option if Magellan highly competitive
   - Lower threshold for competition
   - Same faculty mentorship requirement

### Tier 2: Conference/Travel Specific
- **Magellan Voyager Award** ($500)
- **Conference Presentation Grant** ($500)
- *Combine with Tier 1 for total support*

### Tier 3: External/Summer Opportunities
- **NSF REU Programs** (varies, typically $5,000-$6,000+ for summer)
- Usually available across multiple disciplines
- Competitive but provides full summer support + stipend

### Tier 4: Specialized Programs
- **Department/College-specific programs**
- Contact your college/department directly

## 13. Application Resources

### Where to Apply
- **General OUR Grants:** https://sc.edu/about/offices_and_divisions/undergraduate_research/funding_opportunities/our_funding/
- **Magellan Scholar Grant:** Separate application portal (NOT general form)
- **Mini-Grants:** Same general application form
- **Conference Travel:** Separate application

### Key Requirements for All Applications
- Faculty mentor identification (name, department, contact)
- Project title and description
- Budget justification (salary, materials, travel)
- Compliance information (animals, human subjects, international travel)
- PDF/DOC project documentation

### Application Documents Needed
- Research proposal/project description
- Faculty mentor recommendation/support
- Budget breakdown
- Timeline/deliverables
