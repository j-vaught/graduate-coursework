**Compiled:** 2026-01-29
**Author:** J.C. Vaught

## 1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The Magellan Scholar Award is USC's flagship undergraduate research funding program that has supported outstanding student scholarship since 2006. It funds research, scholarly, and creative projects across all disciplines and campuses.

**Award Amount:** Up to $2,500 per student (Note: Some sources mention up to $3,000 for variations within the Magellan system)

**Program Scope:**
- Open to all USC system campuses
- All academic disciplines
- Research, scholarly, and creative projects
- Requires collaboration with a faculty mentor

---

## 2. ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

### 2.1 Student Eligibility

**Required:**
- **Minimum GPA:** 3.200 institutional GPA
- **Status:** USC degree-seeking student
- **Enrollment:** All academic years (freshman through senior)

### 2.2 Campus Eligibility

**ALL USC system campuses are eligible:**
- Columbia
- Aiken
- Beaufort
- Upstate
- Lancaster (Palmetto College)
- Salkehatchie (Palmetto College)
- Sumter (Palmetto College)
- Union (Palmetto College)

### 2.3 Repeat Applicants

**Resubmissions Allowed:**
- Application video is only required once (not required for repeat applicants who previously watched it)
- Must use current semester's guidebook, forms, and requirements (do NOT reuse old forms)
- Pay special attention to resubmission section in the guidebook
- Changes occur each application round

**Note:** Search results did not explicitly confirm whether previous Magellan Scholar recipients can apply for entirely new projects. Contact Office of Undergraduate Research for clarification.

### 2.4 Group Projects

**Collaborative Projects Allowed:**
- Maximum of 3 students per mentor (one group proposal OR one group + one single proposal)
- All groups must be justified in both the methods section of the proposal AND mentor collaboration form
- Mentors cannot submit a group proposal if another group project is currently funded through Magellan Scholar with the same mentor
- File naming: StudentLastName1_StudentLastName2
- USCeRA title format: Magellan-StudentLastName1_StudentLastName2-Project Title
- Special research report guidelines apply for group projects

## 4. APPLICATION PROCESS - STEP BY STEP

### 4.1 Required Steps (in any order)

1. **Watch Application Video Series**
   - Duration: ~1 hour (divided into 4 parts)
   - Required only ONCE per applicant (not required for repeat applicants)

2. **Review Scholar Guidebook**
   - Current semester version only
   - Covers eligibility, formatting, all guidelines
   - Read carefully before writing proposal

3. **Consult with Faculty Mentor**
   - Discuss project scope, timeline, submission process
   - Mentor must complete collaboration form

4. **Submit Applicant Information Form**
   - Indicates intent to apply
   - Submit ASAP

5. **Develop Project Description**
   - **Length:** 2 pages
   - Follow guidebook specifications

6. **Prepare Project Budget**
   - Use official budget form (Word document)
   - Justify all expenses

7. **Complete Submission Checklist**
   - Verify completeness before submission

8. **Obtain Mentor Collaboration Forms**
   - Primary mentor form (required)
   - Secondary mentor form (if applicable)
   - Mentors complete separately

9. **Submit Through USCeRA**
   - Combine all documents into ONE Word or PDF file
   - Components: project description + budget form + mentor form(s)
   - Deadline: 5:00 PM on submission date

### 4.2 USCeRA Submission Process

**Important:** The MENTOR submits through USCeRA, NOT the student
- Students do NOT register in USCeRA
- Mentor creates project profile in USCeRA
- Mentor registers research project
- Mentor uploads combined proposal document
- System tracks all approvals and documentation

**USCeRA Guide Available:** Separate PDF guide for mentors

**Page Limit:** 2 pages

## 7. BUDGET REQUIREMENTS

### 7.1 Award Amount & Project Period

**Maximum Award:** $2,500 per student
**Project Duration:** 12 months from grant start date OR graduation (whichever comes first)

**Grant Start Dates:**
- Spring awards: January 1
- Summer/Fall awards: May 1

### 7.2 Allowable Expenses

#### Student Salary/Hourly Compensation
- Students must be hired as hourly workers through student hire system
- Use Magellan Scholar account number
- Weekly electronic timecards required
- Indicate estimated hours/week and hourly rate by semester
- **RESTRICTION:** Cannot pay for work done BEFORE grant start date

#### Materials & Supplies
- Items, quantity, estimated price (include taxes)
- Faculty permission required BEFORE purchase
- **Unused equipment becomes university property**
- Must be returned to department or Office of Undergraduate Research

#### Travel (Domestic & International)
- Location and purpose required
- Itemized costs: transportation, lodging, registration, etc.
- **Pre-approval required at least 1 week before departure**
- Must obtain cost estimates beforehand
- Save ALL receipts
- Complete reimbursement within 10 days of return
- Must comply with USC policies and procedures
- **International:** Must comply with Education Abroad Office requirements
- **Must be completed BEFORE graduation**

#### Fringe Benefits
- Student salary multiplied by student fringe rate

### 7.3 NOT Allowed Expenses

**Explicitly Prohibited:**
- Funds for mentors (salary, benefits, travel)
- Graduate student compensation
- Funds transferred to different students
- Work performed before grant start date
- Study abroad program tuition (though room and board MAY be covered)

**Important:** Funds may ONLY be used for the student awardee(s) and the project

### 7.4 Budget Form Requirements

**Official Form:** Use Word document template (mgs_budget-form.pdf)

**Categories to Include:**
1. Student salary breakdown by semester
2. Materials/supplies with quantities and prices
3. Travel with itemized costs
4. Fringe benefits calculation

### 7.5 Spending Procedures

**Account Setup:**
- Controller's Office establishes accounts 3-4 weeks after award announcement
- Individual account for each recipient
- NOT a standard bank account

**Authorization:**
- **Mentor must authorize ALL expenditures**
- Pre-approval requires multiple weeks
- **Plan in advance**

**Compliance:**
- All expenditures must remain compliant with E fund procurement requirements

### 7.6 Unspent Funds

**Balances $100 or more:**
- Must be returned to Office of the Vice President for Research

**Balances under $100:**
- May transfer to mentor's research fund or department account

**Extensions:**
- NOT permitted beyond grant end date

## 8. POST-AWARD REQUIREMENTS

### 8.1 Initial Compliance (One-Time)

#### CITI Training - Responsible Conduct of Research (RCR)
- **Required for ALL Magellan Scholars**
- Online training module
- Valid for 3 years
- How-To Guide available
- **NOT the same as Human Subjects training**

#### Additional CITI Training (if applicable)

**For projects involving human subjects:**
- CITI Human Subjects training required
- Faculty oversight required
- Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval may be needed

**For projects involving vertebrate animals:**
- Animal Care and Use Committee approval required

#### International Research Compliance
- Education Abroad Office registration required
- Comply with all university travel policies
- USC-sponsored travel health insurance required

### 8.2 Ongoing Semester Requirements

**Each Semester During Grant Period:**
- Register project at beginning of semester
- Maintain consistent effort toward project completion

### 8.3 End-of-Grant Requirements

#### Research Presentation
**Required:** Present at ONE USC-sponsored event
- **Preferred:** Spring Discover USC
- **Alternatives:** Summer Poster Symposium OR Fall Showcase
- **Encouraged:** Multiple presentations throughout project timeline

#### Research Report
**Format:** 1-2 page research report with pictures
**Process:**
1. Draft report
2. Obtain mentor review and approval
3. Submit via Blackboard
**Deadline:** 1 month after grant end date OR before graduation (whichever comes first)
**Group Projects:** Special instructions apply (see guidebook)

### 8.4 Additional Terms & Conditions

**Full Documentation:** "Requirements, Terms, and Conditions" document must be reviewed to maintain funding eligibility

## 14. IMPORTANT REMINDERS

1. **High Competition Expected:** 2025-2026 anticipates lower funding rates than previous years due to high application volume

2. **Forms Change Each Cycle:** Always use current semester's forms and guidebook, even if reapplying

3. **Mentor Submits, Not Student:** Unlike many applications, the mentor (not student) submits through USCeRA

4. **No Retroactive Funding:** Cannot pay for work or expenses incurred before grant start date

5. **All Expenditures Need Approval:** Mentor must authorize all spending; plan weeks in advance

6. **Completion Before Graduation:** All work and travel must be completed before graduation

7. **Account Closure:** Balances $100+ returned to VP for Research; no extensions permitted

8. **Formatting Matters:** Failure to meet formatting requirements can impact application success

9. **CITI Training Valid 3 Years:** RCR and Human Subjects trainings don't need to be repeated if completed recently

10. **International Travel Registration Required:** ALL international research/internships/volunteer work must be registered with Education Abroad Office

## 15. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (Based on Research)

**Q: Can I apply if I'm a freshman?**
A: Yes, all academic years are eligible as long as you meet the 3.200 GPA requirement.

**Q: Can students from regional campuses apply?**
A: Yes, ALL USC system campuses are eligible (Columbia, Aiken, Beaufort, Upstate, and all Palmetto College campuses).

**Q: Can I reapply if I was previously declined?**
A: Yes, but use the current semester's forms and guidebook, and review the resubmission section carefully.

**Q: Can I apply again if I already received a Magellan Scholar Award?**
A: The research did not definitively answer this. Contact our@sc.edu for clarification.

**Q: Can I work with multiple students on the same project?**
A: Yes, up to 3 students per mentor, but must justify group composition in proposal and mentor form.

**Q: Who submits the application?**
A: The MENTOR submits through USCeRA, not the student.

**Q: How long is the proposal?**
A: The project description is 2 pages (plus separate budget form and mentor forms).

**Q: When do I get the money?**
A: Accounts are established 3-4 weeks after award announcement with start dates of January 1 (spring) or May 1 (summer/fall).

**Q: Can I use the money for myself as salary?**
A: Yes, but you must be hired as an hourly worker, submit weekly timecards, and can only be paid for work after the grant start date.

**Q: What if I don't spend all the money?**
A: Balances of $100+ must be returned to the Office of the VP for Research. No extensions are permitted.

**Q: Can I travel internationally with this funding?**
A: Yes, but you must register with Education Abroad Office, get travel insurance, and complete travel before graduation.

**Q: Do I need IRB approval?**
A: If your project involves human subjects, yes. You'll also need CITI Human Subjects training.

**Q: What do I have to do after I get the award?**
A: Complete CITI RCR training, register each semester, present at a USC event, and submit a 1-2 page research report.

2. **Read the Guidebook:** The current semester's guidebook is the authoritative source for all requirements

3. **Describe Your Plan Well:** According to official guidance, this is the key to success

4. **Follow Formatting Exactly:** Non-compliance with formatting can impact your application

5. **Budget Realistically:** Justify every expense; mentor must approve all spending

6. **Plan Timeline Carefully:** All work and travel must be completed before graduation

7. **Use Current Materials:** Forms change each cycle; always download fresh copies

8. **Communicate with Mentor:** Regular consultation ensures alignment on scope, timeline, and submission

9. **Attend Q&A Sessions:** Virtual sessions provide valuable insights into the review process
