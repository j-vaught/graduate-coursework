Tell us a little about yourself (include work history, hobbies, and other interests).

Hi! My name is Jacob Vaught, and I am a passionate student and engineer from the University of South Carolina. My academic interests are quite broad and include electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, and computer science, but my curiosity extends far beyond the classroom. I am very fascinated by the intersections of different fields and thrive on exploring unfamiliar territories.

I’ve been lucky enough to work under some incredible professors, each project with them being an exciting expedition into new knowledge territories. I've had the opportunity to take part in varied research groups, including the Advanced Real Time Systems Laboratory, the Additive Manufacturing Laboratory, and the Public Health Research Center. My time in these groups has allowed me to delve into areas such as finite element analysis modeling, additive manufacturing, structural health monitoring, real time systems, nuclear magnetic resonance, and my most recent addition, Heart rate monitoring and energy expenditure calculation. I was also awarded the Magellan Scholar award in Spring 2023 for my work on the development of a finite element model to predict residual stress and distortion in laser powder bed fusion additive manufacturing.

For those who've had the pleasure of knowing me deeply, they can attest that one of my greatest passions is being a mentor and imparting knowledge to others. I have done so as a Teaching Assistant for Mechatronics and Thermodynamics at the University of South Carolina, and I've relished the thrill of watching students have their own "lightbulb" moments.

In a similar vein, I have a knack for leadership, currently serving as a Student Senator, Vice President of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers, Secretary of Pi Tau Sigma, and having served as co-founder and senior advisor of the USC Rocketry Club, President of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, and President of the Society of Automotive Engineers.

Other than my academic research experience, I had the chance to intern at the renowned Westinghouse Electric Company where I honed my skills in computer-aided design, data analysis, and report writing, all while making some great friends along the way.

Outside academics and work, I believe in active participation in campus life. I am likely to be spotted on campus in a button-up shirt, khakis, and dress shoes, often paired with an interesting tie to express my unique style.
