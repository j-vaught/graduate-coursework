\section*{Problem Statement}

A closed, rigid tank contains \SI{2.50}{\kg} of water, initially a two-phase liquid-vapor mixture at \(T_1 = \SI{10}{\celsius}\). Heat transfer occurs until the tank contains only saturated vapor at \(T_2 = \SI{45}{\celsius}\). We are tasked to:

\begin{itemize}
  \item[a.] Determine the initial pressure \(p_1\) in \si{\kPa}.
  \item[b.] Determine the initial specific internal energy in \si{\kJ/\kg}.
  \item[c.] Determine the change of internal energy in \si{\kJ}.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Given Information}

\begin{align*}
T_1 &= \SI{10}{\celsius} \\
T_2 &= \SI{45}{\celsius} \\
m &= \SI{2.5}{\kg}
\end{align*}

We refer to Table A-2 for thermodynamic properties at these temperatures.

\subsection*{Solution}

\subsubsection*{Part a: Initial Pressure}

Referring to Table A-2, the initial pressure \(p_1\) is given by:
\[
p_1 = \SI{0.01228}{\bar} = \SI{1.228}{\kPa}
\]

\subsubsection*{Part b: Initial Specific Internal Energy}

From Table A-2, we have:
\[
v_{1g} = 106.379, \quad v_{1f} = 1.0004 \times 10^{-3}, \quad u_{1f} = 42, \quad u_{1g} = 2389.2
\]
The quality \(x\) can be calculated as:
\[
x = \frac{v_1 - v_{1f}}{v_{1g} - v_{1f}} = \frac{15.258 - 1.0004 \times 10^{-3}}{106.379 - 1.0004 \times 10^{-3}} = 0.1434225
\]
Hence, the initial specific internal energy \(u_1\) is:
\[
u_1 = u_{1f} + x(u_{1g} - u_{1f}) = 42 + 0.1434225(2389.2 - 42) = \SI{378.6}{\kJ/\kg}
\]

\subsubsection*{Part c: Change of Internal Energy}

The internal energy at states 1 and 2 are:
\[
U_1 = m \times u_1 = 378.6 \times 2.5 = \SI{946.5}{\kJ}
\]
\[
U_2 = m \times u_2 = 2436.8 \times 2.5 = \SI{6092}{\kJ}
\]
The change in internal energy \(\Delta U\) is:
\[
\Delta U = U_2 - U_1 = 6092 - 946.5 = \SI{5145.5}{\kJ}
\]

\section*{Summary}

The initial pressure is \(\SI{1.228}{\kPa}\), the initial specific internal energy is \(\SI{378.6}{\kJ/\kg}\), and the change in internal energy is \(\SI{5145.5}{\kJ}\).
