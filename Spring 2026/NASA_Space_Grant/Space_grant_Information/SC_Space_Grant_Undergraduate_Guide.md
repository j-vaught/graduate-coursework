## Organization Overview

### About SC Space Grant Consortium

The **South Carolina Space Grant Consortium (SCSGC)** is part of NASA's national Space Grant network, established in 1989 and operating as a "Capability Enhancement" state program since 1991. The consortium operates through the **College of Charleston's Department of Geology and Environmental Sciences**.

### Mission

> "Expand opportunities for Americans to understand and participate in NASA's aeronautics and space projects by supporting and enhancing science and engineering education, research and public outreach efforts."

The consortium works to:
- Increase South Carolina's aerospace-related research infrastructure
- Address educational pipeline challenges in STEM fields
- Connect students with NASA missions (Artemis, Europa Clipper, Hubble, Voyager, etc.)

### Member Institutions (16 total)

The consortium includes 9 four-year colleges/universities, 3 research universities, and 1 institution in the U.S. Virgin Islands:

- **College of Charleston** (Headquarters)
- **Clemson University**
- **University of South Carolina**
- Medical University of South Carolina
- Francis Marion University
- Morris College (added 2023)
- Bob Jones University (added 2024)
- And additional partner institutions

## Undergraduate Student Research Award

### Overview

The Undergraduate Student Research Award supports undergraduates conducting authentic NASA-related research during the academic year or summer at their home institution.

### Funding

- **Stipend:** $7,500
- **Minimum Hours:** 350+ hours on research project
- **No indirect costs allowed**
- Administered through institution's Grants and Sponsored Programs Office

### Eligibility Requirements

- **Citizenship:** U.S. citizen only
- **Academic Standing:** Rising sophomore, junior, or senior
- **Enrollment:** Full-time at a SC Space Grant Consortium member institution
- **Faculty Sponsor:** Must have faculty advisor sponsorship
- **Interest Area:** Demonstrated interest in aerospace/space-related studies

### Required Application Materials

1. **Resume** - Educational and work experience history

2. **Unofficial Transcripts** - From current institution

3. **Research Proposal** (Maximum 2 pages, references excluded)
   - Single-spaced, 12-point minimum font
   - Standard 1-inch margins
   - Unlocked searchable PDF (10MB max)
   - Must address:
     - General project outline
     - Research methods
     - Goals and expected results
     - Project timeline
     - **Relevance to NASA** (critical!)

4. **Personal Essay** (500 words maximum)
   - Past activities
   - Current interests
   - Future plans and career goals

5. **Faculty Letter of Recommendation**
   - From primary research mentor
   - Post-docs and PhD students are NOT eligible to submit this letter
   - Due: Monday, February 2, 2026

### Eligible Research Areas

- Basic sciences
- Science education
- Astronomy
- Planetary science
- Environmental studies
- Engineering
- Aerospace/space-related studies

### Selection Criteria

Applications are evaluated based on:
- Academic qualifications
- Recommendation letter quality
- Demonstrated interest in space science careers
- Faculty sponsorship strength
- Research proposal merit relative to NASA's mission

### Awardee Obligations

- Submit final research report at academic year end
- Present work at research symposium or conference
- Participate in SC Space Grant Longitudinal Tracking program
- Submit email updates through first year of employment (NASA requirement)
- Participate in at least one Outreach/Public Engagement activity during award period

| Faculty Recommendation Due | Monday, February 2, 2026 |

## Palmetto Academy Research Program

### Overview

The **Palmetto Academy** is a competitive 10-week summer research program providing undergraduates with intensive, hands-on research experience in STEM fields related to NASA missions and aerospace. This is an immersive, integrated multidisciplinary program.

### Program Objectives

- Provide intensive education in scientific methods
- Develop creativity, teamwork, and professional ethics
- Build confidence to pursue NASA internships and advanced STEM programs
- Enhance communication skills through written reports and oral presentations
- Foster interest in graduate-level NASA research
- Facilitate career development in aerospace and STEM fields
- Enhance workforce development within South Carolina in STEM areas

### Funding

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Base Stipend | $7,500 |
| Housing Allowance | $500 |
| **Total** | **$8,000** |

- Students arrange their own housing
- Consortium covers transportation, lodging, and per diem for opening/closing events in Charleston
- Stipends only (hourly wages not permitted)

### 2026 Program Dates

- **Start Date:** May 26, 2026
- **End Date:** August 7, 2026
- **Duration:** 10 weeks
- **Opening/Closing Events:** Charleston, SC
- **Minimum Research Hours:** 350 hours

### Eligibility Requirements

- Currently enrolled as undergraduate at SC Space Grant Consortium member institution
- U.S. citizen or possess citizenship of U.S. territories
- Demonstrated interest in STEM, aerospace, or space-related studies
- **First-time participant only** - Prior Palmetto Academy participants cannot reapply

### Required Application Materials

1. **Personal Statement** (2 pages maximum) addressing:
   - Why you want to participate in this program
   - Your career objectives
   - How you would contribute to your chosen research topic
   - What outcomes/learning experiences you anticipate
   - Any prior NASA-related research experience

2. **Program Preferences**
   - Rank ALL available programs in order of preference
   - Only one ranking per selection allowed

3. **Faculty Recommendation**
   - Due: Monday, February 2, 2026
   - Submitted through the application system

4. **Academic Transcript** - One copy from current institution

5. **Resume** - Educational history and work experience

- **Stipend:** $7,500

   - Current interests
   - Future plans

   - Due: Monday, February 2, 2026

## NASA Student Internships

### Overview

The SC Space Grant Consortium provides funding for junior and senior college students to participate in various NASA internship programs, including NASA Academy and the Undergraduate Research Program. These programs enable students to work 10 weeks on-site with a mentor at NASA centers.

### Funding

| Level | Stipend |
|-------|---------|
| Undergraduate | $8,200 |
| Graduate | $9,900 |

### Eligibility Requirements

- U.S. citizen
- Attend a consortium member institution
- Rising junior or senior
- Faculty advisor sponsorship required
- **Must have applied/been accepted to a NASA Center internship program**
- Demonstrate interest in aerospace and space-related studies

### Required Application Materials

1. **Education Information** with unofficial transcripts

2. **One Letter of Reference**

3. **Internship Essay** (500 words)
   - Background
   - Interests
   - Future plans
   - Career goals

4. **List of NASA Internships Applied To**

### Eligible NASA Programs

- NASA Academy (Goddard, Ames, other centers)
- Undergraduate Research Program (USRP) at Langley
- Langley's LARSS program
- ESMD programs
- JPL Internships
- Kennedy Space Center programs
- Marshall Space Flight Center programs

### Important Note

**Students must apply directly to NASA programs AND separately to SC Space Grant for funding support.** The SC Space Grant application supplements your NASA internship application - it does not replace it.

| Milestone | Date |
|-----------|------|
| Application Due | Friday, February 27, 2026 (11:59 p.m. ET) |
| NASA Selection Period | January 2026 - April 2026 |
| Program Duration | June 1 - August 7, 2026 (10 weeks) |

## Application Tips & Best Practices

### Before You Apply

1. **Identify a faculty mentor** - Start this process early; faculty are limited to 3 mentees
2. **Review all programs** - Determine which best fits your interests and timeline
3. **Read project descriptions** - For Palmetto Academy, download and review all project blurbs
4. **Prepare your materials** - Have transcript and resume ready before starting

### Writing Your Research Proposal

1. **Address NASA relevance explicitly** - This is critical for selection
2. **Be specific about methods** - Show you understand how to approach the research
3. **Include a realistic timeline** - Demonstrate you've thought through the work
4. **Stay within page limits** - 2 pages maximum, no exceptions
5. **Use required formatting** - Single-spaced, 12pt font, 1-inch margins

### Writing Your Personal Essay

1. **Connect your past to your future** - Show a coherent narrative
2. **Highlight NASA/aerospace interests** - Even tangential connections matter
3. **Be specific about career goals** - Vague statements are less compelling
4. **Stay within word limits** - 500 words maximum

### For Palmetto Academy Specifically

1. **Rank ALL programs** - You must rank every available project
2. **Tailor your statement** - Address all five required points
3. **Highlight prior NASA experience** - If you have any
4. **Note this is first-time only** - Prior participants cannot reapply

### Faculty Recommendation

1. **Choose carefully** - Post-docs and PhD students cannot write these
2. **Give advance notice** - Contact your recommender well before the deadline
3. **Provide context** - Give them your resume and research proposal
4. **Remind them of the deadline** - Recommendations are due February 2, 2026

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Missing the midnight deadline (applications close at midnight on January 26)
- Forgetting to coordinate faculty recommendation
- Not explicitly addressing NASA relevance
- Exceeding page or word limits
- Submitting locked or unsearchable PDFs
- Applying for programs you've already participated in (Palmetto Academy)
