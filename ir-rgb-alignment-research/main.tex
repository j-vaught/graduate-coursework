\vfill
{\normalsize
This document surveys methods for dense, per-pixel registration of infrared (IR) and
visible (RGB) imagery using deep learning. It presents a proposed architecture
(IRAlign-Net) that predicts displacement fields with confidence masks, along with
comprehensive coverage of augmentation strategies, loss functions, and training
methodologies for the limited-data regime of a few thousand aligned video frames.\par}
\end{titlepage}
