\begin{abstract}
Machine-learning models for marine radar are often trained on synthetic data because real labeled logs are expensive to collect. A key challenge is the sim-to-real gap: models that perform well in simulation can fail on real sensor data. This proposal studies an environment-aware reinforcement learning controller that tunes simulator parameters using feedback from held-out real validation data, with environment labels such as lake, river, and coast. We compare the method against fixed randomization, Bayesian optimization, and unconditioned RL baselines, and evaluate both performance gains and sample efficiency under domain holdout and stress conditions.
\end{abstract}
