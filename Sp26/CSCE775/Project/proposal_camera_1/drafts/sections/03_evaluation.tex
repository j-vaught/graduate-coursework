\subsection{Experimental Setup}
We evaluate on maritime and multimodal IR/RGB benchmarks with explicit domain-shift slices (e.g., day-to-night, clear-to-fog) and misalignment stress conditions. Splits are organized by condition and sequence to avoid leakage.

\subsection{Baselines}
We compare against five classes of methods:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Classical fixed registration}: feature-based matching + RANSAC.
    \item \textbf{Fixed augmentation}: static hand-designed augmentation schedule.
    \item \textbf{RandAugment}: global randomized augmentation policy \cite{cubuk2020randaugment}.
    \item \textbf{AutoAugment/PBA}: offline searched static policy \cite{cubuk2019autoaugment,ho2019population}.
    \item \textbf{Context-free RL}: same control pipeline without context features.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Metrics}
Primary metrics are mAP@50 (detection) and mean reprojection error (alignment quality). Secondary metrics include performance drop under induced misalignment, robustness under adverse weather/lighting shifts, and preprocessing latency overhead.

\subsection{Hypothesis Validation}
Success is defined as outperforming static augmentation and fixed-registration baselines on the hardest shift splits while maintaining acceptable compute overhead. We also analyze action-selection behavior to verify policy adaptivity (e.g., choosing conservative transforms when feature support is weak).
