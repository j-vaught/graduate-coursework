\vspace{0.4cm}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Modular:} Any backbone (ViT-S, ViT-L, ViT-G) plugs in; CSTM is backbone-agnostic
  \item \textbf{Two key innovations:} Canonical Camera Space Transform (CSTM) and Joint Depth-Normal Optimization
\end{itemize}
