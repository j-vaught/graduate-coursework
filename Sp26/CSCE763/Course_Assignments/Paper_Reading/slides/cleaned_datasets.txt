Booster High-resolution stereo pairs with fine-grained, non-Lambertian surfaces (transparent/reflective objects like glasses, bottles, mirrors).

ETH3D Indoor and outdoor scenes with LiDAR ground truth. Mix of architectural interiors, building facades, and courtyards.

Middlebury Classic close-range stereo benchmark with tabletop/lab scenes at very high resolution. Extremely precise sub-pixel ground truth.

NuScenes Autonomous driving dataset from Boston and Singapore with LiDAR ground truth. Multiple cameras on a car roof capturing 360-degree street scenes.

Sintel Synthetic benchmark from an animated movie. Provides dense optical flow and depth for complex, dynamic scenes with motion blur, fog, and specular effects.

SunRGBD Indoor scenes (bedrooms, offices, kitchens) captured with 4 different RGB-D sensors (Kinect v1/v2, Xtion, RealSense).

NYUv2 Dense indoor depth from Kinect v1 across 464 scenes (apartments, offices, stores).

KITTI Outdoor driving with sparse LiDAR ground truth from a Velodyne sensor.
