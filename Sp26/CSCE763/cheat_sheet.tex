\section{Image Operations}
\small

{\color{garnet}\textbf{Negative}} ($s = L{-}1{-}r$):
Inverts all intensities. Dark becomes bright, bright becomes dark.

{\color{garnet}\textbf{Log Transform}} ($s = c\log(1+r)$):
Compresses high intensities, expands low ones. Reveals detail in dark regions.

{\color{garnet}\textbf{Power-Law / Gamma}} ($s = cr^\gamma$):
$\gamma < 1$: brightens dark regions.
$\gamma > 1$: darkens image, suppresses low intensities.

{\color{garnet}\textbf{Contrast Stretching}}:
Maps a narrow intensity range to the full $[0, L{-}1]$ range. Increases overall contrast.

{\color{garnet}\textbf{Intensity-Level Slicing}}:
Highlights a specific intensity band while suppressing or preserving all others.

{\color{garnet}\textbf{Bit-Plane Slicing}}:
Decomposes into 8 binary planes. Higher planes carry structure; lower planes carry fine detail/noise.

{\color{garnet}\textbf{Thresholding}} ($s = 0$ or $L{-}1$):
Produces binary image. Separates foreground from background.

{\color{atlantic}\textbf{Histogram Equalization}}:
Flattens histogram to use full intensity range. Increases global contrast.

{\color{atlantic}\textbf{Histogram Matching}}:
Reshapes histogram to match a specified target distribution.

{\color{atlantic}\textbf{Local Histogram Processing}}:
Applies equalization in sliding neighborhoods. Reveals local detail that global methods miss.

{\color{congaree}\textbf{Addition / Averaging}}:
$\bar{g}(x,y) = \frac{1}{K}\sum g_i(x,y)$. Reduces noise variance by $1/K$. Images must be registered.

{\color{congaree}\textbf{Subtraction}}:
Highlights differences between two images. Removes static background.

{\color{congaree}\textbf{Multiplication / Division}}:
Mask with ROI to isolate regions. Division corrects uneven illumination/shading.

{\color{horseshoe}\textbf{Smoothing (Box / Gaussian)}}:
Averages neighborhood pixels. Reduces noise but blurs edges.

{\color{horseshoe}\textbf{Median Filter}} (nonlinear):
Replaces pixel with neighborhood median. Removes salt-and-pepper noise while preserving edges.

{\color{horseshoe}\textbf{Sharpening (Laplacian, Unsharp Mask)}}:
Enhances edges and fine detail. Makes transitions crisper.

{\color{rose}\textbf{NOT}}: swaps foreground/background.
{\color{rose}\textbf{AND}}: intersection of two masks.
{\color{rose}\textbf{OR}}: union of two masks.
{\color{rose}\textbf{XOR}}: pixels in one mask but not both.
