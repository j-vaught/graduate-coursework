## LINEAR SYSTEMS


Many processes in our world can be described by statespace systems. These include processes in engineering, economics, physics, chemistry, biology, and many other areas. If we can derive a mathematical model for a process, then we can use the tools of mathematics to control the process and obtain information about the process. This is why statespace systems are so important to engineers. If we know the state of a system at the present time, and we know all of the present and future inputs, then we can deduce the values of all future outputs of the system. Statespace models can be generally divided into linear models and nonlinear models. Although most real processes are nonlinear, the mathematical tools that are available for estimation and control are much more accessible and well under- **stood for linear systems. That is why nonlinear systems are often approximated as** linear systems. That way we can use the tools that have been developed for linear systems to derive estimation or control algorithms.
