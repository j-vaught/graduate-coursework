\section*{Problem \#1}
Assume that the received power at the receive antenna is \(1 \times 10^{-10}\) W. 
The RF cable between the receive antenna and RF circuits for down conversion attenuates 
the received signal power by a factor of 10.

\subsection*{Solution for Part a}
To find the power at the antenna in dBW, the formula used is:
\[
P_{\text{dBW}} = 10 \log_{10}(P_{\text{W}})
\]
\[
P_{\text{dBW}} = 10 \log_{10}(1 \times 10^{-10}) = -100 \, \text{dBW}
\]

\subsection*{Solution for Part b}
To find the power at the antenna in dBm, first convert the power in W to mW and then use the dBm formula:
\[
P_{\text{dBm}} = 10 \log_{10}(P_{\text{mW}})
\]
\[
P_{\text{dBm}} = 10 \log_{10}(1 \times 10^{-7}) = -70 \, \text{dBm}
\]

\subsection*{Solution for Part c}
To find the received power at the input of the RF circuit in dBm, we take the attenuation into account.
\[
P_{\text{dBm, RF}} = 10 \log_{10}(1 \times 10^{-8}) = -80 \, \text{dBm}
\]

\subsection*{Solution for Part d}
To find the attenuation due to the cable in dB, we use the attenuation formula:
\[
\text{Attenuation in dB} = 10 \log_{10}\left(\frac{\text{Initial Power}}{\text{Final Power}}\right)
\]
\[
\text{Attenuation} = 10 \log_{10}\left(\frac{1 \times 10^{-7}}{1 \times 10^{-8}}\right) = 10 \, \text{dB}
\]

\subsection*{Solution for Part e}
To find the SNR in dB, we use the following formula:
\[
\text{SNR in dB} = P_{\text{dBm, RF}} - (-100 \, \text{dBm})
\]
\[
\text{SNR} = -80 \, \text{dBm} + 100 \, \text{dBm} = 20 \, \text{dB}
\]

\subsection*{Solution for Part f}
To find the SNR in dB with thermal noise, we first calculate the thermal noise power and then use it in the SNR formula.
Thermal noise power:
\[
P_{\text{Thermal}} = 1.38 \times 10^{-23} \times 290 \times 100 \times 10^6 = 4 \times 10^{-15} \, \text{W} = -114 \, \text{dBm}
\]
SNR with thermal noise:
\[
\text{SNR with Thermal noise} = -80 \, \text{dBm} - (-114 \, \text{dBm}) = 34 \, \text{dB}
\]
