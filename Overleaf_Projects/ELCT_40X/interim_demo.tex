\textbf{\LARGE Interim Demo Contributions Report}

\textit{JC Vaught}
\end{center}

\vspace{1em}

For the interim demo, our team used two ADALM Pluto SDRs. One SDR simulated multiple antenna signals, while the other received and attempted to decode them. While one of our planned software demos failed, we successfully demonstrated the hardware system. This demonstration highlighted the capabilities of our SDR-based GPS system and allowed us to identify key areas for further improvement.

As the antenna lead, my primary role was selecting, testing, and integrating the antenna. After reviewing several options, I chose an automotive antenna for its balance of performance and cost. This selection involved a detailed comparison of performance metrics such as gain, frequency range, and cost-effectiveness. Once the antenna was selected, I verified the SDR’s Bias Tee functionality to ensure it could power the active antenna. I also sourced the necessary adapters to integrate the antenna into the system, which was not straightforward. Integration presented several challenges, including signal inconsistencies and delays caused by missing components. These issues delayed our project.

I collaborated closely with Parker, who led the SDR subsystem, to integrate the antenna and SDR into a cohesive system. This process included setting up the hardware, resolving persistent signal issues, and adjusting software configurations to improve data interpretation. One of my key contributions was ensuring that the antenna subsystem provided a reliable input for the SDR, which involved iterative testing and refinement. This allowed us to demonstrate signal decoding during the demo.

In addition to hardware responsibilities, I contributed to software efforts by working on the MATLAB correlator code for GPS signal processing. This included testing the code to ensure it decomposed signals correctly and provided accurate outputs for further analysis. The results were less accurate than previously hoped.

My involvement extended to assisting with the testing and debugging process during the demo setup. I helped identify issues with the SDR configuration and worked with the team to optimize the decoding process. This collaborative effort ensured that the demonstration showcased the progress we’ve made while also providing valuable insights for addressing current limitations.
