Discussion for part b)

When \(U_{F}\) is lower than the flutter speed the system is stable with
no imaginary components in the eigenvalues. The damping shows that the
oscillations decay. When \(U_{F}\) is at the flutter speed the system
becomes unstable and imaginary components appear in the eigenvalues. The
damping shows that the oscillations don't decay. When \(U_{F}\)
surpasses the flutter speed the system becomes increasingly unstable
with imaginary components. The damping shows that the oscillations grow.
