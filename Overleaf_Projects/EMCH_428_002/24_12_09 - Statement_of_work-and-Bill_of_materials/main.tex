\section*{1. Scope of the Project}
Our design team will construct a Rain Barrel Water Pump System that effectively utilizes collected rainwater to irrigate surrounding plants. The system will include the following features:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Solar Power System:} Solar panels and a 100Ah battery to ensure consistent use.
    \item \textbf{120V AC Pump:} A 1525 GPH, 115VAC, 3/4 hp pump coupled with a 1500W inverter.
    \item \textbf{Dual Spigots:} Two spigots allowing for both pressurized and manual operation.
    \item \textbf{Pump House:} A protective pump cover to safeguard the pump from environmental factors.
    \item \textbf{Winterization Features:} Elements that allow draining of pipes to prevent damage.
\end{itemize}
Refer to \textbf{Attachment 1} for detailed illustrations of the prototype (e.g., CAD diagrams).
