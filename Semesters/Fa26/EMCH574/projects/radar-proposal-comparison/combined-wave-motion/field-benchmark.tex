\subsection{Comparison with a field-fitted pitch response}

We compared the prescribed peak pitch with a published model fitted to inertial measurements from the 5 m AutoNaut. Mounet et al. fitted a linear transfer function using measured motion spectra and modeled directional waves from a coastal deployment \cite{Mounet2024Hydrodynamic}. We evaluated their weighted-average parameter set at zero forward speed and a wave propagation angle of $25^\circ$ to the bow. Multiplying the response amplitude by the regular-wave amplitude gives $3.47^\circ$ at $0.350$ Hz and $0.150$ m, and $9.21^\circ$ at $0.3325$ Hz and $0.432$ m. The prescribed oscillator gives $3.20^\circ$ and $8.28^\circ$ at those two points.

The two models give peak pitches within $12\%$ under these conditions. This comparison establishes the scale of the prescribed amplitudes relative to a field-fitted transfer function for another hull. The calibrated spectrum magnitudes leave the phase between pitch and the local crest unresolved, and the radar study retains its prescribed frequency response and phase.
