# AutoNaut pitch amplitude comparison

The calculation uses the field-fitted linear pitch transfer function of a 5 m AutoNaut. Table 4 gives weighted-average parameters $L=4.360$ m, $B=1.180$ m, and $T=0.265$ m, with alternate inputs for the simple-mean fit and the default geometry. Section 2.2 sets the block coefficient to unity. The length, breadth, and draft in the fitted model are effective geometry. The dimensions absorb effects such as the appendages that the box geometry does not represent. [Mounet et al. 2024, §§2.2, 4.4, 5.1.2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.measurement.2024.114724)

The radar model's wave vector points in the direction of propagation, matching the relative propagation angle defined by Mounet et al. in Fig. 1. The unsigned angle to the bow is $25^\circ$. At $U=0$ the encounter frequency equals the intrinsic frequency and the pitch amplitude depends on the heading through $k_e=|k\cos\beta|$. Both angular sign conventions, and a reversal by $180^\circ$, give the same amplitude at zero speed. The input to NetSSE `pitchCF` is $2\pi f$ in rad/s. The output is rad/m and gives peak pitch after multiplication by the first-harmonic surface amplitude, followed by conversion to degrees. [Mounet et al. 2024, Fig. 1 and §2.2.1](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.measurement.2024.114724), [NetSSE source documentation](https://netsse.readthedocs.io/en/latest/_modules/netsse/model/ship.html#pitchCF)

`src/autonaut_pitch_benchmark.py` reduces the published expressions to zero forward speed. The Smith correction uses $\exp(-kT)$ to match the authors' NetSSE implementation. Jensen et al. printed $\exp(-k_eT)$ in Eq. (2.10), an alternative recorded in the CSV. The `autonaut_rao_rad_m` column matches `pitchCF` from NetSSE 2.0.0. The package is publicly archived. The associated DTU Orbit record catalogs software and documentation. Mounet et al. report that their measurement data are available on request. [Jensen et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0029-8018(03)00108-2), [NetSSE 2.0.0](https://pypi.org/project/netsse/2.0.0/), [NetSSE DTU Orbit record](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/datasets/netsse/)

The source study fits model parameters from assumed wave spectra and measured motion spectra in a moving vessel. Its wave hindcast covers 0.03 to 1 Hz, and it reports the bulk of the wave energy below 0.4 Hz. The weighted fit used 152 of 324 stationary segments. The representative transect in Fig. 5 shows segment-mean speeds concentrated around 0.15 to 0.32 m/s. Fig. 12 curves use 1 m/s and selected bearings, so the zero-speed values are evaluations of the model at the radar study conditions. The authors report that the AutoNaut's true transfer functions are unknown. This benchmark checks the amplitude scale of the radar study's assumed pitch against the fitted model. It supplies neither a measured pitch-to-wave phase nor field radar outcomes. [Mounet et al. 2024, §§3.2–3.3, 4.4, 5.2–5.3](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.measurement.2024.114724)

Generate the comparison with `uv run python src/autonaut_pitch_benchmark.py --output data/autonaut_pitch_benchmark.csv`.
