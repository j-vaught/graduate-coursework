# Independent operating study audit

The audit used COMECH-2080 and the final `src/paired_experiment.py` and `data/experiment-results.json`. All 111 operating-map points satisfy the proposal limits $ka \le 0.20$, peak pitch $\le 10^\circ$, and peak roll $\le 10^\circ$. The maximum retained steepness is 0.19747 and maximum pitch amplitude is $8.284^\circ$. The regular-wave roll amplitude is 0.280 times the pitch amplitude for the prescribed heading and forcing. For the irregular reference, the triangle-inequality upper bounds are 0.195361 for combined slope, $6.246^\circ$ for pitch, and $1.748^\circ$ for roll.

The zero-wave control returned all 10 visits usable for all three cases and zero mapping error. Across the stored map, below-threshold, acquired-but-outside-tolerance, and usable fractions sum to one. Midpoint and spoke processing share the same below-threshold fractions. The per-spoke and level-pointing mapping errors are near numerical precision with exact measurements and the true target elevation. This is the expected oracle property of the inverse-plane reconstruction.

The reference case and two largest correction-gain map points were rerun for all 32 random phase realizations using the same simulation implementation. Their fractions and gains match the stored results to numerical precision. For $f=0.3325$ Hz and $a=0.432$ m, the fractions are 0.142904, 0.380534, and 0.389323. The paired correction gain is 0.237630 with a 0.004119 normal-interval half-width over independently sampled initial phases. These intervals describe phase sampling for the stated deterministic model, not uncertainty in the model or its parameters.

The final pilot at $f=0.35$ Hz and $a=0.36$ m was independently recomputed using 1201 path points. Of 192 visits, 96 fall below threshold, 48 are acquired with midpoint position error above 0.50 m, and 48 are usable with midpoint processing. Per-spoke and ideal-level processing each retain 96 usable visits in this realization. Its steepness is 0.17747. The matched-pitch comparison contains 24 retained cases, with four frequencies, three scan periods, and two pitch phases. The same code was used for recomputing numerical cases, so this audit checks reproducibility and physical invariants rather than constituting a separate implementation.
