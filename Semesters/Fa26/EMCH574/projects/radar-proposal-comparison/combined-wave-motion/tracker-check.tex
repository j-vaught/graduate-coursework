\subsection{Holding the last fix with orbital buoy motion}
The buoy moved horizontally by $-a\sin\chi$ along wave travel, where $\chi$ is the wave phase at its mean position. We held the last acquired finite fix and scored it against contemporaneous truth at every visit. Visits before the first fix counted as unusable. With range and bearing noise standard deviations of 0.25~m and $0.25^\circ$, midpoint and spoke-time usability were 14.3\% and 24.5\% for fresh fixes and 37.7\% and 50.8\% with holding at the strongest-gain condition. At the reference condition, the corresponding fractions were 56.3\% and 59.9\% for fresh fixes and 60.7\% and 65.2\% with holding.
