\section{Numerical verification}
Across 19 frequency ratios, direct pitch integration and the analytical solution agreed within $5.50\times10^{-12}$ in relative amplitude and $3.47\times10^{-10}$ degrees in phase. An independent range-circle and bearing-plane intersection reproduced mapped positions within $6\times10^{-14}$ m in three conditions. Refining the ray from 601 to 2,401 subdivisions changed margins by at most 0.0211 dB there and preserved all classifications. The negative leveling gains remained negative under further refinement at the 0.4475 Hz map cell ($-0.31$ to $-0.26$ points) and the off-grid 0.355 Hz, 0.155 m cell ($-1.06$ to $-0.90$ points).
