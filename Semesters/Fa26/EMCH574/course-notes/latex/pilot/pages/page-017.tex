\section{Spring--Mass Vibrating Systems}
Consider a vibrating system consisting of a mass $m$ and a spring of stiffness $k$ as shown in Figure 1.

\setcounter{figure}{0}
\PilotFigure{p017-horizontal-spring-mass}{Spring-mass vibration system}

\subsection{Free Vibration of Spring--Mass Systems}
Assume the mass $m$ is displaced from the equilibrium position by a time-dependent displacement $u(t)$.

\subsubsection{Free-Body Diagram}
As shown in Figure 1, the free body diagram (FBD) of the mass $m$ gives the sum of $x$-direction forces as
\begin{equation}
    \sum F_x=-k u(t)
    \tag{1}
\end{equation}
where $k u(t)$ is the reaction force from the spring.

\subsubsection{Newton's Law of Motion}
Newton's law of motion (NLM) applied to the mass $m$ is stated as
\begin{equation}
    \sum F_x=m\ddot{u}(t).
    \tag{2}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Equation of Motion}
Substitution of Eq.~(1) into Eq.~(2) yields
\begin{equation}
    m\ddot{u}(t)=-k u(t).
    \tag{3}
\end{equation}
