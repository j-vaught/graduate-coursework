\section*{Prompts \& Advice for Writers}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Finding Stories}

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    Focus on things that aren't in the direct spotlight.
  \item
    Pay attention to what's going on around you.
  \item
    When you see unfairness, think about that as a story.
  \item
    Pay attention to what bothers you personally.
  \item
    Write about things that are topical and current.
  \item
    Look for interesting personalities on campus to submit letters to
    the editor.
  \item
    Reach out to student leaders who want to get their own message out.
  \item
    Ask students: What do you go through in your daily life?
  \item
    Talk to strangers.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Political Angles}

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    Ask students: Are you registered to vote?
  \item
    Look into voter apathy.
  \item
    What elections are coming up?
  \item
    Remember: The governor decides how much funding the university gets
    from the state.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
