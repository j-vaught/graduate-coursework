\section*{Finding Your Story}

\subsection*{Look Around You}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Focus on things that aren't in the direct spotlight
    \item Pay attention to what's going on around you every day
    \item When you see unfairness, think about that as a potential story
    \item Pay attention to what personally bothers you---those frustrations often resonate with others
    \item Write about things that are topical and current
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Talk to People}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Talk to strangers---you never know what stories they have
    \item Ask students: What do you go through in your daily life?
    \item Look for interesting personalities on campus who might submit letters to the editor
    \item Reach out to student leaders who want to get their own message out
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Follow Your Curiosity}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item If you keep seeing the same problem, there's probably a story there
    \item When you hear a rumor, verify it---if true, it could be newsworthy
    \item Notice patterns: Are multiple students complaining about the same thing?
\end{itemize}

\section*{Political \& Civic Angles}

\subsection*{Voter Engagement}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Ask students: Are you registered to vote? Why or why not?
    \item Investigate voter apathy---what's causing it?
    \item What elections are coming up? (local, state, federal)
    \item Remember: The governor decides how much funding the university gets from the state
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Policy Impact}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item How do state policies directly affect students?
    \item What's happening with education funding in South Carolina?
    \item Are there any bills in the legislature that would impact USC?
\end{itemize}

\section*{Types of Articles}

\subsection*{Opinion Pieces}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Take a clear stance on an issue
    \item Support your argument with facts and examples
    \item Acknowledge counterarguments but explain why your position is stronger
    \item End with a call to action or clear conclusion
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{News Articles}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Present factual information without personal bias
    \item Include quotes from multiple sources
    \item Cover the who, what, when, where, why, and how
    \item Verify all claims before publishing
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Features}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Tell a story that goes deeper than breaking news
    \item Profile interesting people or organizations
    \item Explore trends or issues over time
    \item Use narrative techniques to engage readers
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Editorials}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item How the university handles its own history and state history
    \item Reflective pieces from seniors looking back on their experiences at USC
    \item Student leaders who want to share their message
\end{itemize}

\section*{Article Angles Framework}

When developing any topic, consider writing it from multiple perspectives:

\subsection*{The ``Pro'' Angle}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item What are the benefits of this policy/trend/decision?
    \item Who does it help and how?
    \item What positive outcomes could result?
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The ``Con'' Angle}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item What are the drawbacks or risks?
    \item Who is harmed or disadvantaged?
    \item What could go wrong?
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The ``News'' Angle}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item What are the objective facts?
    \item What do official sources say?
    \item What data or statistics are available?
\end{itemize}

\section*{Questions to Ask Yourself}

Before pitching or writing:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Why does this matter to USC students right now?
    \item Who is affected by this issue?
    \item What do I want readers to think, feel, or do after reading?
    \item Is this timely? Is there a news hook?
    \item Do I have access to sources who can speak on this?
    \item Has this been covered recently? If so, what's my new angle?
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Quick Story Starters}

When you're stuck, try these prompts:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item What's the biggest complaint you've heard from friends this week?
    \item What changed on campus recently that nobody's talking about?
    \item What's something everyone assumes is true but might not be?
    \item What would you change about USC if you could?
    \item What question do new students always ask that has a complicated answer?
    \item What's the most unfair thing happening on campus right now?
    \item What's an unsung hero or overlooked group on campus?
    \item What do students spend money on that they shouldn't have to?
    \item What do administrators say vs. what actually happens?
    \item What's working really well that nobody gives credit for?
\end{enumerate}
