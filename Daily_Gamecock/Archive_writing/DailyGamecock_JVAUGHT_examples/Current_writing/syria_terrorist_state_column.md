# Column: How the West and Turkey Turned Syria Into a Terrorist State

[Ahmed al-Sharaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_al-Sharaa), the champion of Turkey's state-sponsored terrorism, has been embraced by the West. This is despite his ongoing massacre of the Alawites, the former ruling ethnic group of the country. It is clear that the terrorist government will not produce the democracy they claim would happen after the ousting of Bashar al-Assad.

Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa was the leader of [Hayat Tahrir al-Sham](https://www.wilsoncenter.org/article/al-qaedas-latest-rebranding-hayat-tahrir-al-sham), a rebranded version of Al-Nusra Front. Al-Nusra Front is better known by its nickname Al-Qaeda in Syria. In 2012, then-spokesperson for the U.S. State Department Victoria Nuland [said](https://2009-2017.state.gov/r/pa/prs/ps/2012/12/201759.htm) it was an alias for Al-Qaeda in Iraq, a year before AQI became known as the Islamic State.

However, as Hayat Tahrir al-Sham splintered from Al-Qaeda, they claim to be reformed now. Unlike the more secular Assad government, this U.S.-backed new government believes in mandating women to wear the hijab. This is despite the U.S. backing of Iranian protesters who have become known for burning their hijabs as protest against the Islamic Republic of Iran.

So why would the West and Turkey be willing to work with Al-Qaeda to overthrow Bashar al-Assad? In 2015, the Brookings Institution [said](https://www.brookings.edu/blog/markaz/2015/03/23/are-syrian-islamists-moving-to-counterbalance-al-qaeda-will-it-last/) that "the vast majority of the Syrian insurgency has coordinated closely with Al-Qaeda since mid-2012 — and to great effect on the battlefield."

One of the Syrian opposition groups, Al-Nusra, was known to have its hands in what is referred to as the Free Syrian Army. [Jamal Maarouf](https://www.meforum.org/mef-online/the-syrian-revolutionaries-front-interview-with-jamal-maarouf), leader of the Syrian Revolutionaries Front, said that the Al-Nusra Front "had either bought or terrorized F.S.A. fighters into compliance."

Moreover, Army Lt. Gen. Michael Flynn [said on Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2015/7/16/retired-us-general-drones-cause-more-damage-than-good) that the United States government made a willful decision to give arms to groups like Al-Qaeda in Iraq, which is now ISIS, along with the Muslim Brotherhood and the Salafists. These three groups, according to a [Defense Intelligence Agency document](https://archive.org/details/DIASyriaDocsAlQaedaRebels), are the major forces driving the insurgency against Syria, at least when the document was written in August 2012. This is followed by a statement that said, "The West, Gulf countries, and Turkey support the opposition while Russia, China, and Iran support the regime."

This implies that the opposition being referenced in this document is the three groups mentioned, which Flynn helps corroborate. The Defense Intelligence Agency document would also predict a Salafist principality, which would come true when Al-Qaeda in Iraq eventually formed into ISIS. The DIA in the document would express support for that Salafist principality, saying this is "exactly what the supporting powers to the opposition want, in order to isolate the Syrian regime."

The United States overthrow of Libya, according to [CNN](http://edition.cnn.com/2011/WORLD/africa/08/26/libya.militants.analysis/), led to the U.S.-backed Libyan rebels freeing Al-Qaeda supporters in Tripoli. Moreover, [MI6 whistleblower David Shayler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Shayler) alleged that British intelligence paid large sums of money to an Al-Qaeda cell in Libya to kill Muammar Gaddafi.

The Libyan Al-Qaeda cell included [Anas al-Liby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Anas_al-Libi), who despite being a high-ranking Al-Qaeda operative was given political asylum in Britain and lived in Manchester until May 2000, when he eluded a police raid on his house and fled abroad.

The Turkish government has had accusations against them over their involvement with ISIS. Allegations range from military cooperation and weapons transfers to logistical support, financial assistance and the provision of medical services. One of these accusations comes from leaked [WikiLeaks documents](https://wikileaks.org/berats-box/article) that say Berat Albayrak, son-in-law of Recep Tayyip Erdogan and Turkish minister of energy and natural resources, may have had indirect ties to ISIS's oil trade through a company called Powertrans.

More accusations came from Joe Biden as vice president. He would have to [apologize](https://www.cnn.com/2014/10/05/politics/isis-biden-erdogan-apology/index.html) for talking about how Turkey and other Middle East allies funded Al-Nusra and Al-Qaeda. Biden would also talk about how Turkey allowed foreign fighters to cross the Turkish border to Syria to join ISIS.

That is not to say the West and Turkey are the only ones supporting fundamental terrorism. The United States allies Saudi Arabia and Qatar have faced allegations of [private donors funding ISIS](https://www.channel4.com/news/factcheck/factcheck-qa-is-saudi-arabia-funding-isis). The same could be said for Israel, as while ISIS declared war on Hamas, Israel has worked with the [Abu Shabab gang](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2025/12/4/who-was-yasser-abu-shabab-israel-backed-militia-leader-killed-in-gaza). Their leader, Yasser Abu Shabab, who operated the gang before his death, previously did drugs and arms smuggling with ISIS ties. Yair Golan, a leader of an opposition party, The Democrats, [said](https://news.antiwar.com/2025/06/05/israel-is-arming-an-isis-affiliated-gang-in-southern-gaza/) that Netanyahu was "promoting a new dangerous concept: arming a Gazan militia with ties to ISIS."

Turkey and the West have strangled the Middle East. It is time for the Middle East to be independent from those who want to exploit it for their own gain. Syria deserves to be ruled by those who are willing to do what is best for Syria and not what is best for the West or Turkey.
