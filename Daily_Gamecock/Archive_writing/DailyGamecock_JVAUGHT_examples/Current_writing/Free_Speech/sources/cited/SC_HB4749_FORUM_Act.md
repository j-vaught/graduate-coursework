# SC HB 4749: FORUM Act of 2026

## Bill Summary

The "Forming Open and Robust University Minds (FORUM) Act" aims to protect student expression on public higher education campuses. It establishes measures for safeguarding student and student organization speech in designated campus areas, sets institutional policy requirements, and creates enforcement mechanisms for violations.

## Bill Description

An Act to amend the South Carolina Code of Laws by enacting the "Forming Open and Robust University Minds (FORUM) Act" by adding Chapter 148 to Title 59 so as to:

- Define necessary terms
- Provide measures to protect expressions by students and student organizations in certain places on the campuses of public institutions of higher learning
- Provide related requirements for policies and procedures
- Provide specific responsibilities of public institutions of higher learning
- Provide means of redress for violations of this act
