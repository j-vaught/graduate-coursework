# SC HB 3184 – Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Restrictions at State Universities

## Bill Overview

- **Bill Number:** H 3184
- **State:** South Carolina
- **Session:** 126th (2025-2026)
- **Chamber:** House
- **Introduced:** January 14, 2025
- **Current Location:** House Committee on Education and Public Works

## Summary

This legislation proposes amendments restricting diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives at state universities, prohibiting consideration of political ideology in admissions and employment decisions. The bill seeks to amend South Carolina Code Section 59-101-680 to regulate DEI practices at public higher education institutions.

H. 3572

## Key Provisions

The bill would amend South Carolina Code Section 59-101-680 to:

### Prohibitions on DEI Considerations
- Prohibit institutions from using "political ideology or statements on diversity, equity, and inclusion" in making admissions or employment decisions
- Ban mandatory DEI training programs
- Prohibit adverse employment actions against those refusing to participate in DEI initiatives

### Reporting Requirements
- Require annual reporting to the Commission on Higher Education regarding:
  - Administrative positions dedicated to DEI programs
  - Total operating costs of DEI programs

### Institutional Obligations
- Mandate institutions distribute the bill's language to all students, employees, and faculty
- Require protection of First Amendment speech rights for students, faculty, and employees

### Exemptions
- Include exemptions for federal law compliance requirements
- Include exemptions for accreditation requirements
