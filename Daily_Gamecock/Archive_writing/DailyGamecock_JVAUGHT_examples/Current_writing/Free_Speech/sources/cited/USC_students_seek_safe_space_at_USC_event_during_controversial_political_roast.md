Columbia, S.C. (WACH) — The student organization sponsored Blatt Bonanza was an all out success Wednesday at the Blatt Recreational Field as nearly two thousand students came out looking for a good time. The event was filled with dancing, a rage room, inflatables free food and several different activities. It's uniting us all together and it's giving us good vibes and good energy," one student said.

The University of South Carolina Student Body President Patton Byars said the event brought awareness to the universities core values.“This is the event on our campus. It’s not an alternative or counter event; this is just what we do here," said Byars.

This event happened at the same time and just down the street from a controversial political roast targeting Vice President Kamala Harris that was put on by another student group. While Byars said the event was not a counter event or alternative solution for the political roast some attendees viewed the Blatt Bonanza as a more positive alternative to the political roast taking place at the Russell House. “If they can have their event, we can have ours, with a more unified message than whatever is being spewed over there," said one student Eric Center.

Another student who lived nearby noted the event gave her a safe space. "I wanted to get out of my room because I was close to Russell," said Caitlyn Bulford.

The University of South Carolina President Michael Amiridis also made an appearance at Wednesday's event and students were grateful for the free festivities. "It’s just like a balanced fun experience where all of us can be a part of the Carolina experience and be happy, said another student.

Event organizers said more than 1,500 students came out to the event.
