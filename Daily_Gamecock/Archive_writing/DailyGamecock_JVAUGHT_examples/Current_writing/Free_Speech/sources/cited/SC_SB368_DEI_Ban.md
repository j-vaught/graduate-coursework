# South Carolina Senate Bill 368
## Ending Illegal Discrimination and Restoring Merit-Based Opportunity Act (2025-2026)

## Bill Information

**Bill Number:** SB 368
**Session:** 2025-2026 (126th General Assembly)
**Status:** Introduced February 20, 2025

### Sponsors
Senators: Garrett, Kimbrell, Cash, Fernandez, Johnson, Adams, Corbin, Rice, Zell, Leber, Elliott, Blackmon

### Companion Legislation
House Bill 3927 (HB 3927) - introduced February 6, 2025

---

## Bill Summary

SC Senate Bill 368, titled the "Ending Illegal Discrimination and Restoring Merit-Based Opportunity Act," proposes comprehensive amendments to the South Carolina Code of Laws to prohibit diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) programs and activities across state government, political subdivisions, higher education institutions, and school districts.

---

## Main Provisions

### Primary Purpose
The bill seeks to:
1. Prohibit state offices, divisions, and departments from promoting diversity, equity, and inclusion
2. Eliminate DEI programs and activities at all state institutions and political subdivisions
3. Promote individual initiative, excellence, and hard work as the state's guiding principle
4. Direct enforcement of existing civil rights laws

### Key Requirements

**Section 1-1-1910 - Restrictions on DEI Activities:**
- Defines "promoting diversity, equity, and inclusion" as efforts to influence hiring or employment practices based on protected characteristics or conduct trainings/programs related to race, sex, color, ethnicity, gender, or sexual orientation

**Prohibited Activities:**
- Establishing DEI offices or divisions
- Hiring DEI personnel
- Requiring diversity statements from applicants or employees
- Providing preferential treatment based on protected categories
- Mandating DEI training participation

**Applicable Entities:**
- All state offices and departments
- All political subdivisions
- Institutions of higher learning
- School districts

### Permitted Exceptions
- Compliance with federal law requirements
- Academic instruction
- Scholarly research
- Student organizations
- Academic achievement initiatives

**Section 1-1-1920 - Contractor and Grant Certifications:**
Contractors and grant recipients must certify non-operation of prohibited DEI programs as a condition of receiving state funds or contracts

---

## Enforcement Provisions

### State Auditor Authority
- State Auditors are directed to determine if departments or offices have spent state money in violation of this law
- Departments found in violation have 180 days to cure the violation
- Failure to cure triggers escalation to State Fiscal Accountability Authority
- State Treasurer may withhold future distributions to non-compliant entities

### Attorney General Authority
- Authorization for Attorney General to take appropriate enforcement action
- Focus on combating illegal private-sector DEI preferences, mandates, policies, programs, and activities

### Private Rights of Action
- Private rights of action are limited
- Primary enforcement authority rests with state law enforcement and the Attorney General

---

## State Policy

The act establishes as state policy the promotion of:
- Individual initiative
- Excellence
- Hard work

The state is directed to enforce existing civil rights laws and combat what the bill characterizes as "illegal private-sector DEI preferences, mandates, policies, programs, and activities."

---

## Effective Date

The act becomes effective for fiscal and school year 2025-2026.

---

## Veteran Preferences and Constitutional Protections

The act preserves:
- Veteran preference laws
- First Amendment protections and activities
