# Arizona State Instructor Followed, Injured by Turning Point USA Crew

**Date:** October 14, 2023

**Author:** Ryan Quinn

---

## Summary

An Arizona State University writing instructor named David Boyles was confronted by operatives from the right-wing organization Turning Point USA on campus. The incident involved aggressive questioning, filming, and physical altercation that left Boyles injured.

## Key Details

**The Confrontation:**
Two Turning Point associates followed Boyles while he was leaving class. One individual posed inflammatory questions while recording video, making accusations about "pushing sodomy" and other inflammatory rhetoric. The encounter escalated when Boyles attempted to block the camera.

**Physical Altercation:**
Security footage from ASU showed the Turning Point crew member pushing Boyles face-first onto concrete. Boyles posted images showing blood on his face. Turning Point claimed Boyles "lunged" and "clawed" at their cameraman.

**Investigation:**
Campus police investigated the incident as possible aggravated assault including bias toward LGBTQ+ individuals.

**Background:**
Boyles, an ASU instructor since 2015 specializing in rhetoric and digital literacy, appears on Turning Point's Professor Watchlist—a database flagging academics for alleged ideological bias. His listing references his advocacy for Drag Queen Story Hour–Arizona.

**University Response:**
ASU President Michael Crow previously wrote to Turning Point founder Charlie Kirk, expressing concerns that the watchlist encourages harassment through direct contact information.
