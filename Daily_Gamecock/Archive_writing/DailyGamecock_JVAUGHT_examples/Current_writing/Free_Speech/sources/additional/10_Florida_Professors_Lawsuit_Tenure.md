# Florida Professors' Lawsuit Against Tenure Restrictions

## Case Overview

Three Florida professors filed a lawsuit in Leon County against the state Legislature's Republican supermajority, challenging restrictions on tenure protections enacted in 2023.

## Plaintiffs

1. **Sarah Hernandez** — New College of Florida
2. **Adriana Novoa** — University of South Florida
3. **Steven J. Willis** — University of Florida

## The Legal Challenge

The professors accused the state Legislature of depriving them of "substantive protections inherent in their current tenured position" through legislation directly targeting diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) programs.

## 2023 Florida Law Details

The legislation established:
- **Five-year reviews** for tenured professors
- **New authority for college presidents** to fire "defiant faculty"
- Direct challenge to traditional tenure protections built up over decades

## Impact on Academic Freedom

The law effectively shifted employment status from permanent tenure to what faculty characterize as "five-year contracts," giving administrators unprecedented authority to remove faculty based on subjective assessments of compliance with state-preferred policies.

## Pattern of Enforcement

The lawsuit emerged as part of a broader pattern across Florida universities where:
- Professors were terminated or forced out over DEI-related activities
- Post-tenure reviews disproportionately targeted certain faculty
- Self-censorship increased among remaining faculty

## Broader Context

This lawsuit represents one of several legal challenges to Florida's anti-DEI legislation and tenure restrictions, part of a nationwide trend of legal challenges to academic freedom restrictions.

## Significance

The case demonstrates:
1. Direct legislative attack on tenure protections
2. Use of litigation to challenge state academic freedom restrictions
3. Intersection of DEI initiatives with broader academic freedom concerns
4. Professors' determination to defend institutional protections through courts when administrative processes fail

## Outcome Status

As a recent filing, the case represents an ongoing legal challenge to Florida's tenure-restricting legislation.
