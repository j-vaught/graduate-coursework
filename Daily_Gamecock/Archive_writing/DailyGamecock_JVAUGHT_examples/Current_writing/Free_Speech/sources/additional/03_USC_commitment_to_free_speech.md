# USC's Free Speech Commitment

The University of South Carolina has reaffirmed its dedication to open discourse by adopting the Chicago Principles, which state that "it is not the proper role of the University to attempt to shield individuals from ideas and opinions they find unwelcome, disagreeable, or even deeply offensive."

## Key Actions

**Policy Reforms:**
- Event flyers no longer require campus organization sponsorship statements
- Updated free expression policies to strengthen protections

**Institutional Context:**
USC leadership initiated these changes after the university ranked poorly in a national survey measuring campus free expression. The board's adoption of the Chicago Principles underscores the institution's commitment to fostering constructive debate across its diverse student body.

## Recognized Boundaries

While promoting open inquiry, USC acknowledges limits to protected expression. Students cannot disrupt speakers, obstruct others' access to events, or make direct threats against campus community members. The university frames these guidelines as essential to maintaining a learning environment where encountering diverse viewpoints becomes an opportunity for intellectual growth.

## Board Adoption

The University of South Carolina's Board of Trustees unanimously adopted a resolution in June 2023 embracing the core principles articulated in the University of Chicago's "Report on the Freedom of Expression."
