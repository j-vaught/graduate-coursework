# Violent Protests at UC Berkeley: Milo Yiannopoulos Event Disrupted

## Event Overview
On February 2, 2017, approximately 1,500 protesters gathered at the University of California, Berkeley to oppose a scheduled speech by controversial Breitbart editor Milo Yiannopoulos. The demonstration escalated into violence, forcing police intervention and cancellation of the event.

## Key Details

### The Disruption
"Violence erupted at the University of California, Berkeley, Wednesday night, forcing police to clear crowds when some 1,500 people gathered to protest a scheduled speech" by the controversial figure.

### Scope of Violence
Demonstrators engaged in property destruction and arson. "Police appeared to use flash bangs and pepper spray to clear the surging crowd," and the entire campus was locked down with students sheltering in place as protesters moved downtown.

### About Yiannopoulos
He has been criticized for racist and misogynist views and was banned from Twitter. He's described as "the leading member of the alt-right movement" and denies involvement with white nationalist groups.

### The Response
Yiannopoulos released a video stating that even if accusations against him were true, "it still wouldn't be an appropriate response" to the violence. The incident occurred at the institution where the Free Speech Movement originated, drawing criticism from college Republicans and political figures.
