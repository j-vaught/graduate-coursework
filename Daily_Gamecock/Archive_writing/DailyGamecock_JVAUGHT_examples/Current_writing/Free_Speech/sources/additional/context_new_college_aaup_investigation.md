# AAUP Report: Political Interference in Florida Higher Education

## Overview

The American Association of University Professors issued a December 2023 report documenting what it characterizes as an unprecedented assault on academic freedom in Florida's public higher education system under Governor Ron DeSantis.

## New College Takeover

The report details a dramatic institutional transformation at New College of Florida following DeSantis's January 2023 appointment of six partisan trustees. Key developments include:

**Leadership Changes**: The board immediately terminated President Patricia Okker and appointed Richard Corcoran, a former education commissioner and Republican House speaker, as interim president.

**Tenure Denials**: In April 2023, the board denied tenure to five faculty members despite their applications being approved through normal channels. President Corcoran inserted a memo citing "extraordinary circumstances" and the college's "renewed focus on ensuring...a more traditional liberal arts institution"—essentially applying ideological rather than merit-based standards.

**Program Elimination**: The board voted to terminate the gender studies program in August 2023, prompting the sole tenure-track faculty member to resign.

**Non-Renewal Case**: Visiting historian Erik Wallenberg's contract was not renewed after he co-authored an op-ed criticizing the takeover. Trustee Christopher Rufo celebrated the decision on social media, declaring the college would cease being "a jobs program for middling left-wing intellectuals."

**Faculty Exodus**: Approximately 41 faculty members (40+ percent of the faculty) departed through resignation, retirement, or unpaid leave.

## Broader Systemic Concerns

**Governance Issues**: The report identifies patterns of political patronage in presidential searches, with Republican politicians and operatives receiving preferential treatment for university leadership positions.

**Legislative Restrictions**: Florida's Stop WOKE Act (SB 7) and related legislation restrict classroom discussion of race, sexuality, and gender identity, creating what faculty describe as a "Kafkaesque" atmosphere of fear and confusion.

**Accreditation Interference**: Governor DeSantis challenged federal accreditation authority through litigation, seeking to weaken this professional oversight mechanism.

**Administrative Complicity**: The report expresses particular disappointment that most university presidents remained silent about developments at New College rather than defending institutional autonomy and academic freedom.

## Key Findings

The committee concluded that Florida's public higher education system faces "a politically and ideologically driven assault unparalleled in US history," with potential consequences for higher education nationwide if such interference becomes a model for other conservative-led states.
