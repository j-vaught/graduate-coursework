# Milo Yiannopoulos Event Canceled After Violence Erupts

**Author:** Public Affairs
**Date:** February 1, 2017

## Overview

UC Berkeley canceled a scheduled speech by controversial Breitbart News editor Milo Yiannopoulos after violent disruptions occurred on campus. The event, organized by the Berkeley College Republicans, was halted around 6 p.m. due to safety concerns.

## Key Details

The cancellation followed "an apparently organized violent attack and destruction of property at UC Berkeley's Martin Luther King Jr. Student Union." Approximately 150 masked agitators infiltrated an otherwise peaceful protest of over 1,500 people gathered outside the venue.

The violent incidents included:
- Deliberately set fires, including one at a campus Amazon outlet
- Molotov cocktails that ignited generator-powered lights
- Commercial-grade fireworks hurled at police officers
- Damage to the Stiles Hall construction site
- Rocks thrown at officers

## University Response

Chancellor Nicholas Dirks emphasized that while opposing Yiannopoulos's views, "UC Berkeley is bound by the Constitution, the law and the university's values." Campus officials noted they "regret that the threats and unlawful actions of a few have interfered with the exercise of First Amendment rights."

UCPD reported minor injuries and no arrests by 9:30 p.m. Officers exercised restraint to protect innocent bystanders from crossfire.
