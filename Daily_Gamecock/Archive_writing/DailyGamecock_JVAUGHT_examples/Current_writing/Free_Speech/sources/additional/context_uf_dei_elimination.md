# University of Florida Eliminates DEI Office

The University of Florida has dismantled its diversity, equity, and inclusion program in compliance with a 2023 state law championed by Republican Governor Ron DeSantis.

## Key Actions

The university eliminated 13 staff positions and removed 15 faculty administrative appointments. Affected employees received 12 weeks of severance and had until April 19, 2024 to apply for other positions at the institution.

## Financial Reallocation

The institution redirected $5 million previously designated for DEI initiatives toward faculty recruitment efforts.

## Official Stance

University leadership stated they would maintain "our commitment to universal human dignity" despite the program's elimination. The memo emphasized the school's continued commitment to fostering "a community of trust and respect for every member of the Gator Nation."

## Broader Context

Florida's law prohibits state colleges and universities from using public funds for "programs that categorize individuals based on race or sex for the purpose of differential or preferential treatment."

Governor DeSantis declared on social media that "DEI is toxic and has no place in our public universities," encouraging other states to follow Florida's example. The measure represents part of a broader national trend, with approximately 50 similar anti-DEI bills advancing in Republican-controlled legislatures nationwide.

## National Pattern

This action at University of Florida exemplifies how conservative-controlled state governments have begun systematically dismantling diversity initiatives at public institutions, often framing such actions as compliance with state law and fiscal responsibility while critics argue it represents a coordinated effort to eliminate programs focused on addressing systemic inequities.
