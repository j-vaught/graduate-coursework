# Charlie Kirk visits USC, draws crowd of fans, protesters

**Author:** Hayden Davis
**Date:** April 9, 2025

## Overview

Conservative activist Charlie Kirk visited the University of South Carolina this week as part of his American Comeback Tour, drawing hundreds of students and residents to Greene Street for debates. Kirk, who has a 6.4 million-follower TikTok account, engaged in back-to-back one-on-one debates with students willing to challenge him at a microphone.

## Kirk's Background

Kirk leads Turning Point USA, an organization promoting conservative activism among high school and college students. USC has its own chapter, one of over 1,000 nationally.

## Supporters' Perspective

**Student Rachel Griffin** discovered Kirk through social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok. Another supporter, **Caroline Bailey**, attended hoping for "intense" debate discussions.

## Kirk's Strategy and Reach

According to a November 2024 podcast appearance, Kirk stated: "on TikTok alone, it was a billion views in 90 days." His debate videos consistently attract millions of views.

## Protesters' Concerns

Protester **Jest Dennis** criticized Kirk's editing practices, arguing that "they never post any videos where Charlie Kirk is put on the ropes." She characterized his platform as "dangerous" and expressed concerns about how his ideology could encourage harmful actions on campus.
