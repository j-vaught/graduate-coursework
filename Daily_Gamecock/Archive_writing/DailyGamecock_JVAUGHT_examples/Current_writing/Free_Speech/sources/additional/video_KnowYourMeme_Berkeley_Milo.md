# 2017 Milo Yiannopoulos UC Berkeley Protest

**Event Date:** February 2, 2017
**Know Your Meme Entry Last Updated:** May 9, 2025

## Overview

The event was a demonstration at the University of California, Berkeley protesting a scheduled talk by Breitbart editor Milo Yiannopoulos. The talk was supposed to address "cultural appropriation" as a topic.

## What Happened

The protest escalated into violence, prompting law enforcement to cancel the event entirely. After the protest turned violent, police canceled the event and evacuated Yiannopoulos off campus.

## Historical Context

This incident occurred during a period of heightened political tensions in early 2017 and became a notable flashpoint regarding free speech debates on college campuses and protest tactics.

The event subsequently led to broader discussions about the balance between protest rights and maintaining institutional order, and it remains a frequently cited reference point in ongoing debates about free speech and campus activism in American higher education.
