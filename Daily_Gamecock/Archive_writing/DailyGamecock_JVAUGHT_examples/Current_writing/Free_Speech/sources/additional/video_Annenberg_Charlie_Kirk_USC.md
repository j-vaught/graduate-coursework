# Hundreds attend, one arrested at Charlie Kirk's first appearance at USC

**Authors:** Sydnie McClain, Ryan Kassebaum, Benjamin Gamson
**Date:** March 3, 2025

## Overview

Conservative commentator Charlie Kirk visited USC on Monday as part of his "American Comeback Tour," drawing hundreds of students to Hahn Plaza near Tommy Trojan. The event featured a debate format where Kirk made controversial claims, including that "police brutality is largely a lie" and "white privilege doesn't exist."

## The Arrest

During the event, a former USC student was arrested after refusing to leave when DPS officers asked her to remove amplified sound equipment she was operating without a permit. According to DPS Assistant Chief David Carlisle, the individual "severely twisted an officer's hand" during removal, resulting in charges of assaulting a school official.

## Kirk's Approach to Dialogue

Kirk, founder of Turning Point USA, framed the event as an opportunity for students to challenge his positions. However, some attendees criticized his approach. Student Safiya Nicol stated that Kirk's "prove me wrong" concept aims to "trigger people" rather than foster genuine dialogue.

## Debate Topics

The event included heated exchanges on topics including affirmative action and diversity initiatives. Kirk argued that "only merit should matter" in college admissions. The event concluded at 2:30 p.m., with Kirk's next tour stop scheduled for California State University-Northridge.
