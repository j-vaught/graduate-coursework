# Ohio State Professors on Turning Point USA's Watchlist

## The Listing

Four Ohio State professors appear on Turning Point USA's "Professor Watchlist": Hasan Jeffries, Treva Lindsay, Pranav Jani, and Scott Leibowitz. None of their profiles have been updated since 2023. The watchlist was designed to "expose and document college professors who discriminate against conservative students and advance leftist propaganda in the classroom."

## Pranav Jani's Experience

Jani, an associate professor of English, became aware of his listing around 2016. He has been politically active as former president of Ohio State's AAUP chapter and advisor to Students for Justice in Palestine.

Over his 22-year tenure at Ohio State, Jani has faced harassment, violent messages, and threats targeting himself and his family—though he cannot definitively attribute specific incidents to the watchlist versus other sources like Canary Mission.

Jani characterizes such lists as tools designed to "turn us into examples to make everyone else silent."

## Academic Freedom Concerns

Jani argues these directories contradict free speech principles. He stated: "sometimes it's an attack on what we say in our capacity as individual citizens." Jani's Canary Mission listing—which falsely labels him as a "terror supporter"—ranks highly in Google search results, damaging his professional reputation despite being inaccurate.

## Broader Context

The article notes that professors on similar watchlists have experienced serious consequences beyond Ohio State. Arizona State's David Boyles was assaulted by Turning Point employees in 2023, while Rutgers' Mark Bray temporarily relocated to Europe after escalating threats amplified by a student petition.

## Institutional Chilling Effect

The watchlists contribute to broader pressures on academic instruction. With Ohio Senate Bill 1 reshaping higher education policy, professors face uncertainty about classroom speech and potential political targeting. These databases create an environment where faculty anxiety about public criticism and external surveillance fundamentally alters how professors approach their work.

## Advocacy for Institutional Defense

Jani advocates for universities to collectively defend academic freedom and free speech principles without allowing them to become politically weaponized. He emphasizes the importance of institutional support for faculty facing public campaigns.
