# PEN America Book Ban Index 2024-2025

## Overall Statistics

PEN America found that **6,870 book bans** were enacted during the 2024-25 school year, across:
- 23 states
- 87 public school districts

PEN America recorded 6,870 instances of book bans affecting **nearly 4,000 unique titles**.

## Top States for Book Bans

| State | Number of Bans |
|-------|----------------|
| Florida | 2,304 |
| Texas | 1,781 |
| Tennessee | 1,622 |

These three states account for approximately 70% of all documented school book bans.

## State-Mandated Bans: A New Phenomenon

A particularly concerning trend is the emergence of state-level "no read" lists. This Index captures an unprecedented phenomenon in the book banning crisis — the removal of specific books from **all public schools statewide** due to state policies.

### Utah's Trigger Ban (HB 29, 2024)

Utah's law functions as a "trigger ban," where a book is **automatically banned statewide** if a certain number of school districts or charter schools remove it from their shelves.

**Results:**
- Nineteen different titles have been banned from public schools across the state
- These state-level bans occurred after only a few schools removed them locally

### South Carolina State-Level Bans

South Carolina also implemented state-mandated book removal policies, representing another example of statewide rather than purely local censorship.

## Normalization of Book Banning

PEN America reports that book banning has become normalized in American schools, with routine removals now part of standard practice in many districts.

## EveryLibrary's Role

Much of organized resistance has been supported and amplified through **FightForTheFirst.org**, a free platform created by EveryLibrary to help people launch and grow local campaigns against censorship.

## Significance

The 2024-25 data demonstrates:
1. Continuation and escalation of book banning from 2023-24
2. Geographic concentration in conservative-led states
3. Shift from local to state-level censorship mechanisms
4. Unprecedented use of algorithmic/trigger bans
5. Growing normalization of book removal as standard practice

## Related Concerns

These book bans disproportionately target:
- LGBTQ+ themed books
- Books addressing race and racism
- Books featuring marginalized communities
- Books addressing social justice themes
- Books by authors of color

## Implications for Free Speech

This data demonstrates that book banning extends the academic freedom and free speech crisis beyond higher education into K-12 public education, affecting access to information and ideas for millions of young people.
