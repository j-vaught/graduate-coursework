# UC Berkeley Protest Photo Gallery: Milo Yiannopoulos and Free Speech Week

## Event Details
The gallery documents protests at UC Berkeley on September 24-25, 2017, centered on right-wing speaker Milo Yiannopoulos and a planned "Free Speech Week."

## Key Information

### Protest Scale and Response
"Several hundred demonstrators rallied and marched through the University of California at Berkeley campus" to oppose the event. The protests involved both supporters and counterprotesters clashing on campus.

### Symbolic Location
Yiannopoulos made a brief appearance at Sproul Plaza, described as "the birthplace of the free speech movement of the 1960s," after the scheduled Free Speech Week was canceled due to organizers missing venue deadlines and scheduled speakers canceling their appearances.

### Security & Arrests
Security was substantial, with "only about 100 people allowed into the plaza." According to the captions, "Ten other people were arrested that day, including several for taking banned items into the plaza area."

### Campus Costs
UC Berkeley had incurred significant security costs related to these events—approximately $1.4 million since February 2017 for various controversial speaker appearances.

## Documentation
The gallery includes 16 photographs documenting the demonstration and police presence at the event.
