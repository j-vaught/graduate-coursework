# Impact of Turning Point USA's Professor Watchlist on Ohio State Professors

Turning Point USA's "professor watchlist" has created significant harm for targeted Ohio State educators. The database, launched in 2016 to identify professors deemed to promote "leftist propaganda," lists four OSU faculty members: Hasan Jeffries, Treva Lindsay, Pranav Jani, and Scott Leibowitz.

## Harassment and Safety Concerns

Professor Treva B. Lindsey reported receiving a surge in hostile messages after her listing on the watchlist in 2021. She characterized the communications as containing "racist language and threats of sexual violence," forcing her to remove her work email from the university website. Lindsey implemented community-based safety protocols and noted that "the threats feel more immediate" following recent events.

Associate Professor Pranav Jani has faced sustained harassment over his 22-year tenure, including violent messages and threats targeting himself and his family.

## Impact on Academic Freedom

Associate Professor Pranav Jani argues these directories contradict free speech principles. He stated: "sometimes it's an attack on what we say in our capacity as individual citizens." Jani's Canary Mission listing—which falsely labels him as a "terror supporter"—ranks highly in Google search results, damaging his professional reputation despite being inaccurate.

## Institutional Chilling Effect

The watchlists contribute to broader pressures on academic instruction. With Ohio Senate Bill 1 reshaping higher education policy, professors face uncertainty about classroom speech. Lindsey noted increased anxiety: "there's this sense of am I being recorded? Are people signing up for classes simply to antagonize?"

## Broader Pattern

Arizona State's David Boyles was assaulted by Turning Point employees in 2023, while Rutgers' Mark Bray temporarily relocated to Europe after escalating threats amplified by a student petition. These incidents demonstrate the real-world consequences of being listed on such databases.

## Mutual Support Networks

Despite institutional limitations, targeted professors have developed informal mutual aid networks to provide solidarity and practical guidance to newly listed colleagues. Jani advocates for universities to collectively defend academic freedom and free speech principles without allowing them to become politically weaponized.
