# Bicameral Resolution Condemning Book Bans

## Summary

Congressman Jamie Raskin (MD-08) and Senator Brian Schatz (D-HI) introduced a resolution opposing escalating censorship in schools and libraries across America during Banned Books Week.

## The Crisis

According to PEN America, book bans nearly tripled between school years 2022-2023 and 2023-2024, with over 10,000 instances documented in the latest year alone. This represents an alarming acceleration in attempts to censor educational materials in American schools and libraries.

## Targeted Books and Patterns

Frequently banned titles include:
- *To Kill a Mockingbird*
- *1984*
- *The Handmaid's Tale*

Books are disproportionately removed when they feature:
- LGBTQIA+ content
- Racial justice themes
- Discussions of trauma and abuse

## Congressional Support

The resolution garnered:
- 43 House cosponsors
- Endorsement from major organizations including:
  - American Library Association
  - PEN America
  - National Education Association
  - National LGBTQ Task Force Action Fund

## Key Quote

Representative Raskin stated: "We must close this chapter of censorship and turn our attention to resources students need to succeed."

## Related Initiative

Rep. Raskin launched a #ResolveToRead social media challenge encouraging people to share passages from banned books, bringing public attention to the breadth and scope of book removal efforts across the nation.

## Significance

This resolution represents a broader pushback against book banning efforts that have intensified in recent years. The bipartisan effort to document and condemn these practices underscores the threat posed by censorship to intellectual freedom and educational quality in American schools.
