# 2025 College Free Speech Rankings: Key Findings and Methodology

## Top Findings

**Best and Worst Schools:**
The University of Virginia ranks first with a score of 73.41, followed by Michigan Technological University (73.15) and Florida State University (72.46). Harvard University ranks last for the second consecutive year with a score of -21.58, joined in the bottom tier by Columbia University and New York University, all designated "Abysmal."

**Major Concerns:**
- A record 55% of students identified the Israeli-Palestinian conflict as "difficult to have an open and honest conversation about" on campus
- Support for disruptive protest tactics increased: 68% of students now find shouting down speakers at least "rarely" acceptable, up from 63% in 2023
- 32% of students view violence against speakers as at least "rarely" acceptable

**Administrative Impact:**
Bottom-ranked schools performed poorly on administrative support metrics. Harvard ranks dead last (250th) on "Administrative Support," while top schools demonstrate consistent commitment to protecting speech through policy and practice.

## Methodology

The survey included **58,807 undergraduates** from 257 institutions, conducted January-June 2024. College Pulse administered the survey using its American College Student Panel of 850,000+ verified students.

**Scoring Components (14 total):**
- Seven perception-based measures: comfort expressing ideas, self-censorship frequency, speaker tolerance, disruptive conduct acceptance, administrative support, and topic openness
- Seven behavioral metrics using FIRE's databases tracking deplatformings, scholar sanctions, and student sanctions

**Data Weighting:**
Post-stratification adjustments aligned samples with Census Bureau, NPSAS, and IPEDS demographic distributions across race, gender, class year, and financial aid status.
