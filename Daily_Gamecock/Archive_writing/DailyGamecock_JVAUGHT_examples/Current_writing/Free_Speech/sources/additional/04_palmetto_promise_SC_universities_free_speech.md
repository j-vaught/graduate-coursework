# South Carolina Universities' Free Speech Protection Analysis

The Palmetto Promise Institute examined free speech policies at South Carolina's public universities using standards from the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE).

## Key Findings

**No institution received failing grades.** The report states: "Palmetto Promise Institute is pleased to announce that no university in the state scored a 1 average," indicating all schools met minimum free speech protections.

However, **consistency remains lacking.** Four universities achieved perfect 3.0 scores (Clemson, Coastal Carolina, College of Charleston, and USC Columbia), while others ranged from 2.33 to 2.88, suggesting variability in policy quality across the system.

## Critical Insight

The report emphasizes that "the proof is in the pudding—or in specific policies, rather." While many universities have adopted the Chicago Statement on free expression, this aspirational declaration alone is insufficient. The analysis reveals a critical gap: **adoption of high-level commitments hasn't uniformly translated into strong, protective policies across all institutions.**

## Implications for USC

USC Columbia received one of four perfect scores (3.0), indicating strong free speech protections in institutional policy. However, the broader findings suggest that policy quality varies significantly across South Carolina institutions.

## Recommendations

Lawmakers should reintroduce legislation similar to the FORUM Act (H.3467) to establish baseline protections. However, the report advocates for voluntary institutional action, suggesting universities adopt comprehensive free speech policies without legislative mandate.
