# Pro-Palestine Protest at University of South Carolina (April 2024)

## Event Overview

FITSNews reporter Dylan Nolan attended a pro-Palestine demonstration at USC on April 24, 2024, where students gathered between the Russell House student union and the library fountain.

## Key Activities

The event featured a Jewish Passover Seder ceremony led by mathematics professor Joshua Cooper, who explained the ritual's significance to liberation movements. After the ceremony, student organizers announced plans to "occupy" the Russell House the following Wednesday.

## Participant Composition

One organizer revealed she was not a USC student. The group included members from Jewish Voice for Peace (JVP), and student activists wore "party for socialism and liberation" apparel.

## Counter-Perspective

Dr. Josef Olmert, former Israeli Director of Communications, argued that JVP represents only marginal Palestinian sentiment among Jewish Americans. He claimed "the vast majority of Jewish students belong to GFI- Gamecocks for ISRAEL" and suggested some participants were there to observe the event.

## Scale

Compared to larger protests erupting simultaneously at major universities nationwide, this demonstration remained relatively small, suggesting limited campus support for divestment initiatives.

## Arrests Context

This protest occurred in the broader context of campus-wide demonstrations in April 2024, during which two USC students were arrested and charged with breaching the peace after demonstrations inside the Russell House student union.

## Significance

This case represents student activism around free speech and campus expression issues, demonstrating both the diversity of viewpoints on campus and the tensions surrounding pro-Palestinian expression.
