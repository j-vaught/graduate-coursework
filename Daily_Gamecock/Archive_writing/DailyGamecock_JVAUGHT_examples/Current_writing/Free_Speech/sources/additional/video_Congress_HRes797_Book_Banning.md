# House Resolution 797: Book Banning Concerns

## Overview

H. Res. 797, introduced October 8, 2025, expresses concern about increasing book bans and threats to freedom of expression in the United States. The resolution was introduced by Mr. Raskin and has 48 co-sponsors, primarily Democratic representatives.

## Key Statistics

According to the resolution, PEN America documented significant book banning activity:

- **6,870 instances** of individual books banned between July 2024 and June 2025
- **3,751 unique titles** censored during this period
- **2,589 authors, illustrators, and translators** whose works were affected
- **23 states** limited access to certain books during this timeframe

## Most Affected States

The states with the highest number of banned or restricted books are:

1. **Florida**: At least 2,304 banned or restricted books across 33 school districts
2. **Texas**: At least 1,781 books
3. **Tennessee**: At least 1,622 books

## Targeted Content

The resolution documents disproportionate targeting of specific categories:

- Books featuring LGBTQ+ themes
- Works addressing race and racism
- Classic literature, including works by Harper Lee and Margaret Atwood
- Approximately 596 books related to diversity and inclusion at Department of Defense Education Activity schools
- Nearly 400 books removed from military academy libraries

## Federal Actions Documented

The resolution cites executive orders directing:

- Department of Defense Education Activity schools to remove approximately 596 books related to diversity and inclusion
- Military academies removing nearly 400 books from libraries

## Resolution Calls For

The resolution advocates for the following actions:

- Adherence to established book challenge guidelines
- Return of removed Department of Defense school books
- Repeal of executive orders restricting reading materials in federal educational institutions

## Committee Referrals

The resolution was referred to:

- Committee on Education and Workforce
- Committee on the Judiciary
- Committee on Armed Services

## Significance

H. Res. 797 represents a significant congressional expression of concern regarding First Amendment issues and freedom of expression in educational settings across the United States. It documents the scale of book banning activity and its disproportionate impact on specific communities and topics.
