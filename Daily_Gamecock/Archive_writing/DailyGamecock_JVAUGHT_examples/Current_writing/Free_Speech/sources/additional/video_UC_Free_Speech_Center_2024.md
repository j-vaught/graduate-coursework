# #SpeechMatters 2024: Politics, Polarization and Perils on Campus

## Overview

The UC National Center for Free Speech and Civic Engagement hosted its sixth annual #SpeechMatters conference on April 18, 2024. The virtual event brought together legal experts, journalists, and college and university leaders to explore current challenges and opportunities for learning, activism, and engagement during a transformative moment in higher education.

As the Center noted in its 2024 Annual Report: "As campuses sought to weather a free speech storm in 2024, the UC National Center for Free Speech and Civic Engagement remained a steady force for education and guidance—pivoting to meet the moment with robust programming and resources."

## Conference Theme: Politics, Polarization and Perils on Campus

The 2024 conference addressed three interconnected challenges facing higher education institutions:

### 1. Title VI and Campus Climate

The federal Department of Education's Office for Civil Rights issued updated guidance on Title VI and opened multiple investigations of colleges and universities under the statute. Institutions face complex questions about how to respond to allegations of a hostile educational environment while safeguarding freedom of expression. The conference examined:

- Federal civil rights guidance and institutional compliance
- Balancing inclusion with free expression protections
- Managing Title VI investigations and institutional responses

### 2. Political Polarization and Culture Wars

Higher education has become a battleground in broader culture wars, with legislatures across the country targeting:

- Diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) programs
- Tenure protections and job security for faculty
- Shared governance structures on campuses
- Curriculum content and academic freedom

The conference explored how these external pressures affect campus speech, expression, and academic inquiry.

### 3. Elections as Catalysts for Campus Engagement

As the 2024 presidential election approached, panelists discussed how electoral cycles can serve as springboards for:

- Student activism and political engagement
- Democratic participation and civic learning
- Classroom-based discussions and critical thinking
- Community dialogue and debate

## Conference Sessions

The #SpeechMatters 2024 agenda included four main discussion panels:

### Session 1: Title VI and Campus Climate
Examined federal civil rights guidance and the practical challenges institutions face in balancing inclusion with free expression protections.

### Session 2: Debate to Dialogue
Featured a conversation between scholars about frameworks for productive discourse and moving beyond polarized debate toward meaningful dialogue.

### Session 3: Targeted Harassment
Explored the distinction between protected speech and unprotected harassment of university employees, addressing one of the most pressing legal gray areas on campuses.

### Session 4: Student Engagement
Discussed how elections can promote democratic participation on campus and serve as opportunities for learning and civic engagement.

## Context: The 2024 Campus Free Speech Environment

The conference took place against a backdrop of significant challenges to campus free speech and expression:

- Increased legislative scrutiny of higher education
- Deepening political polarization affecting campus communities
- Growing number of federal civil rights investigations
- Tensions between inclusion initiatives and free expression protections
- Student activism around election-related issues

As noted by Campbell Kersteen of the University of California: "With campus expression issues splashed across the front page," institutions face complex challenges regarding polarization and political pressure while maintaining their educational mission.

## Significance for Higher Education

The #SpeechMatters 2024 conference reflects broader trends and challenges in higher education:

1. **Polarization in Academia**: Universities are microcosms of larger societal divisions, requiring intentional strategies for productive dialogue

2. **Regulatory Environment**: Federal oversight through Title VI investigations creates compliance challenges while raising free speech concerns

3. **Mission Alignment**: Institutions must balance multiple commitments to inclusion, academic freedom, and free expression

4. **Student Formation**: Elections and political engagement offer opportunities to develop students' civic capacities and critical thinking

5. **Leadership Needs**: Administrators, faculty, and staff require training and resources to navigate complex speech issues

## Conclusion

The #SpeechMatters 2024 conference demonstrated the UC National Center's commitment to helping higher education institutions navigate one of the most challenging aspects of contemporary campus life: maintaining robust free speech protections while fostering inclusive, productive communities. By bringing together diverse experts and leaders, the conference modeled the kind of informed, nuanced dialogue necessary to address the "politics, polarization and perils on campus" that increasingly define the higher education landscape.

As campuses continue to grapple with federal investigations, legislative pressure, and internal polarization, the work of institutions like the UC National Center becomes increasingly vital to preserving free speech, academic freedom, and the educational mission of universities.
