This directory contains 13 markdown files with YAML front-matter documenting broader university context on free speech controversies, conservative censorship, and attacks on academic freedom. Each file includes the source URL, title, and access date (2026-01-30).

## Key Themes

These sources document:

1. **Conservative institutional takeovers** of universities to implement ideological curriculum changes
2. **State-level legislation** directly restricting faculty speech on topics like race and gender
3. **Coordinated targeting** of progressive professors through watchlists and databases
4. **DEI program eliminations** across major universities under political pressure
5. **Contradictory narratives** about campus free speech—conservatives claim persecution while attacking progressive DEI initiatives; progressives defend inclusion while opposing conservative speaker platforms
6. **Federal pressure** through executive orders and funding threats forcing universities to eliminate diversity programs
7. **Academic freedom erosion** through both legislative mandates and institutional capitulation to political pressure

## Central Argument for Your Article

These sources provide crucial context showing that conservative claims about campus free speech persecution are hypocritical when conservative-controlled governments and organizations are simultaneously:
- Legislatively restricting faculty classroom speech on prescribed topics
- Orchestrating institutional takeovers to control curricula and academic programs
- Using publicly funded watchlists to target and harass progressive professors
- Funding campaigns to eliminate academic programs in diversity and ethnic studies
- Creating federal pressure through executive orders to dismantle diversity initiatives
- Imposing state laws with vague language that chill academic freedom through self-censorship

Meanwhile, the same parties claim to champion "free speech" as a principle while actively using government power to censor progressive speech and perspectives in educational settings.
