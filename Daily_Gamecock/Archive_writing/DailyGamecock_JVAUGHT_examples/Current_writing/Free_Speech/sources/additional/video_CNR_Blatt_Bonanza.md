# Blatt Bonanza showcases student unity in the face of Russell House 'roast'

**Author:** Jayden Simmons
**Date:** September 20, 2024

## Event Overview

Over 1,000 University of South Carolina students attended the "Blatt Bonanza" event Wednesday night, where more than 20 organizations provided entertainment and activities including bounce houses, video games, food, and live music.

## Context

The event served as a counterpoint to a controversial speaking engagement at Russell House featuring far-right personalities Milo Yiannapolous and Gavin McInnes, invited by the student group Uncensored America.

## Student Response

**Sophomore Skye Mabrey** stated: "I felt like it was my duty as a South Carolinian and as a student at USC to show that I don't support what (the speakers at the Russell House) are coming here to do."

## Event Philosophy

**Student Body President Patton Byars** emphasized the Blatt event aimed to serve students rather than directly respond to the speakers. **RHA National Communications Coordinator Mark Mekhaeil** noted: "All these diverse and different types of student organizations came together to create a really big and really inclusive event for all students on campus."

## Music as Protest

**Ears Wide Open's artistic director Alyssa Santivanez** explained that "Jazz is originally America's protest music," positioning the live band as both entertainment and subtle commentary.

## University Leadership

President Michael Amiridis called the event "wonderful," highlighting its role in campus community building.
