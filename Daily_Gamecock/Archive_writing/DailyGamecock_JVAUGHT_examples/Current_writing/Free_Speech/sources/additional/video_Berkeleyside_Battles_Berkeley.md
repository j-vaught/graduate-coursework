# One day, one night: The fuse that lit the Battles of Berkeley

**Author:** Frances Dinkelspiel
**Published:** January 31, 2018
**Last Updated:** September 27, 2022
**Word Count:** 12,064 words
**Categories:** City, Crime, Schools, Video

## Overview

This is a comprehensive oral history by Berkeleyside Executive Editor Frances Dinkelspiel examining a pivotal moment in Berkeley's recent history when demonstrations escalated into significant civil unrest surrounding a controversial speaking engagement at UC Berkeley.

## Methodology

The piece is an oral history that tells the story through the voices of those who were there. The narrative interweaves:
- Interviews with key participants and observers
- Social media posts (tweets, Facebook posts)
- Court statements
- Nixle alerts and police reports
- Official memos
- Op-eds
- First-hand observations

This multimedia approach creates a vivid narrative that reveals the deep political divisions prevalent in the United States during this period.

## Subject Matter

The article focuses on the events surrounding Milo Yiannopoulos' controversial February 2017 visit to UC Berkeley and the subsequent confrontations between protesters and law enforcement, examining:
- The political tensions leading to the event
- The protest itself and escalation to violence
- Law enforcement response and policing decisions
- The broader political context of the time

## Recognition

The Northern California chapter of the Society of Professional Journalists honored Berkeleyside with the 2018 Longform Storytelling award (in the print/online small division) for this article, recognizing its excellence in narrative journalism and comprehensive coverage of a complex political moment.
