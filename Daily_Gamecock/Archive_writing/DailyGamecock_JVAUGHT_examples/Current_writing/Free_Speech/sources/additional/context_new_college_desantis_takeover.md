# Ron DeSantis's Takeover of New College

## Summary

Florida Governor Ron DeSantis has orchestrated a dramatic overhaul of New College of Florida, a small liberal arts institution with approximately 700 students in Sarasota. DeSantis appointed a conservative board of trustees who swiftly removed the college president and pledged sweeping reforms, reportedly advancing his political agenda while building credentials for a potential presidential campaign.

## Student Impact

The transformation has deeply unsettled the campus community. One fourth-year student expressed the disorienting nature of the changes, stating: "This last month has felt a little dystopian. These people like DeSantis...clearly don't know what New College is, but they're trying to control what we are learning and who we are."

## Academic Freedom Concerns

Students perceive DeSantis's intervention as part of a broader conservative initiative targeting educational curricula and institutional autonomy. The rapid removal of leadership and promised institutional restructuring raise questions about whether academic institutions can maintain independence when subjected to externally-imposed political directives.

The episode reflects escalating tensions between state political actors and higher education independence, particularly regarding curriculum determination and institutional governance.

## Institutional Model

Trustee Christopher Rufo explicitly framed New College as a blueprint for conservative takeover of higher education nationwide: "If we can take this high-risk, high-reward gambit and turn it into a victory, we're going to see conservative state legislators starting to reconquer public institutions all over the United States."
