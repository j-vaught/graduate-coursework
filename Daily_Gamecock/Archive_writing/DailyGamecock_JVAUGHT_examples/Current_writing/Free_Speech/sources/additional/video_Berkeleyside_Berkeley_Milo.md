# Dramatic Photos: Protests Erupt Over Milo Yiannopoulos Talk at UC Berkeley

## Article Details

**Author:** Lance Knobel

**Publication Date:** February 2, 2017

**Categories:** City, Public Safety, Schools

## Featured Content

The article emphasizes visual documentation of the dramatic protests. One notable image caption mentions "Sproul Hall lit with LGBT colors prior to Milo demonstrations."

The piece captures the intensity and scale of the protest response to controversial speaker Milo Yiannopoulos's scheduled appearance on the UC Berkeley campus on February 1-2, 2017.

## Context

This article from Berkeleyside, a local Berkeley news outlet, documents the February 2017 incident when protests against Yiannopoulos's scheduled speech escalated into violence, leading to property damage across campus and downtown Berkeley. The event became a flashpoint in broader national debates about free speech, political expression, and campus activism.
