# H.Res. 927 - Condemning Antisemitism on University Campuses

## Resolution Overview

H.Res. 927 strongly condemns the rise of antisemitism on university campuses across the United States and addresses testimony presented by university presidents to the House Committee on Education and the Workforce on December 5, 2023.

## Sponsorship and Legislative History

- **Primary Sponsor**: Rep. Elise Stefanik
- **Introduction Date**: December 12, 2023
- **Passage Date**: December 13, 2023
- **Vote**: 303-126 with 3 members present (Roll no. 721)
- **Procedure**: Passed on a motion to suspend the rules

## Context and Motivation

The resolution was introduced following Congressional hearings addressing the significant rise in antisemitic incidents on college campuses, particularly in the months following October 7, 2023, when Hamas attacked Israel.

### Documented Antisemitic Incidents

According to the Anti-Defamation League:
- **Total incidents**: 2,031 antisemitic incidents recorded in the months following October 7, 2023
- **Campus incidents**: 400 incidents occurred on college campuses
- **Increase rate**: More than 330-percent increase from the year prior

## University Presidents' Testimony

The resolution specifically addresses testimony from university presidents before the House Committee on Education and the Workforce. These hearings generated significant controversy regarding institutional responses to campus expressions of pro-Palestinian sentiment and concerns raised by Jewish students and faculty.

## Legislative Significance

This resolution represents Congressional action to document and condemn antisemitism as a significant threat on American university campuses. The broad voting margin (303-126) indicates substantial bipartisan concern about antisemitic harassment and discrimination affecting Jewish students and faculty.

## Broader Context

H.Res. 927 is part of a larger Congressional response to campus tensions following October 7, 2023, which included multiple hearings, resolutions, and legislative proposals addressing both free speech protections and specific concerns about antisemitism and other forms of campus discrimination.

## Related Actions

The resolution connects to broader legislative efforts addressing campus protest activities, institutional accountability, and protection of student and faculty rights in the wake of heightened campus activism related to international conflicts.
