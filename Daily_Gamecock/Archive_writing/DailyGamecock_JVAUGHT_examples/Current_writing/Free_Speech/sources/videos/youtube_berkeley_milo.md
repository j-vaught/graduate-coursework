## Event Context

**Date of Primary Incident:** February 1, 2017

**Location:** University of California, Berkeley (UC Berkeley MLK Jr. Student Union)

**Key Facts:**
- Over 1,500 people initially gathered peacefully to protest Yiannopoulos's scheduled speech on cultural appropriation
- Approximately 150 masked agitators, claiming to be affiliated with Antifa and "By Any Means Necessary," disrupted the peaceful protest
- Property damage totaled approximately $100,000
- Violence included: Molotov cocktails, commercial-grade fireworks, smashed windows, property destruction, and assaults
- The event was cancelled approximately 2 hours before the scheduled 8 p.m. start time
- Yiannopoulos was evacuated from campus by UC Police Department

**Secondary Event:** September 2017 "Free Speech Week"
- Planned as week-long event with multiple conservative speakers including Yiannopoulos, Steve Bannon, Ann Coulter, and others
- Officially cancelled on September 23, 2017
- Yiannopoulos briefly appeared on September 24, 2017 at Sproul Plaza without sound system or formal event structure
