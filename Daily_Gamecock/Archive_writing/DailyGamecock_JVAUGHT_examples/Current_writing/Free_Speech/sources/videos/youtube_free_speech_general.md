# YouTube Videos: Free Speech, Book Banning, and Academic Freedom

## Documentary Series

### 1. Silence U: Is the University Killing Free Speech and Open Debate?

- **Full YouTube URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Silence-U-1 (available via We the Internet TV channel)
- **Channel Name**: We the Internet TV
- **Video Title**: Silence U: Is the University Killing Free Speech and Open Debate?
- **Description**: Original viral hit from 2016 that examines whether universities are suppressing free speech and open debate on campuses. Directed by Rob Montz, this short documentary explores cases where controversial issues cannot be openly discussed in university settings.

### 2. Silence U Part 2: What Is Yale Becoming?

- **Full YouTube URL**: https://www.youtube.com/c/WetheInternetTV (video available on We the Internet TV channel)
- **Channel Name**: We the Internet TV
- **Video Title**: Silence U Part 2: What Is Yale Becoming?
- **Description**: Follow-up documentary examining Yale University's policies on free speech and administrative controls, particularly focusing on the Halloween costume controversy that sparked debates about institutional overreach in regulating student expression and administrative guidelines.

### 3. Silence U Part 3: Can the University of Chicago Solve the Campus Free Speech Crisis?

- **Full YouTube URL**: https://www.youtube.com/c/WetheInternetTV (video available on We the Internet TV channel)
- **Channel Name**: We the Internet TV
- **Video Title**: Silence U Part 3: Can the University of Chicago Solve the Campus Free Speech Crisis?
- **Description**: Third installment in the Silence U series examining whether the University of Chicago's commitment to free speech principles can serve as a model for resolving the broader campus free speech crisis affecting American higher education.

### 4. No Safe Spaces Documentary

- **Full YouTube URL**: https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=no+safe+spaces+documentary
- **Channel Name**: Available on Prime Video and multiple streaming platforms (often excerpted on YouTube)
- **Video Title**: No Safe Spaces
- **Description**: 2019 documentary directed by Justin Folk featuring commentator Dennis Prager and comedian Adam Carolla traveling across college campuses to discuss free speech controversies, safe spaces, and identity politics. The film examines cases like Evergreen State College's treatment of Professor Bret Weinstein and explores how identity politics and suppression of free speech are spreading into American society.

### 5. The Librarians (2025 Documentary Clips)

- **Full YouTube URL**: https://www.pbs.org/video/the-librarians-1759695945/
- **Channel Name**: PBS / Independent Lens
- **Video Title**: The Librarians - Public School Librarians Fight Book Bans
- **Description**: Award-winning 2025 documentary directed by Kim A. Snyder examining librarians on the front lines of the book banning crisis. Follows librarians in Texas, Florida, and other states as they unite to combat restrictions on library content, featuring interviews and stories of resistance to censorship in public schools.

### 6. Bodies Upon the Gears (FIRE Documentary)

- **Full YouTube URL**: https://www.thefire.org/bodies-upon-the-gears (also available on FIRE's YouTube channel)
- **Channel Name**: FIRE (Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression)
- **Video Title**: Bodies Upon the Gears
- **Description**: Short documentary (2024) presented by FIRE and directed by Oscar-winning filmmaker Paul Wagner, narrated by FIRE Senior Fellow Nadine Strossen. Explores the dramatic birth of the 1960s Free Speech Movement at UC Berkeley, documenting the turning point that established college students' First Amendment rights on campuses.

### 7. Free To Speak - PBS Documentary Series

- **Full YouTube URL**: https://www.pbs.org/show/free-to-speak/ (available on PBS YouTube channel)
- **Channel Name**: PBS / Free To Choose Network
- **Video Title**: Free To Speak (3-part series)
- **Description**: Three-part public television documentary series hosted by Nadine Strossen, former ACLU President and FIRE Senior Fellow. The documentary presents thought-provoking, ironic, and often heartbreaking stories about why free speech is necessary for human survival and the current controversies threatening free expression in America.
