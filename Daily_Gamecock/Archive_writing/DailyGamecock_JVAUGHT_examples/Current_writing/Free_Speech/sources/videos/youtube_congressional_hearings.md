## Overview

This document compiles YouTube video sources for major congressional hearings related to campus antisemitism and free speech, particularly the December 5, 2023 hearing before the House Committee on Education and the Workforce featuring Harvard President Claudine Gay, University of Pennsylvania President Liz Magill, and MIT President Sally Kornbluth. This hearing became the most-viewed congressional testimony in history with over 1 billion views.

## December 2023 Congressional Hearing on Campus Antisemitism

### Primary Congressional Hearing: "Holding Campus Leaders Accountable and Confronting Antisemitism"

**Event Details:**
- **Date:** December 5, 2023
- **Committee:** House Committee on Education and the Workforce
- **Location:** 2175 Rayburn House Office Building
- **Time:** 10:15 AM ET
- **Duration:** 5 hours

**Key Participants:**
- Claudine Gay, President of Harvard University
- M. Elizabeth Magill, President of University of Pennsylvania
- Sally Kornbluth, President of MIT

## Key Themes and Context

### December 2023 Hearing Significance

The December 5, 2023 hearing became historically significant for several reasons:

1. **Most-Viewed Congressional Hearing:** Achieved over 1 billion views across platforms
2. **Viral Questioning:** Rep. Elise Stefanik's exchange regarding "calling for the genocide of Jews" and campus codes of conduct went viral globally
3. **Presidential Resignations:**
   - Liz Magill resigned as University of Pennsylvania President on December 9, 2023 (4 days after hearing)
   - Claudine Gay resigned as Harvard President on January 2, 2024 (amid plagiarism allegations and hearing backlash)
   - Sally Kornbluth remained as MIT President with Board of Trustees support

### Core Issue Discussed

The central controversy involved the university presidents' inability or unwillingness to state clearly that calls for genocide of Jewish people would violate their codes of conduct regarding bullying and harassment. Their responses that such speech would need to be evaluated "depending on context" sparked massive backlash.

## November 2023 Free Speech Hearing Context

### Key Issues Addressed

- **Antisemitic Threats:** Cornell University canceled classes on November 3 following antisemitic threats
- **Student Experiences:** Jewish students testifying about fear and safety concerns on campuses
- **Conservative Student Treatment:** Conservative student activists describing harassment for hosting speakers with different viewpoints
- **Protest Disruptions:** Approximately 10 pro-Palestine protesters arrested during the hearing for obstruction
- **First Amendment Concerns:** Examination of threats to constitutional free speech protections on campuses
