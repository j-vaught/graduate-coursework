# Dartmouth Earns FIRE's Top Rating for Free Speech

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/news/dartmouth-earns-fires-top-rating-free-speech

## Content

Dartmouth College has reclaimed the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression's (FIRE) "green light" speech code rating, becoming the only Ivy League institution to hold this designation. This achievement follows policy reforms implemented under new President Sian Beilock.

### The Rating System

FIRE awards green light ratings to schools with no policies that significantly threaten student free speech rights. Dartmouth joins only 67 other institutions nationwide with this distinction.

### Historical Context

- Dartmouth first earned the green light rating in 2005
- In 2013, a "bias incident reporting" protocol was established that allowed peer-reporting and investigations for minor infractions like "joke telling"
- FIRE downgraded the school to yellow light status in 2015 due to these concerns

### Recent Changes

Under President Beilock's leadership (since 2023), Dartmouth revised its harassment policies and reformed the bias reporting protocol. The revised system maintains compliance with legal standards while clarifying that speech protected by free speech principles won't face investigation or punishment.

### Campus Initiatives

The college launched Dartmouth Dialogues in January 2024, featuring the Dialogue Project to facilitate conversations across differences. An expanded Open Expression Facilitators program now covers the entire campus community, with trained observers ensuring events proceed without disruption.

### Future Implications

If Dartmouth had held its green light status during the 2026 College Free Speech Rankings survey, it would rank 107th instead of its current 224th position.
