 11:07pm

## Recap: South Carolina volleyball concludes 2025 season with room to grow

 

 
 
 
 By Genevieve Navare
 
 | 
 Dec. 3
 
 
 
 

 South Carolina volleyball concluded the 2025 regular season with a 10-16 overall record, highlighted by strong non-conference performances and key wins over Arkansas, No. 25 Florida and Ole Miss. Despite challenges in SEC play, the Gamecocks showed growth through balanced offensive production, improvised defensive consistency and contributions from both veteran players and emerging talent.
