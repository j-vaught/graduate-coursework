# Virginia Commonwealth University Earns Top Rating for Free Speech

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/news/virginia-commonwealth-university-earns-top-rating-free-speech

## Content

## Article Summary

Virginia Commonwealth University (VCU) has received a "green light" rating from the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE), indicating the university has no written policies that seriously threaten student speech rights.

## Key Details

**Background:** VCU began its journey toward this rating in 2018 when university administrators consulted with FIRE about improving speech protections. FIRE identified six policies requiring revision.

**Progress:** The university successfully updated five policies covering dormitory decorations, computer usage, student conduct, sexual harassment, and campus space reservations. However, a problematic sex-based misconduct policy prevented a full green light rating until recently.

**Recent Achievement:** Following Virginia Governor Glenn Youngkin's campus speech summit in fall 2023, VCU administrators renewed discussions with FIRE about the remaining problematic policy. The revised policy clarifies that conduct examples must meet the definition of harassment to be punishable.

## Significance

VCU joins four other Virginia institutions earning green light status: the University of Virginia, George Mason University, the College of William and Mary, and Radford University. Virginia now ranks third nationally for green light schools, with nearly 75,000 undergraduates benefiting from strong speech protections.
