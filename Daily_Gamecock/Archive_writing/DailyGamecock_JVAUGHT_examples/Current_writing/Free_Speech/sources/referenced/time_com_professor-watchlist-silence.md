# Professors Targeted by Conservative Watchlist Fire Back: 'It's Obviously an Attempt to Silence People'

**URL:** https://time.com/4588165/professor-watchlist-silence-conservative/

## Content

**Author:** Katie Reilly
**Publication:** TIME
**Date:** December 1, 2016

Conservative group Turning Point USA launched a "Professor Watchlist" identifying educators accused of "discriminating against conservative students and advancing leftist propaganda." Targeted professors strongly objected to the initiative.

### Professors' Concerns

University of Pennsylvania's Anthea Butler, listed for a deleted tweet, stated the watchlist "makes it seem like you're some kind of professor who doesn't do good work." She characterized it as "an attempt to silence people."

Enrique Neblett Jr. from University of North Carolina expressed deeper concerns: "My biggest fear is that people will see my name on this list and target me or my family."

### Questionable Inclusions

John Traphagan from University of Texas at Austin was included for a newspaper column advocating gun control—work unrelated to classroom instruction. He questioned whether political opinions constitute discrimination.

### Organizers' Rationale

Charlie Kirk, executive director, claimed the list aims to balance campus discourse, asserting conservative students feel "silenced, ostracized or marginalized." He denied intimidation was the goal, though critics viewed the public listing as inherently threatening.

### Broader Context

Professors noted the timing—shortly after the presidential election—amplified potential dangers, particularly regarding safety concerns in politically charged climates.
