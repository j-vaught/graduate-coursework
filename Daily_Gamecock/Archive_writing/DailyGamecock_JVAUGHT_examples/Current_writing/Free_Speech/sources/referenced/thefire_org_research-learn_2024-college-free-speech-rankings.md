# 2024 College Free Speech Rankings

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/research-learn/2024-college-free-speech-rankings

## Content

## Article Overview

The Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE) and College Pulse released their fourth annual College Free Speech Rankings, surveying 55,102 undergraduates from 254 institutions about campus speech climates.

## Key Findings

**Top Performers:**
Michigan Technological University leads with a score of 78.01, followed by Auburn University (72.53), University of New Hampshire (72.17), Oregon State University (71.56), and Florida State University (69.64).

**Lowest Performers:**
Harvard University scored 0.00—"the lowest score possible"—earning an "Abysmal" rating. The University of Pennsylvania, University of South Carolina, Georgetown University, and Fordham University complete the bottom five.

## Critical Insights

**Student Self-Censorship:**
Approximately one-fifth of students reported self-censoring frequently. More than half expressed concern about reputation damage from misunderstood statements. About one-quarter reported pressure to avoid controversial classroom discussions.

**Contentious Topics:**
Nearly half identified abortion as difficult to discuss. Gun control, racial inequality, and transgender rights each presented challenges for 42-43% of students.

**Speaker Tolerance Gap:**
Students showed significantly more willingness to host liberal speakers (57-72% support) than conservative speakers (29-43% support), revealing ideological asymmetry in campus speech tolerance.

**Disruptive Protest Acceptance:**
Forty-five percent found blocking speech attendees acceptable to some degree, while 27% accepted violence for protest purposes—both increases from previous years.

**Deplatforming Success:**
Schools ranked in the bottom five experienced an alarming "81% success rate" for deplatforming attempts, compared to much lower rates at top institutions.
