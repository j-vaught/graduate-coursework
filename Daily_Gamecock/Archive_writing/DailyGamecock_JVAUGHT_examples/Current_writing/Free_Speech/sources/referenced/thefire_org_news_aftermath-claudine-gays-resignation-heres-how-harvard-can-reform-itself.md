# In the aftermath of Claudine Gay's resignation, here's how Harvard can reform itself

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/news/aftermath-claudine-gays-resignation-heres-how-harvard-can-reform-itself

## Content

Harvard President Claudine Gay stepped down after just six months, the shortest tenure in the university's history. While plagiarism allegations provided the immediate catalyst, FIRE expressed earlier concern that pressure for her resignation could have pushed the institution toward greater censorship rather than reform.

### Key Points

- Harvard currently ranks last on FIRE's 2024 Free Speech Rankings
- The organization noted that "Censorship on campus has been the norm for decades" and administrators may exploit crises to expand speech restrictions
- FIRE emphasized that plagiarism warrants removal, but this moment presents an opportunity for institutional reform

### Proposed Solutions

The article outlines several reform frameworks:

1. FIRE's "10 common-sense reforms" addressing free speech protection and reducing administrative bloat
2. Harvard psychology professor Steven Pinker's five-point plan emphasizing "free speech, institutional neutrality, nonviolence, viewpoint diversity, and disempowering DEI"
3. University of Pennsylvania's constitutional draft with similar principles

FIRE offered Harvard future collaboration, stating the university's next leadership should consider "a substantial university-wide change to its approach to free speech and academic freedom."
