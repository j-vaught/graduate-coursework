# Louisiana Tech Earns Top Rating for Free Speech

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/news/louisiana-tech-earns-top-rating-free-speech

## Content

Louisiana Tech University has received a "green light" rating from the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE), indicating the institution has no written policies that seriously threaten student free speech rights. This achievement places the university among only 65 schools nationwide with this designation.

### The Journey to Green Light Status

The university began with a "red light" rating in summer 2023 due to a dozen speech-restrictive policies. Through collaboration with FIRE's Policy Reform team, Louisiana Tech improved to "yellow light" status by winter 2023, then achieved the top rating in early 2024 after revising 11 additional policies.

### Notable Policy Changes

The university implemented viewpoint-neutral policies regarding room decorations and campus posting. According to FIRE Senior Program Officer Mary Griffin, "At a time when other institutions are sharply limiting student expression by" censoring unpopular viewpoints, the revised policies demonstrate commitment to robust dialogue.

### Broader Impact

Louisiana Tech is part of the University of Louisiana system, which encompasses nine institutions. FIRE has worked with this system for nearly two years, helping revise 32 speech-restrictive policies across all constituent schools. Over 14,000 students now attend green light institutions in Louisiana.
