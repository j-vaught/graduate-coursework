# University of South Carolina Earns Top Rating for Free Speech

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/news/university-south-carolina-earns-top-rating-free-speech

## Content

The University of South Carolina has achieved a "green light" rating from the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE), recognizing it as having no written policies that seriously threaten student free speech rights. This distinction places USC among only 66 colleges and universities nationwide with this designation.

### Key Changes

USC improved its campus free expression environment through policy revisions:

- **Student Code Updates**: Modified harassment and technology misuse provisions
- **Posting Policy**: Revised to allow students to post content freely with only content-neutral restrictions
- **Carolinian Creed**: Updated language clarifying that students retain full free speech rights and won't face discipline for disagreeing with aspirational values

### Institutional Commitment

Beyond policy changes, USC demonstrated comprehensive support for free expression:

- The board of trustees endorsed the Chicago Statement in June 2023
- Launched an educational campaign in February 2024 to inform students about free expression
- Received an Audit & Advisory Services report on free speech policies

### Quote from Leadership

"The Board of Trustees is steadfast in its support for free speech at USC," explained Board Chair Thad H. Westbrook, emphasizing that the commitment centers on fostering "a culture for higher learning that opens eyes and ears rather than closing them to difficult ideas."

### FIRE's Recognition

A FIRE senior program officer noted that USC's approach was exemplary, combining policy alignment with First Amendment standards and institutional communication about free expression values.
