# FIRE Faculty Network Conference

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/faculty-network-conference

## Content

## Title
FIRE Faculty Network Conference

## Overview
The Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE) hosts an annual Faculty Network Conference bringing together faculty supporters committed to promoting free expression and academic freedom at their institutions.

## Key Content

**About the Network**
The FIRE Faculty Network comprises educators interested in defending free speech and academic freedom on college campuses. Membership is open to faculty who wish to join this coalition.

**Attendee Testimonials**
Participants praised the conference's organization and value. One attendee noted: "Overall, it exceeded my expectations for being informative, interesting, friendly" and professionally beneficial. Another shared: "I was hoping to make connections and find people who are also fighting for free speech" at their institutions—a goal they achieved.

**Notable Recognition**
At the 2023 conference, FIRE presented the inaugural "Berkson Courageous Colleague Award" to Mark Berkson, a religion professor at Hamline University, for publicly defending a colleague's academic freedom after controversy over classroom materials.

**Participation**
Interested faculty can join the network and attend the annual conference through FIRE's website. The organization provides resources, speaker opportunities, and advocacy support for faculty members defending free expression on campus.
