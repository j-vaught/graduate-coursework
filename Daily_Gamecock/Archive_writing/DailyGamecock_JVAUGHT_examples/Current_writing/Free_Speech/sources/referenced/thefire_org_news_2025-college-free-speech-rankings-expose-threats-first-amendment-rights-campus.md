# 2025 College Free Speech Rankings expose threats to First Amendment rights on campus

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/news/2025-college-free-speech-rankings-expose-threats-first-amendment-rights-campus

## Content

### Key Findings

**Top Schools for Free Speech:**
1. University of Virginia
2. Michigan Technological University
3. Florida State University
4. Eastern Kentucky University
5. Georgia Institute of Technology

**Lowest-Ranked Schools:**
- Harvard University (ranked 251st)
- Columbia University (250th)
- New York University (249th)
- University of Pennsylvania (248th)
- Barnard College (247th)

### Major Concerns

The rankings, based on over 58,000 student responses from 250+ institutions, reveal troubling trends:

- **52%** of students believe blocking speakers is acceptable, up from 45% previously
- **69%** find shouting down speakers acceptable, compared to 63% the prior year
- **55%** struggle discussing Israeli-Palestinian issues on campus—the highest recorded for any topic
- **17%** report self-censoring due to peer or administrative concerns

Conservative students self-censor most frequently. Very conservative students reported self-censoring at **34%**, compared to only **15%** of very liberal students.

### Administrative Failures

The report notes that **42%** of students doubt their administration protects free speech, while **47%** question whether leadership would defend speakers during controversies.

FIRE CEO Greg Lukianoff stated: "The Middle East crisis plunged campuses into absolute chaos last academic year and administrators largely failed in their response."
