# Middle Tennessee State University Earns Top Rating for Free Speech

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/news/middle-tennessee-state-university-earns-top-rating-free-speech

## Content

## Article Summary

Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU) has received a "green light" rating from the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE), placing it among only 68 colleges nationwide with no policies that jeopardize student free speech rights.

## Key Details

**Institution Recognition:**
MTSU joined the University of Tennessee-Knoxville as Tennessee's only other green light-rated school. The university's President Sidney A. McPhee emphasized that "maintaining a welcoming environment to the free expression of ideas" has been a priority throughout his 20+ year tenure.

**Policy Changes:**
The university refined several policies this spring, including:
- Violence on campus guidelines (now limiting restrictions to true threats)
- Amplified sound regulations (removing "offensive language" ban)
- Electronic mail policies (clarifying that annoying emails are permitted but harassment is not)

**First Amendment Commitment:**
MTSU houses two notable free speech institutions: the John Seigenthaler Chair of Excellence in First Amendment Studies and the Free Speech Center, which serves 5.4 million annual users and maintains the First Amendment Encyclopedia.

**Director Perspective:**
Ken Paulson, Free Speech Center Director, noted the revisions required minimal effort: "tweaking fewer than a half-dozen phrases without changing the university's original intent."
