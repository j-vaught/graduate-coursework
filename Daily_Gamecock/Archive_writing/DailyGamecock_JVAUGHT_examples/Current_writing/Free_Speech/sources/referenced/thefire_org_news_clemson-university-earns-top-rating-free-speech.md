# Clemson University Earns Top Rating for Free Speech

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/news/clemson-university-earns-top-rating-free-speech

## Content

Clemson University has received a "green light" rating from the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE), placing it among only 67 schools nationwide with the highest free speech protections.

### What Changed

Clemson worked with FIRE to revise multiple policies that could restrict student expression. The most significant change involved modifying language in the student code of conduct—replacing the word "expected" with "encouraged" regarding civility standards. This shift prevents the policy from being weaponized against protected First Amendment speech.

### Timeline

- February 2024: Clemson contacted FIRE after receiving a "yellow light" rating
- Spring 2024: The board of trustees adopted a formal "Statement on Freedom of Speech"
- June 2024: Green light rating awarded

### Impact

The revised policies now protect expression rights for more than 144,000 students across seven recently upgraded Southern schools, including Georgia Tech, University of South Carolina, and Louisiana Tech.

### Notable Quote

"These important changes mean the Clemson Tigers are free to roar knowing their First Amendment rights are secure" — Mary Griffin, FIRE Senior Program Officer

FIRE offers free policy consultation to help other institutions achieve similar standards through their Policy Reform team.
