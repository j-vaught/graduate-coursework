# University of Chicago - FIRE Profile

**URL:** https://www.thefire.org/colleges/university-chicago

## Content

## Key Information

**Institution Type:** Private University
**Location:** Chicago, Illinois

## Speech Code Rating: Green

The University of Chicago has received a "Green" rating, indicating that its policies "nominally protect free speech." This represents the highest classification in FIRE's speech code evaluation system.

## Notable Policies

The institution has adopted several key policies reviewed by FIRE:

1. **Report of the Committee on Freedom of Expression** (Green rating) - The university states that "debate or deliberation may not be suppressed because ideas are thought offensive, unwise, [or] immoral."

2. **Harassment Policy** (Yellow rating) - Addresses unlawful harassment as discrimination based on protected identity characteristics.

3. **Title IX Sexual Harassment Policy** (Green rating) - Compliant with federal guidelines on sexual harassment.

4. **Bias Education and Support Team (BEST)** (Green rating) - Provides support for students impacted by bias incidents.

5. **Posting and Protest Policies** (Green ratings) - Permit student expression with designated procedures.

## Additional Recognition

The university has adopted the Chicago Statement for Free Speech, demonstrating institutional commitment to expression principles.

## Rankings & Data

- **Free Speech Rankings:** Ranked 3 of 257 colleges nationally
- **Active Cases:** 6 documented by FIRE
- **Due Process Ratings:** F, D, C grades across different categories
