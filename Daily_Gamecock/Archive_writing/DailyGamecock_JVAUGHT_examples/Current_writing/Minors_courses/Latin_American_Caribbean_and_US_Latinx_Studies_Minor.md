# Latin American, Caribbean, and US Latinx Studies Minor

## Program Requirements

**Language Requirement (does not count toward 18 hours):**
- At least one 3-hour course at the 200-level in Spanish or Portuguese
- This serves as a prerequisite for the minor

**Minimum Credit Hours:** 18

**Interdisciplinary Requirement:** Coursework must span at least two different academic disciplines

---

## Required Courses

None specified beyond the language requirement prerequisite.
