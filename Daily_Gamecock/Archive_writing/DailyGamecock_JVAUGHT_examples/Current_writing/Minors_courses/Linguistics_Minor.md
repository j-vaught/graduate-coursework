# Linguistics Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Overview
The Linguistics Minor is offered through the interdisciplinary studies program in the College of Arts and Sciences. It prepares students for graduate work and careers emphasizing critical thinking, attention to detail, creative problem-solving, and communication skills.

## Course Requirements

### Required Courses (3 credits)
Students must select one of the following:

| Course Code | Course Name |
|-------------|-------------|
| LING 300 | Introduction to Language Sciences |
| LING 301 | The English Language |

### Elective Courses (15 credits)
Complete 5 additional LING courses from available options, which may include:

- Language in society
- Historical linguistics
- Philosophy of language
- Acquisition of language by children
- Language-specific courses (English, French, German, Spanish)

**Note:** Specific course codes for all electives were not available in the source documentation. Students should consult the full academic bulletin or department for complete elective offerings.
