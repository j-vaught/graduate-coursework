# Spanish Minor - Course Requirements
**University of South Carolina**

## Program Overview
- **Total Credits:** 18 hours
- **Level Requirement:** 200-level or above
- **Restriction:** No more than 6 credits below 300-level
- **On-Campus Requirement:** Minimum 9 hours must be completed on campus
- **Minimum Grade:** C required in all courses

---

## Required Courses (3 hours)

| Course Code | Title | Credits |

| SPAN 302 | Advanced Spanish | 3 |

*Note: Students who score S7, SD, or S8 on language placement exams are exempt from SPAN 302 and should replace it with another course matching their placement level.*

## Course Selection Guidelines

**Typical Course Pathway:**
- Take SPAN 209 and SPAN 210
- Complete a minimum of 4 additional courses at the 300- and 400-levels
- Complete at least 1 course at the 400-level (recommended)

**Specialized Tracks:**
- **Business/Professional:** SPAN 316, SPAN 417
- **Healthcare:** SPAN 360
- **Heritage Speakers:** SPAN 311
- **Culture and Literature:** SPAN 400, SPAN 401, SPAN 404, SPAN 405
- **Cinema:** SPAN 380, SPAN 381

**Important:** SPAN 615 may not be applied to the minor. Only SPAN courses at or above a student's language placement level count toward the minor (unless approved by the Spanish Program).
