# Electrical Engineering Minor - University of South Carolina

## Required Courses (12 credit hours)

### Core Requirements - Choose one:
- **ELCT 102** - Electrical Science (3 hrs)
- **ELCT 220** - Electrical Engineering for Non-Majors (3 hrs)

### Additional Core Requirements:
- **ELCT 221** - Circuits (3 hrs)
- **ELCT 222** - Signals and Systems (3 hrs)
- **ELCT 371** - Electronics (3 hrs)

## Elective Courses (6 credit hours)

### Elective Requirements - Select:
- One 300-level ELCT course (3 hrs)
- One 500-level ELCT course (3 hrs)

## Program Summary

- **Total Credit Hours Required:** 18
- **Total Courses:** 7 (5 required + 2 electives)
- **Unique Course Prefixes:** 1 (ELCT)
- **Required Courses:** 5
- **Elective Courses:** 2

## Notes

- Students must choose between ELCT 102 and ELCT 220 for the first core course (counts as 1 course)
- The 500-level elective generally has prerequisites at the 300-level
- Six recommended sequences are available tailored to specific career interests: manufacturing, renewable energy, power systems, power electronics, wireless communications, and semiconductor devices
