# University of South Carolina - Geography Minor

## Program Overview
- **Total Credit Hours Required:** 18 hours
- **Level Restrictions:**
  - Maximum 3 credit hours from 100-level courses
  - Maximum 9 credit hours from 200-level courses

## Structure
The Geography Minor is a **flexible program** offered by the Department of Geography. Students select 18 credit hours from available GEOG courses, subject to the level restrictions above.

## Notes
- The Geography Minor is flexible and does not require specific courses
- Students must complete 18 credit hours total with the level restrictions noted above
- Specialized minors are available in: environmental geography, geographic information science, meteorology and climatology, physical geography, and regional geography (consult faculty advisor for details)
