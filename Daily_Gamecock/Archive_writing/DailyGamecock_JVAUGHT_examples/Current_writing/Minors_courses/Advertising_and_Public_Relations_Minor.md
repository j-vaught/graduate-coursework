# Advertising and Public Relations Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Overview
The Advertising and Public Relations minor is an 18-credit hour program that provides students with a solid background in the principles of advertising and public relations. It complements many other fields of study as nearly every career requires an understanding of internal and external communication, social media marketing, and message development.

Students must select nine hours of any JOUR elective courses to complete the 18-credit minor.
