# Economics Minor - University of South Carolina

## Course Requirements

### Prerequisite Course (3 hours)
- **MATH 122**: Calculus for Business Administration and Social Sciences
- **MATH 141**: Calculus I

### Required Courses

**Students must select ONE of these combinations:**

1. **ECON 221** - Principles of Microeconomics
2. **ECON 222** - Principles of Macroeconomics

OR

1. **ECON 224** - Introduction to Economics
2. **ECON 221** - Principles of Microeconomics

OR

1. **ECON 224** - Introduction to Economics
2. **ECON 222** - Principles of Macroeconomics

**Plus BOTH of these:**
- **ECON 321** - Intermediate Microeconomic Theory
- **ECON 322** - Intermediate Macroeconomic Theory

### Elective Courses (6-12 hours)
ECON electives at the 300 level or higher, excluding ECON 499 and ECON 511.

### Special Notes
- Students earning an A in ECON 224 are exempted from taking ECON 221 or ECON 222 but must complete one additional economics elective instead.
- At least half of the courses in the minor must be completed in residence at the University.
