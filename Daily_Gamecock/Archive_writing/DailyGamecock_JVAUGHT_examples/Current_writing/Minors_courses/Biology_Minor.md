# Biology Minor - Course Requirements

## Prerequisites (19 Hours)
*Must be passed with a grade of C or higher.*

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| BIOL 101 | Biological Principles I | 3 |
| BIOL 101L | Biological Principles I Laboratory | 1 |
| BIOL 102 | Biological Principles II | 3 |
| BIOL 102L | Biological Principles II Laboratory | 1 |
| CHEM 111 | General Chemistry I | 3 |
| CHEM 111L | General Chemistry I Lab | 1 |
| CHEM 112 | General Chemistry II | 3 |
| CHEM 112L | General Chemistry II Lab | 1 |
| CHEM 333 | Organic Chemistry I | 3 |

## Required Biology Courses (9 Hours)

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| BIOL 301 | Ecology and Evolution | 3 |
| BIOL 302 | Cell and Molecular Biology | 3 |
| BIOL 303 | Fundamental Genetics | 3 |

*Note: At least two of these must be completed before progressing to higher-level courses.*

## Elective Courses (9 Hours)

Nine additional credit hours selected from BIOL 300-600 level courses, with at least 3 of these hours being lab credits from courses where both lecture and associated lab are completed.

---

## Summary Statistics

- **Total Courses**: 20
- **Unique Prefixes**: 2 (BIOL, CHEM)
- **Required**: 12 (9 prerequisites + 3 required biology)
- **Elective**: 9 hours (flexible selection from BIOL 300-600 levels)
