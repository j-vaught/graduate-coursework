## Notes
- At least one Religious Studies (RELG) course is required unless already completed as part of other degree requirements
- ARAB 121 and ARAB 122 are recommended for fulfilling the foreign language requirement
- Special topics courses from any Arts and Sciences department may apply with approval if majority of content relates to Islam, Muslim populations, and/or Islamic World contexts
- Students are encouraged to study Arabic and take courses on music, literature, public health, and environment of these regions
