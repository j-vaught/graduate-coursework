# Law and Society Interdisciplinary Minor

## Program Overview
The Law and Society minor consists of **18 credit hours** and requires students to complete **2 required courses (6 hours)** from foundational courses and **4 elective courses (12 hours)** from related courses.

- **Total Available Courses**: 63

- **Unique Course Prefixes**: 19 (ACCT, ANTH, ECON, ENGL, FAMS, GEOG, GLST, HIST, HTMT, ISCI, JOUR, MGMT, PHIL, POLI, RELG, RETL, SOCY, SPCH, SPTE, WGST)
