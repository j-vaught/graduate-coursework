## Minor Requirements Summary

- **Total Credit Hours:** 18 hours
- **Required Courses:** 2 courses (6 hours)
- **Elective Courses:** 12 hours
  - Minimum 3 hours from 200+ level courses
  - Minimum 9 hours from 300+ level courses
  - No more than 1 Independent Study course (3 hours) allowed with Director approval

## Notes

- All elective courses are subject to program director and faculty advisor approval
- Course selection may include approved courses from other departments pertaining to African American Studies
- Current listings based on Spring 2026 course schedule
