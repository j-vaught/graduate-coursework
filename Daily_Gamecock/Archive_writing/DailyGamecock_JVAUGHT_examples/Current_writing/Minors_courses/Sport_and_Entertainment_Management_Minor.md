# Sport and Entertainment Management Minor - Course Requirements

## Minor Overview
The Sport and Entertainment Management minor provides students with knowledge in the sports and entertainment industries relating to marketing, finance, promotion, risk management, and principles of law.

**Total Credit Hours:** 18 credits (9 required + 9 elective)
**Minimum Grade Required:** C in each course

---

## Required Courses (9 credit hours)

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| SPTE 201 | Introduction to Sport Management | 3 |
| SPTE 202 | Introduction to Live Entertainment Management | 3 |
| SPTE 203 | Introduction to Event and Venue Management | 3 |

---

## Elective Courses (9 credit hours)

### Upper-Level Electives (6 credits required)
Students must select either:
- SPTE 110 plus one additional SPTE course at the 300 level, OR
- Two SPTE courses at the 300 level (excluding SPTE 399)

### Advanced Electives (3 credits required)
Select one SPTE course at the 400, 500, or 600 level (excluding SPTE 444, 495, 498, and 499).
