# Ancient Greek Literature Minor

## Program Overview
Students develop fluency in Greek to study classical texts in their original language. Through the study and interpretation of these works, students acquire a deeper understanding of the linguistic and conceptual roots of their own cultures.

## Prerequisite Courses (6 Hours)
These courses must be completed before taking elective courses.

| Course Code | Title | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| GREK 121 | Elementary Ancient Greek I | 3 |
| GREK 122 | Elementary Ancient Greek II | 3 |

## Required/Elective Courses (18 Hours)
