# Audio Recording Minor - University of South Carolina

## Course Requirements

The Audio Recording Minor requires **18 credit hours** with all courses being mandatory (no electives).

### Required Courses

| Course Code | Course Title | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| MUSC 336 | Introduction to Computer Music | 3 |
| MUSC 365 | An Introduction to Audio Recording Techniques | 3 |
| MUSC 565 | Advanced Audio Recording Techniques | 3 |
| MUSC 566 | Fundamentals of Sound Use for Media | 3 |
| MUSC 567 | Recording Studio Techniques | 3 |
| MUSC 571 | Digital Audio Technology | 3 |

### Elective Courses

None - all courses are required.

## Notes

- The Audio Recording Minor is open to students with majors in the School of Music only.
- Total credit hours: 18
