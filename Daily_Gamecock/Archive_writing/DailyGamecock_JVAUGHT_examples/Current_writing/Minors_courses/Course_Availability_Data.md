# USC Minor Course Availability (Spring 2021 – Fall 2025)

To understand whether USC students can actually complete their chosen minors, we analyzed 15 semesters of course offering data across 53 different minors. We examined which required courses were offered each semester and whether students had realistic opportunities to take them.

This analysis revealed two types of problematic courses: **Ghost Courses** are required courses that were never offered during the entire 15-semester period, making it impossible for students to complete their minor. **Zombie Courses** are required courses offered so rarely (less than 30% of the time) that students face significant difficulty finding a semester when they're available.

## Key Findings

- **11 minors cannot be completed** due to missing required courses
- **17 ghost courses** have never been offered in the past five years
- **42 zombie courses** are offered so rarely that completing them on schedule is extremely difficult
- **Significant variation** by department—some departments offer courses consistently, while others rarely offer their required minors courses
- **11 minors at critical risk**, 16 at high risk, and 26 at medium or low risk
