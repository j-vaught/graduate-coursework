# Geological Sciences Minor

## Program Overview
**Total Credit Hours Required:** 20

---

## Required Courses

### Prerequisites (Select One)
- **CHEM 111 & 111L** - General Chemistry I and Lab
- **PHYS 201 & 201L** - General Physics I and Lab
- **PHYS 211 & 211L** - Essentials of Physics I and Lab

### Core Course (Select One - 4 credits)
- **GEOL 101** - Introduction to the Earth
- **GEOL 103** - Environment of the Earth
- **GEOL 201** - Observing the Earth

### Required Upper-Level Course (4 credits)
- **GEOL 302** - Rocks and Minerals

---

## Elective Courses

**Select Three Upper-Level Geology Courses (12 credits minimum, with at least two from this list):**

- **GEOL 305** - Earth Systems through Time
- **GEOL 315** - Surface and Near Surface Processes
- **GEOL 325** - Stratigraphy and Sedimentary Basins
- **GEOL 335** - Processes of Global Environmental Change
- **GEOL 345** - Igneous and Metamorphic Processes
- **GEOL 355** - Structural Geology and Tectonics

---

## Notes
- PHYS 201 or PHYS 211 is required for GEOL 355
- See MATH requirements for GEOL 345
- Prerequisites may vary; CHEM 111 & 111L, PHYS 201 & 201L, or PHYS 211 & 211L are required
