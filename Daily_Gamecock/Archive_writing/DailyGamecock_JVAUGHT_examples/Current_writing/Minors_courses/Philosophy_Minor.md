# Philosophy Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Structure

The Philosophy Minor requires **21 total hours**:
- **3 hours prerequisite** (required before upper-level courses)
- **18 hours upper-level coursework** (200-level and above)

---

## Required Courses

### Prerequisite (3 hours)
- **PHIL 114** - Introduction to Formal Logic I

---

## Notes

- At least half of the courses in the minor must be completed in residence at USC
- Students select their 18 hours of upper-level coursework in consultation with their academic advisor
- PHIL 114 (Introduction to Formal Logic I) is the prerequisite for all upper-level courses
