# University of South Carolina - Sociology Minor

## Program Overview
The Sociology Minor requires 18 total credit hours, consisting of one required introductory course and 15 credit hours of electives at the 300 and 500 levels.

## Required Courses (3 Credit Hours)
| Course Code | Course Title | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| SOCY 101 | Introductory Sociology | 3 |

## Elective Courses (15 Credit Hours)
Students must complete the following:
- One 500-level SOCY course (3 credits)
- Three 300-level SOCY courses (9 credits)
- One additional 300 or 500-level SOCY course (3 credits)

### Sample 300-Level Electives
The following courses are available at the 300 level:
- SOCY 3XX courses (specific course codes and titles vary by semester)

### Sample 500-Level Electives
- SOCY 5XX courses (specific course codes and titles vary by semester)

- The minor requires completion of SOCY 101 as the foundational course
- Students have flexibility in selecting which 300-level and 500-level courses to complete
- Specific elective course offerings vary by semester and should be verified through the current course catalog
