|-------------|-------------|

| EMCH 573 | Introduction to Nuclear Materials |

## Required Engineering Elective (3 hours)

Any 3-credit EMCH course numbered 300 and above that is not used to meet another requirement of the minor or a major requirement in the student's program of study.
