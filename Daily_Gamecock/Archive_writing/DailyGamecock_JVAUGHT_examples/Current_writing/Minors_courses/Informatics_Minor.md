# Informatics Minor - University of South Carolina

## Required Courses (9 credit hours)

| Course Code | Title | Credits |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| ISCI 201 | Data, Information & Society | 3 |
| ISCI 301 | Text Mining in Big Data Analytics | 3 |
| ISCI 434 | Introduction to Knowledge Discovery | 3 |

## Elective Courses (9 credit hours)

Students must select 9 hours from any additional ISCI courses. ISCI 480 can be repeated for credit when covering different topics.

| Course Prefix | Category |
|---------------|----------|
| ISCI | Electives (9 hours required) |

## Summary

- **Total Courses**: 3 required + electives from ISCI
- **Unique Prefixes**: 1 (ISCI)
- **Required**: 3 courses (9 credit hours)
- **Elective**: 9 credit hours from ISCI courses
- **Total Credit Hours**: 18
