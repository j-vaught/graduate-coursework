# Neuroscience Minor - Course Requirements

University of South Carolina - 18 Credit Hours Total

## Required Prerequisites (6 hours)
- **BIOL 101** - Biological Principles I (3 credits)
- **PSYC 101** - Introduction to Psychology (3 credits)

## Required for Minor (3 hours)
- **NSCI 300** - Introduction to Neuroscience (3 credits)

## Elective Courses (15 hours required)

### Biology Courses
BIOL 302, BIOL 302L, BIOL 303, BIOL 405, BIOL 460, BIOL 461, BIOL 505, BIOL 530, BIOL 534, BIOL 541, BIOL 541L, BIOL 543, BIOL 553, BIOL 612, BIOL 614, BIOL 620, BIOL 634, BIOL 635, BIOL 665, BIOL 667

### Engineering/Science Courses
BMEN 321, CSCE 555, ELCT 220

### Exercise Science Courses
EXSC 351, EXSC 303

### Neuroscience Courses
NSCI 498, NSCI 560, NSCI 570

### Philosophy Course
PHIL 351

### Psychology Courses
PSYC 370, PSYC 400, PSYC 405, PSYC 450, PSYC 503, PSYC 507, PSYC 550, PSYC 560, PSYC 570, PSYC 571, PSYC 572

## Notes
- Students may apply up to 6 credits of independent study (NSCI 498) toward elective requirements
- No more than 9 credits from any single course designator (prefix) may be applied
