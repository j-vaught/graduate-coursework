# Art History Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Overview
The Art History Minor requires 18 total credit hours with the following structure:
- **Required Courses:** 6 credit hours (2 courses)
- **Elective Courses:** 12 credit hours (3 courses from 300-level + 1 course from 500-level)

All courses must be passed with a grade of C or higher.

## Additional Information

- All minor courses must be passed with a grade of C or higher
- At least half of the courses in the minor must be completed in residence at the University
- Students pursuing a minor may take more than 18 credit hours if desired
