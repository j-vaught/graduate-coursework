# Environmental Studies Minor - Course List

## Required Courses (Must Include ENVR Courses)

The minor requires **18 credit hours total**:
- At least 12 credit hours with ENVR designators
- Remaining 6 credit hours from environmental studies major credit list

### Core ENVR Courses (with restrictions)

Maximum 8 credit hours from:
- ENVR 101: Introduction to Environmental Issues
- ENVR 101L: Introduction to Environmental Issues Laboratory
- ENVR 200: Environmental Perspectives
- ENVR 201: Environmental Science and Policy I
- ENVR 202: Environmental Science and Policy II

Note: ENVR 480 cannot be used for credit in the minor.
