# Jewish Studies Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Overview
Total Credit Hours Required: 18

## Required Courses
Complete at least ONE of the following core courses:

| Course Code | Course Title |
|-------------|--------------|
| JSTU 381 | Jewish History I: Late Antiquity to 1500 |
| JSTU 382 | Jewish History II: 1500 to the Present |
| JSTU 373 | The Holocaust |

## Elective Courses
Students must complete five (5) additional Jewish Studies program elective courses beyond the core requirement to fulfill the 18 credit hour requirement.

*Note: For current list of approved electives, contact the Jewish Studies Program*

## Summary Statistics
- **Total Courses Required:** 6 (1 required core + 5 electives)
- **Unique Prefixes:** 1 (JSTU)
- **Required Courses:** 1 (at least one core)
- **Elective Courses:** 5

## Program Notes
- The Jewish Studies Program maintains an interdisciplinary approach to scholarship
- Courses may be offered across multiple departments including History, Literature, Religious Studies, and the College of Education
- Students should contact the program for current elective offerings and course approval
