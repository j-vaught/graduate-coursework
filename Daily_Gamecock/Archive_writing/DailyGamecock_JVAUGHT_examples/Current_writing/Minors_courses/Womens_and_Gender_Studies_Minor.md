## Notes
- All minor courses must be passed with a grade of C or higher
- At least half of the minor courses must be completed in residence at the University
- Maximum of one Independent Study course (3 hours) may be applied to the minor
- Special topics courses from various academic departments may apply if approved by Women's and Gender Studies
