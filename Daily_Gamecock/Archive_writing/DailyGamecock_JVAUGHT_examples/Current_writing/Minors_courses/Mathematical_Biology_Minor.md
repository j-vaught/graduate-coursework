# Mathematical Biology Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Overview
The Mathematical Biology Minor is an interdisciplinary program requiring 22 credit hours of study. Students must complete prerequisite courses in mathematics and sciences, followed by specialized coursework in linear algebra, differential equations, and applications to biological systems.

## Prerequisites (11 Hours)

### Mathematics Prerequisites (Required - 11 hours)
- MATH 141: Calculus I (4 credits)
- MATH 142: Calculus II (4 credits)
- MATH 241: Vector Calculus (3 credits)

### Science Prerequisites (Required - Select 2 courses)
- BIOL 101: Biological Principles I
- BIOL 101L: Biological Principles I Laboratory
- BIOL 102: Biological Principles II
- BIOL 102L: Biological Principles II Laboratory
- MSCI 101: The Ocean Environment
- MSCI 102: The Living Ocean

### Additional Requirement (Required - Select 1 course)
- BIOL 301: Ecology and Evolution
- BIOL 303: Fundamental Genetics
- MSCI 314: Physical Oceanography

## Minor Courses (19 Hours)

### Linear Algebra (Required - Select 1 course, 4 hours)
- MATH 344: Applied Linear Algebra
- MATH 544: Linear Algebra

### Differential Equations (Required - Select 2 courses, 6 hours)
- MATH 242: Elementary Differential Equations
- MATH 520: Ordinary Differential Equations
- MATH 521: Boundary Value Problems and Partial Differential Equations

### Additional Electives (Select 3 courses, 9 hours)
- MATH 523: Mathematical Modeling of Population Biology
- BIOL 552/MSCI 552: Population Genetics
- BIOL 652: Evolutionary Biology
- BMEN 212: Fundamentals of Biomedical Systems
- BMEN 240: Cellular and Molecular Biology with Engineering Applications
- BMEN 263: Introduction to Biomechanics
- BMEN 290: Thermodynamics of Biomolecular Systems
- BMEN 391: Kinetics in Biomolecular Systems
- MSCI 582: Marine Hydrodynamics
