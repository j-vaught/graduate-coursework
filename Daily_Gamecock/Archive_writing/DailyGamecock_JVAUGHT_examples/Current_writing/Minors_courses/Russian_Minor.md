## Program Summary

- **Total Credits Required:** 18 credit hours
- **Required Courses:** 4 courses (12 credit hours)
- **Elective Courses:** Minimum 6 credit hours from 200-500 level courses

*Note: Students who test into higher-level language courses may substitute 400-level language classes or additional literature and culture courses (200-500 level) for some of the required language credits.*
