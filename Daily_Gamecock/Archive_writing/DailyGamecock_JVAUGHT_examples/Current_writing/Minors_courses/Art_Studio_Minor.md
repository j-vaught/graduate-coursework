# Art Studio Minor - Course Catalog

**University of South Carolina**

## Program Overview
The Art Studio Minor consists of 18 credit hours of coursework. Students must complete 2 foundation courses (6 hours) and 3 additional courses at the 200+ level (12 hours).

## Notes
- All minor courses must be passed with a grade of C or higher
- At least half of the courses in the minor must be completed in residence at the University of South Carolina
- Students are encouraged to follow discipline-specific advisement tracks: Graphic Design, Drawing, Painting, Printmaking, Photography, Ceramics, Sculpture, Jewelrymaking, or Generalist 2D/3D Studio
- Students may choose to emphasize either 2D or 3D art
