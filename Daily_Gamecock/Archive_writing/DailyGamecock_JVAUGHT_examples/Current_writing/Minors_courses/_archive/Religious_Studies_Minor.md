# Religious Studies Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Requirements
- **Total Credit Hours:** 18
- **Required Foundation:** 1 of RELG 101 or RELG 120
- **Required 200-Level:** 1 course (any 200-level RELG)
- **Required 300-Level:** 4 courses at 300-level (may substitute one 400-level for one 300-level)
- **Minimum Grade:** C or higher in all courses
