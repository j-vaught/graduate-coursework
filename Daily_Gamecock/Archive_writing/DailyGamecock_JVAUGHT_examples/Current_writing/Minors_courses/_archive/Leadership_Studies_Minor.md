# Leadership Studies Minor - Course Requirements

University of South Carolina | College of Arts and Sciences | Department of Political Science

**Total Credit Hours:** 18 minimum

---

## Required Courses (6 credits)

### Introductory Course (3 credits) - Select One:
- POLI 215
- PCAM 205

### Second Course (3 credits) - Select from One Category:

**Public Speaking Options:**
- MGMT 250: Professional Communication
- SPCH 140: Public Communication
- SPCH 201: Popular Communication and Public Culture
- SPCH 230: Business and Professional Speaking
- SPCH 330: Small Group Communication
- SPCH 331: Organizational Communication
- SPCH 380: Persuasive Communication
- SPCH 464: Speechwriting

**Organizational/Group Dynamics Options:**
- POLI 368: Interest Groups and Social Movements
- PSYC 430: Survey of Social Psychology
- PSYC 487: Community Psychology
- SOCY 311: Ecology of Human Social Systems
- SOCY 312: Bureaucracy and Modern Society
- SOCY 354: Collective Behavior
- SOCY 355: Race and Ethnic Relations

**Management Options:**
- POLI 370: Introduction to Public Administration
- MGMT 371: Principles of Management
- MGMT 374: Strategic Human Resource Management
- MGMT 376: Employee Engagement
- MGMT 401: Negotiation and Conflict in the Workplace
- MGMT 402: Managing Teams in the Workplace
- MGMT 403: Leadership in Organizations

---

## Elective Courses (12 credits)

### Experiential Coursework (3 credits) - Select One:
An approved course incorporating:
- Study abroad
- Service-learning (8+ hours)
- Internship
- Similar experiential experiences

### Additional Courses (9 credits) - Select Three from at Least Two Groups:

**Group 1 - Ethics (17+ options)**
Includes philosophy, journalism, and ROTC courses

**Group 2 - Communication (40+ options)**
Includes anthropology, linguistics, management, and speech courses

**Group 3 - Diversity (70+ options)**
Includes African American studies, cultural studies, and gender studies courses

**Group 4 - Advanced Leadership Development (40+ options)**
Includes management, ROTC, social work, and related fields
