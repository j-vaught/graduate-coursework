# University of South Carolina - Marine Science Minor

## Program Overview
- **Total Credit Hours Required:** 18
- **Minimum Grade:** C or higher in all courses
- **Residency Requirement:** At least 50% must be completed at USC

## Notes
- The minor requires a total of 18 credit hours
- 14 credit hours are from required courses (MSCI 101, 102, 311, 313, 314)
- 6 credit hours must be selected from 300-level and above MSCI courses
- Multiple electives are available to meet the 6-credit elective requirement
- Course availability and eligibility should be verified with the Marine Science Department
