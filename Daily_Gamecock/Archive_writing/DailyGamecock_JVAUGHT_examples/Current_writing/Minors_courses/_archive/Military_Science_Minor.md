# Military Science Minor - Course Requirements

## Required Courses (19 Hours)

### Foundational Sequence
- **ARMY 101** - Introduction to Army Fundamental Tactics (1 credit)
- **ARMY 101L** - Lab in Military Science I (1 credit)
- **ARMY 102** - Foundations of Agile and Adaptive Leadership (1 credit)
- **ARMY 102L** - Lab in Military Science II (1 credit)
- **ARMY 201** - Leadership and Decision Making (2 credits)
- **ARMY 201L** - Lab in Military Science III (1 credit)
- **ARMY 202** - Army Doctrine and Team Development (2 credits)
- **ARMY 202L** - Lab in Military Science IV (1 credit)

### Advanced Sequence
- **ARMY 301** - Training Management and War Fighting Function (3 credits)
- **ARMY 301L** - Lab in Military Science V (1 credit)
- **ARMY 302** - Adaptive Leadership and Tactical Operations (3 credits)
- **ARMY 302L** - Lab in Military Science VI (1 credit)
- **ARMY 401** - The Army Officer and the Profession of Arms I (3 credits)
- **ARMY 401L** - Lab in Military Science VII (1 credit)
- **ARMY 402** - The Army Officer and the Profession of Arms II (3 credits)
- **ARMY 402L** - Lab in Military Science VIII (1 credit)

## Elective Courses (Professional Military Education)

Select **one** military history course:
- **ARMY 406** - American Military Experience
- **HIST 468** - American Military Experience

**Note:** ARMY 399 and ARMY 499 may be used as substitutions in the minor as approved by the Military Science department.
