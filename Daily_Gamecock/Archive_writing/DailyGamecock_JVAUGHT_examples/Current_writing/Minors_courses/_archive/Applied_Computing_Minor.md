## Summary Statistics

- **Total Courses in Minor**: 40
- **Unique Course Prefixes**: 6 (CSCE, GEOG, ITEC, MART, ISCI)
- **Required Courses**: 6 (must select 2 from Foundation + 2 from Intermediate + 2 from Advanced)
- **Elective Courses**: 34 (students choose from a pool of electives in each category)

## Notes

- Total credit hours for minor: 18
- Foundation level: Select 2 of 4 courses (6 hours)
- Intermediate level: Select 2 of 11 courses (6 hours)
- Advanced level: Select 2 of 24+ courses (6 hours)
- No course used to satisfy a Carolina Core, Major, or other Minor requirement may be used to satisfy the Applied Computing Minor requirements
