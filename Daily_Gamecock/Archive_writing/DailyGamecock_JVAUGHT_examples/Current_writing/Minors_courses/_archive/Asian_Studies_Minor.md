# Asian Studies Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Requirements
- **Total Credit Hours:** 18 minimum
- **Minimum Groups:** At least 3 different groups
- **Upper-Level Requirement:** At least 12 hours at 300-level or above
- **Language Elective:** One 200-level Chinese or Japanese course may be counted
