# Hospitality and Tourism Management Minor
## University of South Carolina

### Program Overview
The Hotel, Restaurant & Tourism Management Minor provides students with a broad understanding of the hospitality industry, including hotel and restaurant operations, tourism planning, event management, customer service, club management, and strategic leadership.

**Total Credit Hours:** 18 hours
**Minimum GPA Required:** 2.50 in HTMT courses
**Minimum Grade Required:** C or higher per course

---

## Required Courses (12 hours)

| Course Code | Course Title | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| HTMT 110 | Introduction to Hospitality Industry | 3 |
| HTMT 260 | Hotel Management | 3 |
| HTMT 280 | Foundations of Tourism 1 | 3 |
| *Plus 3 additional HTMT credits at any level* | Elective | 3 |

---

## Elective Courses (6 hours)

Students must select 6 credits from:
- HTMT courses at the 300 level or above
