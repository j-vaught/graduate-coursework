# Portuguese Minor - Course Requirements

## Program Overview
Students must successfully complete **18 hours at the 200-level and above** to earn the Minor in Portuguese.

## Eligible Courses (200-level and above)

### Required Prerequisite Path (not counted toward 18-hour requirement)
- PORT 121 – Elementary Portuguese
- PORT 122 – Basic Proficiency in Portuguese

### Available Courses (200-level and above - 18 hours required)

#### Core/Intermediate Courses
- PORT 201 – Intermediate Portuguese I
- PORT 202 – Intermediate Portuguese II
- PORT 299 – Accelerated Portuguese for Speakers of Spanish

#### Advanced Conversation and Composition
- PORT 309 – Advanced Conversation and Composition I
- PORT 310 – Advanced Conversation and Composition II

#### Literature and Culture
- PORT 312 – Introduction to Luso-Brazilian Literature
- PORT 325 – The Brazilian Modern Short Story
- PORT 360 – Social Justice in the Lusophone World
- PORT 375 – Special Topics in Luso-Brazilian Cultural Production
- PORT 398 – Selected Portuguese Topics

#### Specialized Courses
- PORT 316 – Business Portuguese

#### Independent Study & Advanced
- PORT 399 – Independent Study (up to 6 credits allowed)
- PORT 615 – Intensive Readings in Portuguese

## Additional Notes
- Up to 2 independent study courses allowed (max 6 credits total)
- Up to 6 credits from study abroad programs (with coordinator approval)
