## Notes
- Students cannot apply more than one course each from PSYC 399, PSYC 489, PSYC 498, PSYC 598, or PSYC 599 toward the minor
- Students cannot receive minor credit for both PSYC 455/NSCI 300 and PSYC 460
- At least half of the courses in the minor must be completed in residence
