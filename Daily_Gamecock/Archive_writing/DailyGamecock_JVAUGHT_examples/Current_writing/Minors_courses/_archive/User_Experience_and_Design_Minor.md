# User Experience and Design Minor
University of South Carolina - College of Information and Communications

## Program Overview
Total Credit Hours: 18

## Required Courses (6 hours)

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| ISCI 259 | User Experience Research Methods and Tools | 3 |
| ISCI 249 | Principles of User Experience and Design (UX/UI) | 3 |

*Alternative for ISCI 249:* JOUR 249 - Principles of User Experience and Design (UX/UI)

## Elective Courses (12 hours to select)

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| ISCI 250 | Information Design | 3 |
| ISCI 430 | User-Centered Information Architecture | 3 |
| ISCI 496 | Internship in Information Science (UX-focused) | 3 |
| ISCI 560 | Data Visualization | 3 |
| JOUR 203 | Principles of Visual Communications | 3 |

*Note: Additional electives may be approved by the School of Information Science.*

## Summary Statistics
