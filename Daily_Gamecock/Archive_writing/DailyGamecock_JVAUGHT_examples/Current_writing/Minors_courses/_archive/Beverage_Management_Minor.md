# Beverage Management Minor - Course Requirements

## Overview
Total Credit Hours: 18
Minimum GPA: 2.50 in HTMT courses
Minimum Grade: C or higher in each course

## Notes
- A course cannot be counted twice toward the minor (i.e., a course cannot count as both a required course and an elective course)
- Some courses appear in multiple categories (e.g., HTMT 373, HTMT 475, HTMT 476) and can be taken in whichever capacity applies to the student's progress
