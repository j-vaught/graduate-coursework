# German Minor - Course Requirements

## Program Overview
The German Minor requires **18 total credit hours** from courses at the 200-level and above.

## Required Courses (200-Level)

Students must select **up to 2 courses** from the following (maximum 6 credits):

| Course Code | Course Name |
|-------------|-------------|
| GERM 210 | Intermediate German |
| GERM 211 | Intermediate German |
| GERM 280 | German Culture and Civilization |
| GERM 290 | Viking Mythology |

## Course Statistics

- **Total Courses Available**: 24
- **Unique Prefixes**: 1 (GERM)
- **Required Courses**: 4 (select up to 2 from 200-level; minimum 4 from 300+ level)
- **Elective Courses**: 20

## Important Restrictions

- Only **one German course taught in English** (GERM 280, GERM 290, GERM 398, or GERM 580) may count towards the minor
- A maximum of **6 hours of GERM 333** (Summer Study Abroad) may apply to the German minor
- Students majoring in Business are encouraged to enroll in GERM 316 and GERM 416

## Minor Completion Requirements

To complete the German Minor, students must:
1. Select up to 2 courses from the 200-level list (maximum 6 credits)
2. Select a minimum of 4 courses from the 300-level and above list (minimum 12 credits)
3. Total: 18 credit hours minimum
