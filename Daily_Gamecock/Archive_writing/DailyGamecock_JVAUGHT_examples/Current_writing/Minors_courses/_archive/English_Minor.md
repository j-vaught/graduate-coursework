# English Minor - Course Requirements
## University of South Carolina

### Program Overview
The English Minor requires 6 courses total: 1 course at the 200-level and 5 courses at the 300-level or above.

## Notes
- All courses use the ENGL prefix
- 200-level courses provide foundational coursework
- 300+ level courses offer advanced study in literature, creative writing, professional writing, rhetoric, film, media, and linguistics
- Students select 1 course from the 200-level options and 5 courses from the 300+ level options
