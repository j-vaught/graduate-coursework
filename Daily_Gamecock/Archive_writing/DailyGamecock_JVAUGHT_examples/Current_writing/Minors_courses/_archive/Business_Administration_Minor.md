# Business Administration Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Overview
The Business Administration minor is available to all University of South Carolina students except those with majors in the Darla Moore School of Business. The minor requires completion of 18 credit hours with all courses being mandatory.

## Required Courses (18 Credit Hours)

| Course Code | Title | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| ACCT 222 | Survey of Accounting | 3 |
| ECON 224 | Introduction to Economics | 3 |
| FINA 333 | Finance and Markets | 3 |
| MGMT 371 | Principles of Management | 3 |
| MGSC 290 | Computer Information Systems in Business | 3 |
| MKTG 350 | Principles of Marketing | 3 |

## Elective Courses
None. All courses listed above are required.

## Notes
- Alternative prerequisite courses may substitute for specific requirements:
  - ACCT 225 may substitute for ACCT 222
  - ECON 221 or ECON 222 may substitute for ECON 224
