# Mechanical Engineering Minor - Course Requirements

**University of South Carolina**

---

## Required Courses (18 credit hours)

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| EMCH 200 | Statics 1 | 3 |
| EMCH 260 | Solid Mechanics 1 | 3 |
| EMCH 290 | Thermodynamics 1 | 3 |
| EMCH 310 | Dynamics 1 | 3 |
| EMCH 327 | Machine Design | 3 |
| EMCH 360 | Fluid Mechanics 1 | 3 |

## Elective Courses

No designated elective courses are specified in the minor requirements. However, students may petition the Department of Mechanical Engineering to substitute other EMCH courses at the 300-level and above for minor requirements upon departmental approval.

---

## Course Summary

- **Total Required Courses:** 6
- **Total Elective Courses:** 0 (designated)
- **Unique Course Prefixes:** EMCH
- **Total Credit Hours:** 18

**Note:** This minor requires 6 courses with a prerequisite of one semester of physics, three semesters of calculus, and differential equations.

**Source:** [University of South Carolina Academic Bulletins - Mechanical Engineering Minor](https://academicbulletins.sc.edu/undergraduate/engineering-computing/mechanical-engineering/mechanical-engineering-minor/)
