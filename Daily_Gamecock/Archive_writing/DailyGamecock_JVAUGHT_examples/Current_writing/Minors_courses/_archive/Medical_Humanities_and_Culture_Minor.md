# Medical Humanities and Culture Minor

## Program Overview
The minor in Medical Humanities and Culture consists of 18 credit hours or 6 classes. Students must select 2-4 courses (6-12 credit hours) from Group A (Humanities) and 2-4 courses (6-12 credit hours) from Group B (Social Sciences).
