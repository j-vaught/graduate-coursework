# Theatre Minor - Course Requirements

## Required Courses (9 credit hours)

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| THEA 200 | Understanding and Appreciation of Theatre | 3 |
| THEA 201 | Introduction to Theatre Studies | 3 |
| THEA 170 | Fundamentals of Acting | 3 |
| THEA 270 | Beginning Acting | 3 |
| THEA 253 | Stagecraft | 3 |
| THEA 280 | Elements of Design for Theatre Production | 3 |

*Note: One course from each pair above is required (total 9 credit hours)*

## Elective Courses (9 credit hours)

- 3 credits from any THEA 200-level or above course
- 6 credits from any THEA 300-level or above course

## Summary

**Total Credit Hours**: 18

**Unique Course Prefixes**: THEA

**Required Courses**: 3 (one option required from each pair, representing 6 unique course codes with alternatives)

**Elective Courses**: 9 credit hours (unspecified courses from THEA 200+ and THEA 300+ levels)

## Program Information

- Minimum 18 credit hours required
- At least half of the courses must be completed in residence at USC
- The minor allows students to pursue theatre through performance, stagecraft, and theatre studies coursework
