# Political Science Minor - Course Requirements

## Program Overview
- **Total Required Credits**: 18 hours
- **Required Courses**: 3 hours (1 course)
- **Elective Courses**: 15 hours (5 courses minimum at 300+ level)

## Required Courses (Select 1)

| Course Code | Course Title | Credits |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| POLI 101 | Introduction to Global Politics | 3 |
| POLI 201 | American National Government | 3 |

**Note:** POLI 101 and POLI 201 may not fulfill both Carolina Core requirements and the minor requirement. If either course applies to Carolina Core, students must take the alternative for the minor or complete an additional 300+ level elective instead.
