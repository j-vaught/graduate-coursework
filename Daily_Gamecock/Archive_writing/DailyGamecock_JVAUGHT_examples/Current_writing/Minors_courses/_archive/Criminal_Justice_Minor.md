# Criminal Justice Minor - Course Requirements

## Required Courses (9 credit hours)
- CRJU 101: The American Criminal Justice System (3 hours)
- CRJU 341: Sociology of Crime (3 hours)

## Elective Courses (Select 2 of 3 - 6 credit hours)
- CRJU 311: Policing (3 hours)
- CRJU 312: Corrections (3 hours)
- CRJU 313: Criminal Courts (3 hours)

## Additional Electives (Select 3 - 9 credit hours)
Select three additional Criminal Justice courses at the 300-, 400-, or 500-level

**Total: 18 credit hours**
