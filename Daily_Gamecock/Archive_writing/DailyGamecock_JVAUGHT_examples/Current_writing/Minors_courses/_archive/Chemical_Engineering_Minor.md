# Chemical Engineering Minor - Course Requirements

## Required Courses (12 hours)

| Course Code | Title | Credits |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| ECHE 300 | Chemical Process Principles | 3 |
| ECHE 311 | Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics | 3 |
| ECHE 320 | Chemical Engineering Fluid Mechanics | 3 |
| ECHE 440 | Separation Process Design | 3 |

**Note:** ECHE 320 can be replaced with ENCP 360 (Fluid Mechanics)
