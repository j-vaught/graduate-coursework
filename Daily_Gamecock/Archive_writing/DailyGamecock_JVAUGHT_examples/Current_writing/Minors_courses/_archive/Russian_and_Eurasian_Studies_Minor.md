|---|---|

| Course Code | Course Title |
|---|---|

|---|---|

## Summary

**Total Credit Hours: 18**

### Notes
- Students must complete the required course (RUSS 280)
- Students must complete one history or political science elective (POLI 480)
- Students must select 4 additional elective courses from the electives list
- Students may petition the Director to substitute alternative courses with substantial Russian or Eurasian studies content
