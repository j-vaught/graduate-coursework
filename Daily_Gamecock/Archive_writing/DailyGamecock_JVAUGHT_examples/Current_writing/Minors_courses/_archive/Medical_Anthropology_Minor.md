# Medical Anthropology Minor - Course Requirements

## Required Courses (6 credit hours)
- ANTH 551 - Medical Anthropology: Fieldwork (3 credits)
- ANTH 552 - Medical Anthropology (3 credits)

## Restricted Electives (12 credit hours - select from the following)
- ANTH 210 - Human Life Cycles in Different Cultures
- ANTH 212 - Food and Culture
- ANTH 292 - Disease, Health, and Social Inequities
- ANTH 262 - Basic Forensic Anthropology
- ANTH 263 - Medical Experimentation and the Black Body
- ANTH 280 - Humans Going Nuclear: Atomic Bombs, Cold War, and the Fallout
- ANTH 342 - Environmental Anthropology: Cross-cultural Perspectives on Environmental Change
- ANTH 392 - Global Women's Health
- ANTH 396 - Toxic Environments and Invisible Harms
- ANTH 561 - Human Osteology

## Program Summary
- **Total Credit Hours:** 18
- **Total Courses:** 6 (2 required + 4 elective selections)
