# Athletic Coaching Minor - Course Requirements

**University of South Carolina**
**College of Education**
**Total Credit Hours: 18**

## Required Courses

| Course Code | Course Title | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| PEDU 153 | Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation | 1 |
| PEDU 155 | Personal Training Preparation | 3 |
| PEDU 190 | Introduction to the Description and Analysis of Human Movement | 2 |
| PEDU 232 | Philosophy and Principles of Physical Education | 3 |
| PEDU 302 | Foundations of Coaching | 3 |
| PEDU 420 | Motor Learning in Physical Education | 3 |
| PEDU 650 | Theoretical Foundations of Athletic and Mental Performance Coaching | 3 |

## Elective Courses

No elective courses are listed for this minor. All 18 credit hours consist of required courses.

## Notes

- There is a recommended course sequence for this minor that differs from the order listed above
- Students should consult the Athletic Coaching program website for proper sequencing and semester availability
- Source: University of South Carolina 2025-2026 Academic Bulletin
