# Computer Science Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Overview
The Computer Science Minor includes foundational computer programming courses and intermediate/advanced computer science courses. Students will develop competency in software development and programming fundamentals.

## Required Courses (9 credits)

| Course Code | Title | Credits |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| CSCE 145 | Algorithmic Design I | 3-4 |
| CSCE 106 | Scientific Applications Programming | (Alternative) |
| CSCE 146 | Algorithmic Design II | 4 |
| CSCE 215 | UNIX/Linux Fundamentals | 1 |

**Note:** Choose either CSCE 145 or CSCE 106 for the foundational course.

## Elective Courses (9-10 credits)

Students must select electives from:

| Course Code | Title |
|-------------|-------|
| All CSCE courses | Numbered 201 or above (except CSCE 205) |
| MATH 174 | Discrete Mathematics for Computer Science |
| MATH 374 | Discrete Structures |
| MATH 574 | Discrete Mathematics I |

**Note:** Choose one of the MATH courses (174, 374, or 574) OR select additional CSCE courses numbered 201+.

## Summary Statistics

- **Total Required Credits:** 18 credit hours
- **Required Courses:** 3 (CSCE 145 or 106, CSCE 146, CSCE 215)
- **Elective Credits:** 9-10 (depending on foundational course selection)
- **Unique Prefixes:** 2 (CSCE, MATH)

## Important Notes

- Students majoring in Computer Engineering or Computer Information Systems may NOT earn this minor
- Students cannot receive credit for both CSCE 210 and CSCE 212
- Calculus is a prerequisite for some courses
