# University of South Carolina - History Minor

## Program Requirements

Students must complete **18 credit hours** in History courses with the following structure:
- Up to 6 credit hours may be at the 200 level
- All remaining hours (minimum 12) must be at the 300 level or higher
- All courses require approval by both the student's advisor and a History Department advisor

**Note:** The USC History minor does not have a fixed list of required courses. Students select from approved electives below to meet the credit hour and level requirements.
