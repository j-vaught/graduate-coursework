## Program Summary

- **Total Credit Hours Required:** 18
- **Required Courses:** 2 (6 credit hours)
- **Elective Courses:** Minimum 4 courses (12 credit hours)
- **Minimum Grade Required:** C or higher in all minor courses
- **Residency Requirement:** At least half of the courses must be completed at USC

## General Minor Requirements

1. All minor courses must be passed with a grade of C or higher.
2. At least half of the courses in the minor must be completed in residence at the University.
3. No course may satisfy both major and minor requirements.
