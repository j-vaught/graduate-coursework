## Program Notes

The Naval Science Studies minor is the classroom lecture portion of the program for all Navy and Marine Corps ROTC cadets. Students learn about military heritage, the development of naval technologies, military ethics, drill and ceremonies, communication, human relations, and leadership theory and techniques. Any student may sign up for Naval Science courses and complete the minor.
