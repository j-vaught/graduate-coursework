# Chemistry Minor - University of South Carolina

## Prerequisites (9-10 Hours)
- **CHEM 111** - General Chemistry I (3 credits)
- **CHEM 112** - General Chemistry II (3 credits)
- **MATH 111** - Basic College Mathematics (3-4 credits) OR **MATH 115** - Precalculus Mathematics

## Required Courses (12 Hours)
- **CHEM 321** - Quantitative Analysis (3 credits)
- **CHEM 321L** - Quantitative Analysis Laboratory (1 credit)
- **CHEM 333** - Organic Chemistry I (3 credits)
- **CHEM 331L** - Essentials of Organic Chemistry Laboratory I (1 credit)
- **CHEM 334** - Organic Chemistry II (3 credits)
- **CHEM 332L** - Essentials of Organic Chemistry Laboratory II (1 credit)

*Note: CHEM 322 and CHEM 322L may substitute for CHEM 321 and CHEM 321L*

## Chemistry Electives (6 Hours)
- 6 additional hours selected from **CHEM 300 or above**

*Restrictions: CHEM 321, 321L, 322, and 322L cannot count as electives. If CHEM 550 is used, then CHEM 555 and CHEM 556 cannot be used.*
