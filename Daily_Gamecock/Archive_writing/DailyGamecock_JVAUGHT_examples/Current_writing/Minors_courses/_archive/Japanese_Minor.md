# Japanese Minor - University of South Carolina

## Overview
- **Total Credit Hours Required:** 18
- **Minimum Grade:** C or better in all courses
- **General Education Restriction:** Courses applied toward general education requirements cannot be counted toward the minor

## Course Requirements

### Language Courses (7 courses)
- JAPA 221: Intermediate Japanese I
- JAPA 222: Intermediate Japanese II
- JAPA 224: Reading and Writing Japanese
- JAPA 321: Advanced Japanese I
- JAPA 322: Advanced Japanese II
- JAPA 421: Advanced Japanese III
- JAPA 422: Advanced Japanese IV

### Business & Professional Courses (2 courses)
- JAPA 331: Japanese for Business I
- JAPA 332: Japanese for Business II

### Culture & Literature Courses (7 courses)
- JAPA 240: Introduction to Japanese Culture
- JAPA 340: Introduction to Japanese Culture and Literature
- JAPA 341: Modern Japanese Literature
- JAPA 350: Japanese Culture and Society through Film
- JAPA 351: Japanese Culture and Society through Theatre
- JAPA 353: Japanese Culture and Society through Animation
- JAPA 500: Japanese Language in Society

### Variable Courses (2 courses)
- JAPA 398: Selected Topics
- JAPA 399: Independent Study

**Note:** The Japanese Minor requires 18 credit hours of elective courses. Students must complete exactly 18 hours by selecting from the listed options. There are no mandatory prerequisites or required courses specified for the minor itself—all courses count as electives that students choose to meet the 18-hour requirement.
