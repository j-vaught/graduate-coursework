# Geophysics Minor - Course Requirements

## Required Courses

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| MATH 141 | Calculus I | 4 |
| PHYS 201 & 201L | General Physics I and General Physics Laboratory I | 4 |
| PHYS 211 & 211L | Essentials of Physics I and Essentials of Physics I Lab | 4 |
| GEOL 101 | Introduction to the Earth | 4 |
| GEOL 103 | Environment of the Earth | 4 |
| GEOL 201 | Observing the Earth | 4 |
| GEOL 302 | Rocks and Minerals | 4 |

**Note:** Select 1 from PHYS (201/201L or 211/211L) and 1 from GEOL (101, 103, 201, or 302)
