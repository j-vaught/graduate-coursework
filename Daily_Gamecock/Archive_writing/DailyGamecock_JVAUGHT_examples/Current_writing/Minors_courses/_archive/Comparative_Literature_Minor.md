|------------|--------------|

## Elective Courses (12 hours)

### Select Two of the Following (6 hours)

| Course Code | Course Title |
|------------|--------------|
| CPLT 301 | Great Books of the Western World I |
| CPLT 302 | Great Books of the Western World II |
| CPLT 303 | Great Books of the Eastern World |

### Additional Electives (6 hours)

- One CPLT course, 300-level or above (3 credits)
- One literature course in the student's foreign language, 300-level or above (3 credits)
