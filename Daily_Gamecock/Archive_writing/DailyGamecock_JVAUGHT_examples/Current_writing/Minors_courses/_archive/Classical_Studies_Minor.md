# Classical Studies Minor - Course Requirements

## Required Courses (9 Hours)

| Course Code | Course Name | Hours |
|------------|------------|-------|
| CLAS 586 | Classical Mythology | 3 |
| CLAS 305 | Greece and Rome in Film and Popular Culture | 3 |
| CLAS 320 | Sexuality and Gender in Ancient Greece | 3 |
| CLAS 321 | Sexuality, Gender, and Power in Ancient Rome | 3 |
| GREK/LATN | Greek or Latin (300-level or above) | 3 |

**Note:** For the required component, students must take CLAS 586 (3 hours) plus select one additional course from the alternatives listed above (3 hours).
