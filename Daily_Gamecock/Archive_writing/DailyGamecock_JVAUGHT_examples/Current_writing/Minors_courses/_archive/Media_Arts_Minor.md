# Media Arts Minor - Course Requirements

## Program Overview
The Media Arts Minor exposes students to fundamental elements of media production and allows students to explore specific areas of interest including video and sound production, writing for print or screen, and video game design.

**Minimum Credits Required:** 18 hours
**Grade Requirement:** C or higher in all courses
