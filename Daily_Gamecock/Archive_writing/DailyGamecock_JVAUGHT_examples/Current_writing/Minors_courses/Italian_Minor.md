# Italian Minor - Course Requirements

## Required Courses (6 credit hours)

Students must complete one of the following sequences:

### Option A (6 credits)
- ITAL 221 - Intermediate Proficiency in Italian I (3 credits)
- ITAL 222 - Intermediate Proficiency in Italian II (3 credits)

### Option B (6 credits)
- ITAL 230 - Accelerated Intermediate Italian (6 credits)

## Elective Courses (12 credit hours)

Students select 12 credits from 300-level and above courses:

- ITAL 310 - Italian Conversation
- ITAL 311 - Writing in Italian
- ITAL 330 - TEATROMANIA: Italian Theater Practicum
- ITAL 340 - The Other Italians: Emigrants and Immigrants
- ITAL 350 - Advanced Italian Study Abroad
- ITAL 355 - Survey of Italian Literature I (1200-1850)
- ITAL 360 - Survey of Italian Literature II: 19th-21st Centuries
- ITAL 398 - Selected Topics
- ITAL 399 - Independent Study
- ITAL 400 - Contemporary Italian Civilization
- ITAL 402 - Italian Food Culture: Adaptability and Sustainability in Italian and Global Food Systems
- ITAL 404 - Twentieth Century Italian Literature
- ITAL 405 - The Italian Love Lyric
- ITAL 406 - Business Readings in Italian
- ITAL 407 - Advanced Conversation and Composition
- ITAL 411 - Italian Literature in Translation
- ITAL 412 - Post-World War II Italian Cinema
- ITAL 499 - Senior Project
- ITAL 500 - Machiavelli and Renaissance Florence
- ITAL 502 - Cercasi Umore: Understanding Italy
- ITAL 504 - The Italian Novella
- ITAL 560 - Independent Studies in Italian Literature
- ITAL 561 - Independent Studies in Italian Literature

## Program Summary

- **Total Credit Hours:** 18 hours (6 required + 12 elective)
- **Total Courses:** 26 courses across required and elective options
- **Unique Prefixes:** 1 (ITAL)

## Notes

Students placing into 300-level or higher courses must complete 18 hours above their placement level.
