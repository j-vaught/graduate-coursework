# University of South Carolina - Astronomy Minor

## Overview
The Astronomy Minor provides students the opportunity to observe the stars while working with professors on important research about distant galaxies and the evolution of the universe.

## Course Requirements

### Prerequisite Courses (7-8 Hours)
- **ASTR 101** - Introduction to Astronomy (4 credits)
- **MATH 122** - Calculus for Business Administration and Social Sciences (3-4 credits)
  - OR **MATH 142** - Calculus II

### Required Courses (at least 15 Hours)
- **ASTR 201** - Advanced Astronomy Course (3+ credits)
- **ASTR electives** - At least 9 hours in advanced ASTR courses numbered 300 or higher

### Non-ASTR Electives (up to 4 hours)
With departmental approval, students may select from:
- **CHEM 118** - Computational Chemistry I (1 credit)
- **CSCE 145** - Algorithmic Design I (4 credits)
- **EMCH 201** - Introduction to Applied Numerical Methods (3 credits)
- **GEOL 201** - Observing the Earth (4 credits)
- **MATH 174** - Discrete Mathematics for Computer Science (3 credits)
- **PHYS 211** - Essentials of Physics I (3 credits)
- **STAT 201** - Elementary Statistics (3 credits)

## Notes
- All minor courses must be passed with a grade of C or higher
- At least half of the courses in the minor must be completed in residence at the University
- Advance consultation with the Physics & Astronomy Department is recommended to ensure course credits will be acceptable for the astronomy minor
