# Chinese Studies Minor - University of South Carolina

## Program Overview
Students in the Chinese Studies Minor take courses in Mandarin while examining the social and historical aspects of Chinese culture, literature, politics, and religion. The minor requires 18 total credit hours at and above the 200 level.

## Required Courses (12 credit hours)

| Course Code | Course Name | Credits |
|------------|------------|---------|

| CHIN 222 | Intermediate Mandarin Chinese II | 3 |
| CHIN 321 | Advanced Intermediate Mandarin Chinese I | 3 |
